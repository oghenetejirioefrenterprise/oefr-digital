## Preconditions

- Uptrend intact on the daily chart: structure of higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL) confirmed over at least 2–3 prior swing cycles
- Asset has made a pullback from the most recent swing high — this pullback is the setup
- The current pullback is making a higher low vs the prior swing low — trend structure is preserved
- Volume during the pullback is declining (corrective, not distributive)
- Price consolidates for 2–10 days near the higher low (volatility contraction phase / flag / pennant)
- The consolidation is tight: daily candles are getting smaller (inside days or near-inside days during the base)
- Sector or market environment is supportive (BTC trending up for alts; total crypto market cap holding)
- Moving averages (20-day, 50-day) are rising and below current price — not acting as overhead resistance

**Qualifying setups (need at least one):**
- Consolidation within the top 30% of the prior swing range (healthy; not a deep retracement)
- Anchored VWAP from catalyst date or prior major swing low is acting as dynamic support under consolidation
- RSI or squeeze indicator showing compression (energy coiling, not exhaustion)

## Entry trigger

**Feeler entry (starter position):**
1. Consolidation has compressed for 3+ days with declining volume
2. Enter small (20–30% of target size) at the bottom of the consolidation range (at the higher low)
3. Stop: below the prior swing low (invalidates the higher-low structure)

**Breakout entry (full conviction):**
1. Price breaks above the consolidation range high with volume expansion (RVOL ≥ 1.5× average on breakout day)
2. For daily charts: buy the close of the breakout day, or the next-day open if breakout occurred in last hour
3. For 4h charts: buy on break above consolidation high with a 15m close confirmation above the level
4. Stop: below the consolidation low (tight — the consolidation defines the risk)

**Add on retest (optional):**
1. After the breakout, price may retest the consolidation high (now acting as support)
2. If the retest holds with lower volume, add to position on re-break above the retest level
3. This is a secondary entry with defined risk at the retest low

## Invalidation

- Prior swing low is breached: the higher-low structure is broken; trend may be reversing
- Breakout is on below-average volume (RVOL < 1.2×) — suspect breakout; reduce size or wait for retest confirmation
- Consolidation extends beyond 10 days with no directional resolution — energy is leaking; look for a new setup
- BTC (for alts) simultaneously breaks its own swing low — macro environment has turned; stand aside

## Targets

- T1: Prior swing high (minimum target — the most recent HH) — take 30–40% here
- T2: Measured move = consolidation height added to breakout level — take 30% here
- T3: Extension target using prior trend leg length — trail remainder
- Trail: Update stop to below each new daily higher low as price continues to make HH/HL structure
- Exit signal: daily close below the most recent higher low OR a clear momentum divergence (price higher high, volume exhaustion)

## Sizing notes

- Feeler size (20–30%) at consolidation bottom: tight stop allows feeler even in uncertain environments
- Full size at breakout: use the consolidation height as the stop distance for position sizing (risk is fixed, size adjusts)
- 1.5× base size only when: clean higher-low structure + consolidation volume contraction + RVOL spike on breakout + sector/BTC tailwind all align
- For multi-week consolidations (7+ days): these produce larger measured moves; can hold with wider stop and larger time-scale targets

## Crypto adaptation notes

- The higher-low consolidation breakout is the canonical bull-market crypto swing setup — in BTC bull cycles, every major pullback that holds above the prior HHL followed by a tight base is a high-probability long
- Time compression in crypto: what takes 2–3 weeks in equities often resolves in 2–5 days in crypto; check both 4h and daily timeframes for the consolidation pattern
- Wedge/pennant consolidations in crypto before breakout carry extra significance: volume contraction in a wedge followed by expansion on breakout = measured move reliability improves
- On HL perps: open interest during consolidation should be declining or flat — rising OI in consolidation = longs being added aggressively; if OI spikes before the breakout, it reduces edge (crowded trade)
- Funding rate during consolidation: mild positive funding (0.01–0.05%) during the consolidation = market leaning long but not extreme = healthy; extreme funding (>0.10%) = crowded, fade the breakout
- For altcoins: require BTC to be in its own higher-low structure before entering alt higher-low consolidation breakouts; alts amplify BTC's move, not overcome it

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- Entering the consolidation breakout with full size and a stop below the prior swing low — this makes the stop too wide for a proper position size; use the consolidation low as the stop, not the swing low
- Chasing the breakout after it has already moved 2+ ATRs from the consolidation — the feeler/breakout entry must be within the first 0.5 ATR of the consolidation range break
- Ignoring volume on the breakout — a volume-less breakout has 50%+ chance of failure; wait for the next day's confirmation if breakout volume is low
- Taking full profit at the prior swing high — the measured move (T2) and extension (T3) are often achievable in strong trends; scale out in thirds
- Shorting the "deep" pullback during the consolidation — in a confirmed uptrend, the consolidation is the pause before the next leg; do not fight the structure

## Example(s)

**BTC/USDC perp (HL), daily swing — post-halving bull cycle:**
- BTC made swing high $73,500; pulled back to $60,000 (prior swing low at $58,000 → this is a higher low)
- 7-day tight consolidation between $60,000–$65,000; daily candles shrinking; volume declining
- AVWAP from halving date = $62,500 acting as support under consolidation
- Breakout day: BTC closes above $65,000 on 2.2× average volume
- Feeler entry: $61,000 (20% size); Breakout entry: $65,200 (full size add); Stop: $59,500 (below consolidation low)
- T1: $73,500 (prior HH); T2: $70,200 (measured move: $5,000 range + $65,200 breakout); T3: trail daily HLs

**ETH/USDC perp (HL), 4h chart — narrative continuation:**
- ETH in uptrend; HH at $3,800; pulled back to $3,400 forming higher low (prior HL was $3,200)
- 4-day pennant consolidation $3,400–$3,600; RVOL declining each day
- Break above $3,600 on 1.8× RVOL; 15m confirms close above $3,600
- Enter $3,615; stop $3,380; T1: $3,800 (prior HH); T2: $4,000 (measured move); trail 4h HLs

**SOL/USDC perp (HL), bull flag on 4h:**
- SOL in strong uptrend; ran from $120 to $175; flag forms $165–$175 for 4 days
