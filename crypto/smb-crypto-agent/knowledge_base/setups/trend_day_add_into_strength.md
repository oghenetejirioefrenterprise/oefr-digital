## Preconditions

- Trend day confirmed (see trend_day_recognition_checklist.md): VOLD/breadth/CVD all aligned, internals pinned in one direction
- Initial position established from Opening Range Break (ORB) or opening drive long/short
- Price holding above VWAP on 5-min chart (bull trend) or below VWAP (bear trend)
- 9 EMA is above 20/21 EMA on 5-min chart (bull) — EMAs spreading, not converging
- Volume pattern: expansion on the directional moves, contraction on pullbacks
- Macro catalyst still active and not contradicted by new headlines

## Entry trigger

**Primary (VWAP Pullback Buy):**
- Price pulls back to VWAP on light volume (not aggressive selling — sellers not present on pullback)
- Tape or order book shows bid absorption at VWAP level (large bids holding, offers stepping back)
- First green 5-min candle that touches or reclaims VWAP = entry signal
- Enter on close of that candle or on first uptick off VWAP
- Stop: below the low of the VWAP-touch candle (or below consolidation low near VWAP)

**Secondary (9 EMA First Touch):**
- After a strong opening drive leg, price pulls back to 9 EMA on the 1-min or 2-min chart
- First touch of 9 EMA after the momentum leg is the highest-probability add point
- Volume contracts on pullback to EMA (sellers not in control)
- Enter immediately on first touch with stop below the low of the pullback candle

**Tertiary (9 EMA x VWAP Cross — "Fashionably Late"):**
- 9 EMA has crossed above VWAP (confirming trend after opening drive)
- Price pulls back to the 9EMA/VWAP confluence zone on light volume
- Enter at the confluence zone; stop = below the wick at VWAP
- 60/40 rule on exits: take 60% off at first target (1x ATR), hold 40% for extension

**Add cadence on trend days:**
- Add 1: First VWAP pullback after opening drive
- Add 2: Second consolidation forming above VWAP (midday lull) — see trend_day_afternoon_continuation.md
- Add 3: Any subsequent VWAP test that holds while internals remain aligned
- Never add when price is extended more than 0.75 ATR above VWAP — wait for next pullback

## Invalidation

- 5-min close below VWAP with expanding volume (bull thesis)
- 5-min close above VWAP with expanding volume (bear thesis)
- CVD reverses and starts trending in opposite direction
- Two consecutive closes against the trend below (above) 9 EMA on 5-min chart
- New significant negative headline contradicting the catalyst
- Breadth ratio reverts to neutral (internals no longer aligned)

## Targets

- **Trade-to-hold discipline:** Hold the core position until trend structure breaks (5-min close below/above VWAP)
- Partial 1 (20-30%): at first intraday resistance level or 1x ATR from opening drive low
- Partial 2 (20-30%): at prior day's high or psychological round number
- Runner (40-60%): trail with 15-min 20 EMA; exit on first 15-min close below (above for shorts)
- Final exit: on VWAP break at close, or when daily high prints near EOD
- "On trend days, trade to hold — not scalp." Exit reasons: meaningful lower high forming, VWAP lost, >0.75 ATR extended from VWAP without pullback, 15-min 20 EMA break

## Sizing notes

- Full daily risk allocation on trend day core position (confirmed A+ setup with all internals aligned)
- Scale in using the add cadence above — do not front-run; wait for VWAP pullback confirmation
- On A+ confirmed trend days use 40-50% of daily stop budget on the primary position
- After each add, move stop to break-even on prior entries — maintain "controlled aggression"
- Position management: never add when already more than 0.75 ATR extended from VWAP

## Crypto adaptation notes

**VWAP on 24/7 markets:** Anchor VWAP at the daily open (00:00 UTC) or at the US cash session open (13:30 UTC). Reset the VWAP anchor on major catalyst events (e.g., anchor from the exact timestamp of CPI print or major announcement).

**9 EMA on 1-min/2-min for Hyperliquid perps:** Institutional buy programs in crypto show the same EMA-respect behavior as equity momentum stocks. When BTC or a liquid alt pulls back to 9 EMA on 1-min and the first green candle reclaims it — that is the add signal. "When the stock pulls back to the 9 EMA and you see the first green candle that is your entry signal that the institutional buy program is still in control."

**Tape reading analog:** On Hyperliquid order book: watch for large refreshing bid orders at VWAP level. If sellers hit the bid and the bid refreshes at the same price repeatedly = institutional accumulation = add signal. This is the "hidden refreshing bidder" pattern — one of the highest-conviction add triggers in crypto.

**Funding rate confirmation:** On bull trend days, funding rate should remain positive or rising on pullbacks (longs are still willing to pay). If funding goes negative on a pullback, the conviction level drops — reduce add size.

**Venues:** Use Hyperliquid perps for leveraged directional exposure (3-5x leverage conservative). Use Binance spot for long-only positions where you want to hold overnight without funding cost on large size. Use Binance futures (USDⓈ-M) for hedged or short-viable plays on confirmed bear trend days.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- **Scalping out too early:** "The trader who made $2000 added when momentum confirmed; the one who made $200 took profit too early." — hold the core on trend days.
- **Adding when extended:** Never add when price is 1.5-2x ATR above VWAP. Wait for the pullback. "Do not chase the open."
- **Chasing into late adds without confirming internals still aligned:** Before each add, re-check CVD and breadth. If internals have weakened, do not add.
- **Exiting on normal trend day pullback:** Light-volume VWAP pullbacks are add signals, not exit signals on trend days. The mistake is "bailing on your trade just because price pulls back to VWAP."
- **Forgetting 60/40 rule:** Take 60% off at first target to guarantee profit; let the 40% runner work with trailing stop. This optimizes expected value across the trade distribution.
