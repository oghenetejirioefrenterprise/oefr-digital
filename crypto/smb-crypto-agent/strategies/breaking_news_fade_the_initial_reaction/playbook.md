# Breaking News — Fade the Initial Reaction
**Category:** breaking_news  **Timeframe:** intraday  **Confidence:** med  **Venues:** HL perps, Binance futures

## Preconditions
- Asset has already had 2–3 strong directional days into the catalyst — extended from where it was originally accumulated.
- Day-1 or day-2 move was parabolic (100–200% or multi-ATR gap); the "easy money" is already made.
- The initial catalyst reaction is immediate and extreme: gap-up or spike within minutes of the news.
- Price is at or near a major resistance level (prior high, range top, prior consolidation cluster) — overhead supply exists.
- Funding rate on HL perps is at an extreme (>0.1–0.15% per 8h positive = too many longs crammed in).
- For the "false news denial" sub-variant: news turns out to be unconfirmed, overstated, or retracted within minutes — the fade is higher probability.
- For the "sell-the-news" sub-variant: catalyst was well-known and pre-priced in; buy-the-rumor sell-the-news dynamic is active.
- For the "fed day / FOMC fade": market has already rallied into the announcement; initial dovish reaction spikes up then reverses as traders book profits.

## Entry trigger
1. **Lower high formation (primary trigger):** Price spikes on news, pulls back, then bounces but fails to make a new high. Short (or reduce longs) at the lower high with a stop just above the spike high. This is the cleanest fade trigger.
2. **Failure at prior resistance:** Price reaches a major technical level (prior high, VWAP, daily resistance) and shows tape weakness — offers not clearing, bids pulling. Enter short on the rejection.
3. **Third-day gap-up fade:** On day 3 of a sustained news-driven move, any gap-up that fails to hold above the prior day's close within 15–30 minutes is shortable. Enter on the reclaim failure with stop above gap-up high.
4. **False news denial play:** When a news item is quickly denied or retracted, fade the initial direction immediately — the reversal is typically fast and sharp. Do not wait for confirmation; size aggressively.
5. **FOMC / Fed day fade:** After the initial knee-jerk reaction to the announcement (up or down), wait 2–5 minutes for the reaction to exhaust (volume drying up, no new high/low made). Fade the spike in the opposite direction.
6. **Parabolic mania fade (crypto-specific):** Token runs 200–500% on hype/mania catalyst over 1–3 days. On the day the mania peaks (no new highs on multiple attempts, volume divergence), short any pop toward the prior session high.

## Invalidation
- Asset makes new high above the spike high on volume — fade is wrong; cover and reassess.
- Tape shows fresh institutional buying (large bids stepping in, offers clearing rapidly) — not a fade day.
- Second news catalyst drops that extends the original thesis — stand aside.
- Funding normalizes from extreme levels before the fade plays out — risk/reward degrades.
- Price holds above prior day's resistance level for more than 30 minutes with no fade — trend continuation more likely.

## Targets
- **T1:** Back to the opening price or prior day's close; trim 30–50%.
- **T2:** Pre-news support level (accumulation area before the catalyst move).
- **T3:** For 3-day extended moves: target 50% retracement of the entire catalyst-driven move.
- For false news denials: target full reversion to pre-announcement price within 15–30 minutes.

## Sizing notes
- Start smaller than a trend-day trade — this is a counter-trend play with higher failure risk.
- Use 1–2x leverage on HL perps for fades; do not use max leverage on counter-trend setups.
- If the fade triggers at a major resistance confluence (prior high + VWAP + extreme funding), size up to normal unit.
- Trail stops tightly on fades; profits evaporate fast if the trend reasserts.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Crypto "blockchain mania" pump-and-fade maps directly to: new Hyperliquid listing day, token launches, meme coin launches on chain. Day 1 long viable if it consolidates; day 2–3 the fade is the primary setup.
- FOMC dovish surprise → BTC/ETH spike → fade: the initial spike in crypto post-FOMC often reverses within 30–60 minutes as traders sell the news. Watch for BTC to re-test pre-announcement VWAP.
- Extreme funding (HL perps) is the crypto-specific "too extended" signal — the equity analog is TICK at +1200 and ADD at +3000 on multiple days.
- ETF approval "sell-the-news": if BTC ran 40%+ into the ETF approval announcement, the fade after the approval print is the primary play, not continuation.
- Token-specific mania (AI token, meme, new L1 hype): after 200%+ in 3 days, the fade setup is highest probability on the day that funding peaks and new highs fail.
- Watch CVD (cumulative volume delta) divergence on Hyperliquid: price making new high but CVD rolling over = fade signal.
