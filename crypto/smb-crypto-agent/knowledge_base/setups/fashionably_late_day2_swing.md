# Fashionably Late — Day 2+ Swing Continuation
**Category:** fashionably_late  **Timeframe:** intraday/swing  **Confidence:** high  **Venues:** Hyperliquid perps, Binance spot
**Adapted from:** Top_10_Gap_Trading_Mistakes_You_Must_Avoid_And_how_to_make_them_big_winners.transcript.md, Why_Simple_Strategies_Are_Money_Machines.transcript.md, 3_Proven_Swing_Trading_Setups.transcript.md, 5_Explosive_Setups_for_the_Week_Ahead_But_One_Stands_Out.transcript.md, 3_Elite_Trade_Setups_Every_Pro_Trader_Should_Study.transcript.md, SMB_Capital_-_Morning_Game_Plan_Review_APKT.transcript.md, Top_2_Ways_to_Filter_Out_Market_Noise_and_WIN.transcript.md

## Preconditions
- A clear, strong Day 1 move has already occurred and closed near its highs (or lows for short setups) — the trend is confirmed, not speculative
- Day 2 opens flat or slightly pulls back — no gap in the opposite direction that breaks Day 1 structure
- Price is at or approaching a key Day 1 reference level: Day 1 VWAP, Day 1 high, 2-day VWAP, or the breakout level of a multi-day consolidation
- Volume on the Day 2 pullback is declining (healthy; buyers holding); aggressive selling on pullback invalidates the setup
- Broader market (BTC) is not in an opposing trending day
- **Multi-day base variant (pre-breakout entry):** Price has formed higher lows over 3–10+ days with a defined resistance ceiling; daily and weekly trend both up; volume contracting into the base (coiling)

## Entry trigger
**Day 2 VWAP reclaim:** Wait for Day 2 open; if price pulls back to 2-day anchored VWAP and holds, buy the confirmation of a higher low on the 5-min chart. The hold is the entry — do not buy the open gap.

**Day 2 key-level test:** Price tests Day 1 high (long) or Day 1 low (short) on Day 2; if it holds with absorption (hammer, doji, or engulfing on 5-min), enter with stop just beyond the tested level.

**Multi-day base breakout:** On the day price breaks above (below) the defined multi-day resistance (support) with volume meaningfully above the 20-day average, enter on the breakout bar or on the first daily close above the level. If missing the breakout, the first pullback to the former resistance (now support) is the second-chance entry.

**Bad-news-absorbed swing:** Asset in downtrend receives a negative catalyst but fails to make a new low within the session. If price reclaims a key prior high (e.g., prior after-hours high), enter long above that level with stop below it. Thesis: news was already priced in; the failed new low signals exhausted sellers.

**Three-bar reclaim:** One large red bar on volume followed by three consecutive green bars reclaiming VWAP. Enter on breakout of the third green bar's high; stop below the low of the first green bar.

## Invalidation
- Day 2 price closes below 2-day VWAP on a volume expansion bar
- Day 2 fails to make a new high by midday (signals distribution, not continuation)
- Multi-day base: daily close back below the breakout level on volume
- Bad-news variant: price breaks below the key prior high used as entry reference and cannot reclaim
- Three-bar reclaim: price reverses below first green bar low with volume

## Targets
- Day 2 continuation: Day 1 high extension → ATR extension above Day 1 high (first trim); trail with daily 20 EMA for swing hold
- Multi-day breakout: first target 1 ATR above breakout; trim; trail remainder with daily 20 EMA; secondary = next major resistance or round number +10% above breakout
- Bad-news absorbed: gap fill toward next resistance; swing potential if consolidates above reclaimed level
- General trim rule: 60% at first target, hold 40% trailed with daily 20 EMA while structure intact
- 4:1 reward-to-risk is the benchmark for swing continuation; pass if daily structure does not offer it

## Sizing notes
- Do not chase Day 1 — the entire thesis of this setup is that patience to Day 2 provides lower-risk entry with comparable reward
- Enter with planned size at confirmation; do not add unless Day 2 makes new high and pulls back to prior breakout (second add point)
- For multi-day base breakouts, standard unit on the day of breakout; can scale to 1.5x unit on the first pullback to breakout level
- Trail stop with daily 20 EMA for swing legs — do not use arbitrary time exits

## Crypto adaptation notes
- **2-day VWAP anchor:** On Hyperliquid, anchor VWAP to the start of Day 1's session open (midnight UTC or when the catalyst triggered). A 2-day VWAP in crypto often spans 48h of 24/7 trading — use chart software that supports custom VWAP anchoring.
- **Day 2 in crypto is not 24h later:** Because crypto trades continuously, "Day 2" is the next UTC daily candle open. Watch for Asia-session trap (early morning sell-off that recovers into US session) — this is the Day 2 pullback.
- **BTC ATH / multi-week base breakout:** BTC breaking a multi-month consolidation high with volume is the highest-conviction version of this setup. There is no overhead supply at new all-time highs. Enter on ATH break or first daily close above; hold as long as daily candle stays above prior ATH. Monitor IBIT (proxy for institutional ETF flows) and MSTR/COIN as confirmation assets.
- **Funding rate check on Day 2:** If funding has flipped strongly positive by Day 2 on the perp, the easy money may already be gone — reduce size. Moderate positive funding (0.01–0.03% per 8h) is acceptable.
- **Altcoin sympathy continuation:** After a lead narrative (e.g., AI coins, meme coins) pumps a lead token on Day 1, the fashionably-late entry on the lead token (not the sympathy) on Day 2 is higher quality. Sympathy plays are lower quality — their Day 2 reversal is more likely to be the backside-short setup.
- **Bad-news-absorbed variant in crypto:** Token in a downtrend receives negative event (hack, regulatory news, token unlock) but does not make a new low — the low was already priced in. Reclaim of the key prior session high above the event price is the entry. Stop below the event-day low. Works best on large-cap tokens (BTC, ETH, SOL) where news-driven sentiment overshoots fundamentals.
- **Multi-day base + IBIT confirmation:** For BTC base breakouts, IBIT daily volume and price confirming on the same day as the BTC breakout dramatically increases conviction. MSTR and COIN as secondary confirmation.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Buying Day 1 into strength out of FOMO instead of waiting for Day 2 structure — "fashionably late: wait for the VWAP reclaim on day 2" (Top 10 Gap Trading)
- Treating Day 2 as guaranteed continuation — the invalidation criteria must be checked; Day 2 failures that close below 2-day VWAP are strong reversal signals
- Exiting too early on the multi-day base breakout because "it moved a lot" — at ATHs there is no overhead supply; the only direction is continuation until daily structure breaks (Elite Trade Setups)
- Entering bad-news-absorbed too early — wait for the specific key level reclaim, not just "it stopped going down"
- Missing the three-bar reclaim timing — the entry is the breakout of the third bar's high, not at the open of bar 3 or during the bar sequence
- "Avoid the midrange — the edges are where the opportunities are" (Why Simple Strategies) — Day 2 entries at the middle of Day 1's range are low quality; target entries at Day 1 extremes or at the breakout level

## Example(s)
**BTC Day 2 VWAP reclaim:** BTC pumps 8% on Day 1 (e.g., ETF approval news), closes at HOD. Day 2 opens flat, sells off 2% in Asia session to 2-day VWAP at $64,200. Volume on pullback is 60% of Day 1 average. Price forms a higher low at $64,250 on 5-min, bids absorb. Entry long $64,300. Stop: $63,900 (below 2-day VWAP). First target: Day 1 high ($67,500, take 60%). Trail remainder with daily 20 EMA. Exit when daily 20 EMA is breached on close.

**BTC multi-week base breakout:** BTC forms higher lows over 14 days with resistance at $69,000 (prior ATH). Daily and weekly trend up. Volume contracting in base (coiling). Day of breakout: BTC closes above $69,000 with volume 2x 20-day average. Entry: breakout day close or open of next day above $69,000. Stop: daily close below $69,000. Target: $75,000–$76,000 (next round number +10%). Trail with daily 20 EMA.

**Bad-news absorbed (SOL):** SOL in downtrend from $200. Exchange halts trading for maintenance — SOL drops but holds $140 (prior consolidation support) and does not make a new low. Reclaims $155 (prior after-hours high). Entry long above $155. Stop: $148 (below the event-day low). Target: gap fill toward $170.
