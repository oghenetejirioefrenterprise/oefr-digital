METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Research Approach:
- Cross-referenced full archive of 91 existing opportunity briefs to enforce non-duplication
- Used "facility vs. professional" distinction to find legitimate adjacent SKUs (pharmacy facilities ≠ pharmacists; clinics ≠ DVMs; PLS ≠ PE)
- Validated vendor ecosystems via commercial B2B database market patterns (8+ vendors each = sustained demand)
- Validated buyer LTV via channel partner economics, equipment capex tickets, and channel-partner program fee structures
- Focused on opportunities with structurally captive buyer demand (M&A consolidator pipelines, motor-club recruiting, wholesaler account acquisition) — these create recurring buy-side pressure independent of macro cycles

Scoring Criteria:
- Commercial vendor ecosystem depth and pricing transparency
- Buyer LTV and structurally captive buyer demand
- Public data availability and engineering complexity
- Differentiator strength (filters and flags no existing vendor offers)
- Absence of low-cost flat-file alternatives at $99 or below
