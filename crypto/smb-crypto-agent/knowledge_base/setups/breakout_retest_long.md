# Breakout Retest Long
**Category:** second_chance_retest  **Timeframe:** scalp/intraday  **Confidence:** high  **Venues:** HL perps (BTC, ETH, SOL, majors), Binance spot (long-bias alts)

## Preconditions
- Price broke above a clearly identified resistance level on elevated volume (≥1.5× RVOL on the breakout bar).
- After breaking out, price pulled back toward the prior resistance level, now acting as support. Volume contracts on the pullback (healthy retest — sellers not present in size).
- The broader market/BTC is constructive or neutral (not in a hard downtrend).
- Anchored VWAP from catalyst day or from the breakout bar is now below current price; the retest approaches but does not breach it.
- For multi-day consolidation breakouts: 3–10 days of tight range with declining volume precede the breakout (energy coil). The retest arrives within 1–3 sessions.

## Entry trigger
- **Primary (tape-confirmed):** Price pulls into the breakout level (former resistance). Tape shows bids absorbing at the level — offers lifting without price retreating, bids refreshing, or large prints clearing at support. Enter on first sign of re-bid above the level.
- **Candle-confirmed:** 15-min or 5-min bullish reversal candle (hammer, engulfing, or tight consolidation break) at the former resistance. Volume contracts to below-average on the pullback bar; volume expands on the confirmation bar.
- **Range break (second-chance):** After an extended tight consolidation above breakout level (5+ bars), buy the break of the upper consolidation boundary with volume expansion.
- Stop: 2 cents / 0.1–0.2% below the retest low (scalp) or below the breakout level (swing). Never wider than the breakout level itself.

## Invalidation
- Price closes back below the breakout level on a meaningful volume bar — prior resistance was not absorbed; trade is wrong.
- Volume on the retest candle expands (distribution), indicating sellers returning in size.
- For swing variant: daily close below breakout level.

## Targets
- **T1:** 1× ATR above the breakout level (cover 25–33% of position).
- **T2:** Prior swing high / measured move = range height added to breakout level (cover next 33%).
- **Trailing stop:** Trail with 5-min or 15-min higher lows for the remainder; exit on first close back below prior candle low.
- For multi-day breakout: measured move = base width + breakout level is the minimum swing target; scale out 50% at first target, trail remainder.

## Sizing notes
- Tight stop at the retest level = small dollar risk per share → size up relative to normal.
- Size: 50–75% of standard allocation at initial entry; add remaining 25–50% on confirmed hold (second consecutive higher low above breakout level).
- Reduce to 50% of standard size if RVOL on the retest day is below 1.0 (low participation).
- Do not use this setup in low-liquidity environments (weekends, holiday sessions in crypto) — widen stops or skip.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- **BTC/ETH/SOL on Hyperliquid perps:** The setup is the primary venue for leverage. Use 15-min chart for entry trigger, 1-hour for trend context.
- **Binance spot (long-bias alts):** Use for non-leveraged accumulation of stronger alts breaking to new highs at prior resistance. Avoid spot for short-bias scenarios.
- **Anchored VWAP from catalyst:** Anchor VWAP to the day the breakout occurred. The breakout retest should hold above this anchored VWAP; if it undercuts it, reduce conviction.
- **Funding rate context:** On Hyperliquid, a flat or slightly negative funding rate during the pullback into retest confirms shorts are hedging — adds to long conviction. Strongly positive funding during retest = too many longs already, reduce size.
- **CME gap fills:** CME gap-fill attempts that hold at the prior breakout level (not filling the full gap) are a valid subset of this setup for BTC.
- **Sector rotation:** When one narrative sector (e.g., DeFi, L2) is leading, buy the laggard sector member pulling back to its own breakout level while the sector leader holds highs.
- Applicable to BTC, ETH, SOL, and any major alt with clear horizontal resistance on daily/4h chart. Less applicable to micro-cap tokens where levels are illiquid and easily swept.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Chasing the initial breakout** instead of waiting for the retest — lower risk/reward, often initiates at the top of a leg.
- **Buying the midrange of a consolidation** rather than at the retest level — SMB: "putting on trades in the middle of a range is just disaster waiting to happen."
- **Skipping volume confirmation** — entering without tape or volume confirmation at the retest level. If volume expands on pullback, sellers are present; wait or skip.
- **Wide stop placed below a random ATR** instead of directly below the retest low or breakout level — destroys R/R.
- **Third and fourth retests of the same level:** Win rate drops materially after the first and second revisit. Best entries are first and second retest only.
- **Missing the flip:** If the retest breaks back through the level on volume, the setup flips to a breakdown_retest_short — many traders hold long past invalidation.

## Example(s)
**BTC ATH retest (perps, daily timeframe):** BTC breaks above prior all-time high on heavy volume. Three days later it pulls back to the prior ATH level on declining volume. Anchored VWAP from breakout day aligns with prior ATH. Funding rate has moderated from spike levels. 15-min shows hammer candle at the level with large bid absorption visible on L2. Enter long with stop 0.15% below the ATH level. T1: 1 ATR above entry; T2: measured move from base.

**Facebook WhatsApp announcement (SMB playbook):** Tight range (66.40–66.60) forms during news indecision. Price breaks above 66.60 with strong green candle. Pulls back cleanly to 66.60. Bids absorb at 66.60 within 2 bars. Enter long. Stop: 66.40. Target: ~70 area (no overhead resistance). Described as "the buy not the initial breakout."

**BABA range breakout (SMB 4/16/10):** BABA breaks above 136 on heavy volume; 139 becomes new support. Pullback to 139 — buy in front of it. Add above next resistance once confirmed. "In a really strong market we look for buying stocks after they break out and find new support levels."
