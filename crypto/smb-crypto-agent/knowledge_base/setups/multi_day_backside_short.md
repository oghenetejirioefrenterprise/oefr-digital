## Preconditions
**Day-2/Day-3 exhaustion criteria — multi-day continuation short:**
1. Asset ran 100-300%+ on Day 1 (news catalyst, meme, listing event) and closed near the high of day (bullish Day 1 close)
2. Day 2 or Day 3: stock shows exhaustion — fails to make new highs vs prior session; lower high confirmed on hourly or 4h chart
3. 2-day or multi-day anchored VWAP (anchored from catalyst day open) is now acting as overhead resistance
4. Price closed in the bottom 25% of Day 1's range on subsequent day (institutions distributing over multiple days)
5. Institutional ownership dynamics: high institutional ownership in equity analog = forced multi-day selling; crypto equivalent = large wallets distributing (visible on-chain)
6. Short float <10% (crypto: no extreme short interest / squeeze risk)
7. Broad market not aggressively bullish (market regime neutral to weak)

**Three-day extended trend exhaustion (the full arc):**
- Day 1: Strong directional move with acceleration; catalog as "extended" candidate
- Day 2: Continuation with volume declining; closing in weaker position
- Day 3: Gap up fails on open; makes lower high vs prior session; VWAP anchored from Day 1 acts as cap

**Additional confirmation filters:**
- Failed follow-through: price attempted to recover (dead-cat bounce) but failed at prior support-turned-resistance (2-day VWAP, prior consolidation zone, declining 20/50-day MA)
- Fresh negative catalyst on Day 2/3 amplifies the setup (earnings guide miss, protocol negative news, secondary offering below market price)

## Entry trigger
**Primary entry — lower high short at multi-day VWAP:**
Day 2/3 morning or afternoon: price pops into the 2-day or multi-day anchored VWAP overhead; lower high confirms vs prior lower high; short on the failure of that bounce with stop above the high of the failed bounce.

**Secondary entry — break below earnings/catalyst low (full capitulation signal):**
Once the lower high is established, add short on break below Day 1 earnings/catalyst low. This is "free air" — no prior support holding it up. Position: hold core with trailing stop at prior lower high.

**Day 3 gap-up short (highest probability for 3-day pattern):**
Asset gaps up on Day 3 continuation news or momentum; opening spike fails within 30 minutes; lower high vs Day 2 high confirms; short on tape confirmation (offers refreshing, bids pulling). Stop: above Day 3 high of day.

**Swing overnight setup:**
Weak close below key support (bottom 25% of day's range) with no buyer defense into close = overnight short via perp. Cover next morning when gap-fill attempt comes.

## Invalidation
- Price reclaims 2-day VWAP and holds above it for 30+ minutes with buyers absorbing offers; thesis invalidated
- New high above Day 2 high on volume (short squeeze resumes; cover immediately)
- Day 2 fails to make new low by 10:30am ET equivalent (no continuation = early warning that sellers not aggressive)
- Day 1 earnings/catalyst low holds on retest with rising volume (real buyers at that level)
- High short interest discovered — squeeze risk invalidates fade thesis

## Targets
- T1: Day 1 afternoon low or first intraday support below Day 2 open (cover 50% of swing position)
- T2: Prior support level on the 6-month chart or next major daily support (cover 25%)
- T3: 50-70% retrace of the full Day 1 run (hold 25% runner with trailing stop via prior daily bar high)
- T4 (extended 3-7 day swing): Return to origin of the move (pre-catalyst base price)
- Trail: use prior daily bar high as the trailing stop mechanism on daily timeframe; 5-min lower highs on intraday

## Sizing notes
- Full intended swing size on Day 2 lower high confirmation (not just probe size like Day 1)
- Scaling into the swing: 50% at lower high short entry, 50% on break below Day 1 catalyst low
- For meme/low-float crypto tokens: reduce to 50% of intended size due to squeeze risk; use wider stops
- Maximum 3 average green days of risk on any single swing idea (risk table discipline)
- Cover 50% at T1 to bank gains; never hold full position to T3/T4 in volatile crypto

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Multi-day backside shorts are a staple of crypto trading: altcoin pumps on narratives (AI, meme, RWA) follow Day 1→Day 2→Day 3 exhaustion patterns with regularity
- Funding rate flip is the crypto-specific Day 2/3 confirmation: if funding was very positive (longs paying shorts) on Day 1 and starts flipping neutral or negative on Day 2, the squeeze is over and backside is beginning
- Liquidation cascade fuel: on multi-day crypto backsides, there are often trapped leveraged longs from Day 1 who are forced to sell on Day 2/3 margin calls. This accelerates the down move vs equity backsides.
- Token-specific dynamics: check token unlock schedule — if a major vesting unlock is due, that adds supply pressure on top of the exhaustion short
- On-chain analytics as institutional selling equivalent: large wallets sending to exchanges on Day 2/3 = "institutional selling" analog; confirms multi-day distribution
- For BTC/ETH multi-day backside after macro events: use 2-day anchored VWAP from the high as resistance; enter short on lower high below that level; 4h chart for trend; 1h for entry timing

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Entering the swing on Day 1 (too early) — the highest probability multi-day short is Day 2/3 after clear failure signals
- Holding the full swing position through the entire move without scaling — take 50% at T1 to de-risk; no single exit
- Not defining the invalidation level before entry — the stop is the Day 2 high of day; if that's taken out, cover fully
- Ignoring the short float / squeeze risk — high short interest + failed Day 2 = potential squeeze; always verify before sizing full
- Confusing Day 1 pullback (healthy consolidation) with Day 2 backside (exhaustion) — the distinction is: Day 1 pullback = holds above Day 1 VWAP; Day 2 backside = fails to hold Day 1 VWAP on multiple attempts
- "Being too patient" on the pop entry — the lower high is the signal; don't wait for a more perfect entry that never comes

## Example(s)
**ROKU Day 3 (three consecutive up days then gap fade):** Stock up 3 days; Day 3 catalyst gap up; gap fades on open; lower high vs Day 2 high confirms; short with stop above Day 3 high; T1 = prior accumulation area, T2 = 50% retrace of 3-day move.

**Meme token multi-day backside (crypto generalized):** Token pumps 300% on Day 1 on narrative; Day 2 funding flips neutral; lower high forms below 2-day VWAP; short on Day 2 pop failure at 2-day VWAP; cover in 3 tranches over Days 2-4 as origin-of-move approach.

**Earnings second-day continuation short (institutional selling):** Stock drops 20% on Day 1 earnings miss with 5x volume; closes in bottom 25% of range; Day 2 spikes into afternoon resistance (2-day VWAP); tape shows heavy selling at resistance; short on quick fail back through the level; add on break below Day 1 earnings low; T1 = Day 1 afternoon low, T2 = 6-month support.
