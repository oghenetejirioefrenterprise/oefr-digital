## Preconditions

**The Gap:**
- Asset opens meaningfully above prior session close due to a real fundamental catalyst (news, upgrade, earnings equivalent, protocol announcement, listing)
- Gap must be catalyst-driven — technical-only gaps without news have lower follow-through
- RVOL elevated: ≥ 2× average on open (≥ 3× for highest conviction); in crypto: volume in first 15–30 min must exceed the prior session's same-period volume
- Pre-market (or prior session for 24/7 crypto) established a clear range with defined high and low

**The Give:**
- After the open, price pulls back — the "give" phase
- Critical: the give must be ORDERLY, not panic selling
  - Orderly give: volume contracts on the pullback, price moves slowly down in measured steps
  - Panic give: high volume, large candles — this invalidates the setup; wait for consolidation before re-entry
- The give must hold above a meaningful level: gap fill zone, VWAP, pre-session high, or prior resistance-now-support
- For crypto: give should not retrace more than 50% of the initial gap move; deep retrace (>50%) signals gap failure

**Market environment:**
- Broader market (BTC for alts; SPY for equities) must not be in distribution or breaking down simultaneously
- Sector context must be supportive — crypto narrative must remain intact during the give phase

## Entry trigger

**Go entry (primary):**
1. Give phase completes: price holds a meaningful support level (VWAP, prior resistance, pre-session high) for 5–15 minutes with tightening range (pennant/flag forming)
2. Higher low confirmed vs the give low — shows buyers absorbing the pullback
3. Breakout of the flag/pennant or reclaim of the gap open level with volume expansion
4. Enter on the first candle that closes above the consolidation range high (the "go" candle)
5. Stop: just below the consolidation/give low (tight, since the give defined the risk)

**VWAP reclaim variant:**
1. Give pulls back to VWAP; price briefly touches VWAP
2. Immediate re-bid above VWAP (stuff-and-rubber-band)
3. Enter on the reclaim; stop below the VWAP touch low

**Feeler + scale approach (for large positions):**
- T1 (feeler, 20–25% of target size): enter at the go trigger
- T2 (full size add, 50% of target): on confirmed hold above the go entry level after 1–2 candles
- T3 (runner, remaining 25%): only if price breaks to new session high with tape acceleration

## Invalidation

- The give fills the entire gap (price returns to prior session close) — gap is invalid; structural buyers have exited
- The give is on panic volume (>1.5× the opening volume) — distribution not correction; stand aside
- Price holds above the go entry level for <2 candles then reverses back through — false breakout; exit immediately
- Broader market (BTC for alts) simultaneously breaks down vs its own VWAP or key support

## Targets

- T1: Gap high (pre-market/prior session high) — take 40–50% here; first natural resistance after the give
- T2: Measured move = give range (gap-to-give-low) added to the go entry level
- T3: 1 ATR above the gap high for extended moves on A++ catalyst setups
- Trail: 5m 21 EMA is the trailing stop once above T1; exit when price closes a 5m bar below the 21 EMA

## Sizing notes

- Gap + catalyst (A+ setup): size to 20–25% of daily stop — high conviction, tight stop at give low
- Gap only (no catalyst or catalyst unclear): 10% of daily stop maximum
- Never add on the give — only add on the go; adding during the give is buying weakness
- On trend days with gap: be a buyer of VWAP pullbacks throughout the day (not just at the go entry); the structure repeats intraday

## Crypto adaptation notes

- Crypto operates 24/7 so the "gap" occurs at the start of any major session (US open 9:30 AM ET, Asia open 8 PM ET, EU open 3 AM ET); price action from the prior session creates the gap-equivalent
- Most reliable gap-give-go windows in crypto: immediately after major macroeconomic data (CPI, FOMC), token listing on major exchange, or major protocol announcement during low-volume hours that then flows into US session
- The "give" in crypto is often faster and more aggressive than equities — a 5–10% retrace in 30 minutes is normal; the key is volume; if volume contracts during the retrace, the setup is valid
- Anchored VWAP from the catalyst event (e.g., anchor from the ETF approval candle) acts as the natural give support level — most powerful confluence for the go entry
- On HL perps: the funding rate during the give phase is informative — if funding drops back toward 0% during the give, weak longs are exiting = cleaner go phase ahead; if funding spikes negative (shorts piling on), the go has stronger potential

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- Buying the gap itself on the open — the entry is the go phase, NOT the gap; chasing the open is the mistake
- Buying during the give — "never buy into the give" is the first rule; the give is the setup phase, not the entry phase
- Confusing panic selling with an orderly give — if the give has expanding volume and large candles, step aside
- Taking full profit at T1 (gap high) — use the partial exit rule; hold 40–50% for the T2 measured move
- Not planning all three phases before entry: identify the gap level, the expected give support, and the go trigger before the session opens — reactive entries have much lower quality

## Example(s)

**BTC/USDC perp (HL), US session — ETF inflow data gap:**
- BTC closed at $91,000; ETF inflow data released at 5 AM ET showing record inflows
- US session opens: BTC gaps to $95,000; opening 15m bar strong
- Give: BTC pulls back to $93,200 (VWAP at $93,000) over 45 minutes on declining volume
- Tight consolidation forms: $93,000–$93,500 range for 20 minutes
- Go: BTC breaks $93,500 on 2.5× average volume; enter long at $93,600
- Stop: $92,800 (below give low); T1: $95,000 (gap high); T2: $96,800 (measured move: $2,000 added to $94,800 go entry)
- Trail: 5m 21 EMA once above $95,000

**SOL/USDC perp (HL), listing announcement gap:**
- SOL closes $148; major CEX lists new SOL-native token, increasing SOL demand
- Asia session: SOL gaps to $156; give pulls back to $152 (pre-session high = new support)
- Higher low at $152.50 (vs. give low $152); consolidation tightens
- Go: break above $154 with volume; enter $154.20; stop $151.50; T1: $156; T2: $158

**ETH/USDC perp (HL), strong sector gap — VWAP give:**
- ETH gaps up on DeFi protocol upgrade; opens $3,400 vs prior close $3,280
- Give: pulls to VWAP $3,340; volume contracts; immediate re-bid at VWAP
- Stuff-and-rubber-band: wick to $3,320; re-bids to $3,345
- Enter at VWAP reclaim $3,350; stop $3,310; T1: $3,480 (prior session high / gap level)
