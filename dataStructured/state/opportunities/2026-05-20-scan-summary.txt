# Opportunity Research Scan Summary
Date: 2026-05-20
Researcher: opportunity-researcher

## Scan Results: 3 Strong Opportunities (score >= 6)

### 1. Restaurant/Food Establishment Licenses (Score: 8)
- TAM: 700K+ licensed restaurants nationwide
- Commercial validation: Avention Media (702K+ contacts), Openmart (500K+), pricing at $0.10-0.30/contact
- Public data: FL, TX, NY offer downloadable databases
- B2B buyers: Toast/Square/Clover (400K+ restaurants powered), food distributors, equipment vendors, SaaS platforms
- Wedge: $149 vs. $5K-$15K/year subscriptions (97%+ discount)

### 2. Cannabis Dispensary/Cultivation Licenses (Score: 7)
- TAM: 30K-43K licensed cannabis businesses across 38+ legal states
- Commercial validation: eSalesClub (15K+ contacts, 95% accuracy), CampaignLake, Coldlytics, ContactLists
- Public data: CA Dept of Cannabis Control (daily updates), 38+ state registries
- B2B buyers: Dutchie/Flowhub POS (mandatory compliance software at $3.6K-$18K/year), payment processors, insurance brokers, B2B suppliers
- Smaller TAM offset by ultra-high buyer LTV (mandatory compliance software, $15K-$100K/year payment processing revenue)

### 3. Certified Fitness Professionals/Personal Trainers (Score: 7)
- TAM: 130K+ in USREPS registry, 384K+ total fitness professionals per BLS
- Commercial validation: BookYourData (25K+ verified), InfoGlobalData ($0.10-$2.00/contact pricing), 6+ vendors
- Public data: USREPS public registry (130K+ certified), NASM directory, ACSM profinder
- B2B buyers: Mindbody/Zen Planner gym software (70K+ businesses), fitness equipment vendors, CE providers (mandatory recertification), insurance brokers
- Wedge: $149 vs. $13K-$260K for full registry at per-contact pricing (87×-1,745× cost advantage)

## Opportunities Evaluated But Below Threshold (score < 6)

### Dog Groomers/Pet Services (Score: 4)
- Rejected: No standardized licensing requirement in most states
- Limited public data availability
- Voluntary certifications only

### Music Teachers/Private Tutors (Score: 4)
- Rejected: No state licensing requirement for private music instruction
- Only voluntary directory listings
- No centralized public registry

### Event Venues/Wedding Venues (Score: 4-5)
- Rejected: Business permits, not professional licenses
- No standardized public registry across states
- Commercial directories (The Knot, WeddingWire) not public data

### Yacht Brokers/Marina Operators (Score: 5-6)
- Borderline: FL and CA have public registries
- Rejected: Very small TAM (niche luxury market)
- No commercial vendor pricing validation found

### Gym/Fitness Centers as Businesses (Score: 6)
- Borderline: Commercial vendors exist at $0.10-$2.00/contact
- Rejected: Businesses rather than licensed professionals
- Public data less standardized (some state health club registration, not universal)
- Chose certified trainers (professionals) over facilities

## Research Methodology
- Web search for commercial database vendors (validates demand)
- Public data source identification (state/federal registries)
- B2B buyer segment analysis (who pays for this data and why)
- TAM estimation (addressable market size)
- Pricing validation (willingness-to-pay evidence)
- Competitive gap analysis (is there a low-cost alternative?)

## Next Steps
All three opportunity briefs (score >= 6) forwarded to CEO for approval and data-engineer assignment.
