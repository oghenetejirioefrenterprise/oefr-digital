## Preconditions

This is the **A++ variant** of the standard ORB long. It requires a confirmed fundamental catalyst in addition to all technical preconditions. This setup justifies maximum sizing because it has both directional reason (the catalyst explains WHY price should move) and technical trigger (the ORB tells you WHEN to enter).

**Catalyst quality threshold (must score ≥ 7/10):**
| Score | Catalyst type |
|-------|--------------|
| 9–10 | ETF approval, halving, major regulatory green light, BTC ATH break, major CEX listing with tokenomics event |
| 8 | Protocol upgrade (mainnet launch, major feature), institutional treasury strategy announcement, large partnership |
| 7 | Macro event (CPI softer than expected, FOMC dovish), sector-wide rotation into crypto, secondary listing on tier-1 exchange |
| < 7 | Routine news; use standard ORB playbook with smaller size |

**Technical preconditions (all required):**
- RVOL ≥ 3× 20-day average at time of break. Day-1 catalyst plays often produce 5–20× RVOL — the higher the better.
- Price holds in the **top 25–33% of the opening range** (or pre-market range) through the first 15–30 minutes. A catalyst stock that keeps fading into the lower half of its range has rejected the news; do not force the trade.
- Market internals supportive: BTC flat or rising for altcoin catalyst plays; broader crypto breadth positive.
- No significant HTF resistance within 1 ATR of the ORH.

**Strongest catalyst + technical alignment (A+++ conditions):**
- Day-1 ORB: asset is on its first trading day following the catalyst announcement.
- Day-3 breakout: Day 1 = big catalyst move and strong close; Day 2 = low-volume inside consolidation (accumulation); Day 3 = break above Day 2 high in the first 30 minutes. This is the "Day 3 breakout" pattern — one of the highest-probability multi-day setups.
- ATH breakout: asset breaks to all-time highs with the catalyst. No overhead supply = continuation with minimal resistance.

## Entry trigger

**Standard catalyst ORB entry:**
1. Price holds above the lower bound of the opening range for 15–30 minutes without a meaningful fade (top 25% of range).
2. First 5-min candle closes **in the top 25% of its own range** (bullish close signal).
3. Tape shows bids absorbing offers at ORH: large buyers lifting offers with no meaningful pullback.
4. Enter on consolidation break above opening range high with volume expansion. Do not enter before range forms.

**Gap-and-Go direct entry (high-urgency catalyst):**
For very high-magnitude catalysts (RVOL > 10×, major macro or binary event, BTC ATH break):
- If the first tick on the open does NOT go down (bids immediately step up, price climbs from first print), enter immediately on the opening drive.
- Stop at pre-market support level or first 1-min candle low. Accept higher entry in exchange for lower risk (stop distance tighter).
- This variant sacrifices precision for participation on the highest-conviction moves.

**Day-3 breakout entry:**
- Define Day 2 high before the open.
- At the open on Day 3: price pulls back to pre-market support (Day 2 close area). Enter feeler (25% size) on that support hold.
- Add full size when price **breaks above Day 2 high** on expanding volume within first 30 minutes.
- Stop: low of Day 3 session.

**Pre-macro compression breakout:**
For scheduled macro events (CPI, FOMC, token unlock, governance vote):
- Identify compression range (30–60 min before event, price in tight band).
- Trade the breakout in either direction post-event.
- Entry: break of compression range boundary (candle close outside). Stop: opposite boundary of compression range. Target: 1–2× width of compression range.

## Invalidation

- Price **fails to hold the top 25% of the opening range** for 15+ minutes and fades into lower half — the catalyst did not drive sustained buying; stand aside.
- RVOL drops below 2× within first 30 minutes — participation is fading.
- Breakout candle closes with high volume but price closes **back inside the range** (exhaustion bar, not continuation bar). High volume at the breakout level without follow-through = institutional selling into strength.
- For Day-3 breakout: if price fails to break Day 2 high within the first 30 minutes with volume, the setup is invalid for that session.
- Macro event (CPI etc.) breakout: if price snaps back inside the compression range within 5 minutes of the break = false breakout; reverse or stand aside.
- Stop: below the opening range (for day-1 catalyst), below Day 2 high (for day-3 breakout), or below pre-event compression low (for macro breakout).

## Targets

**Day-1 catalyst ORB:**
- T1: 1 ATR above breakout level (cover 30–40%).
- T2: 2 ATR (cover 30%).
- Runner: hold overnight if RVOL remains elevated and candle closes in the top 25% of the day's range. Day-1 catalyst stocks with large news deserve overnight holds when the range holds. Target: 3 ATR on Day 2 continuation.
- Exit runner on VWAP break on Day 2 or loss of the "breakout level held" criterion.

**Day-3 breakout:**
- Scale 1/3 into initial momentum surge (first 30-minute rally above Day 2 high).
- Trail remainder on 2-min 9 EMA. Exit on first 5-min close below 9 EMA.
- Always hold 10–20% as a swing piece to Day 3 close.

**ATH breakout:**
- No overhead supply → extend targets. Use 1.5× and 2× measured moves (prior base width added to breakout).
- Trail using daily higher lows when holding overnight. Exit on daily close back below prior ATH level.

**Macro compression breakout:**
- T1: 1× compression range width above breakout. Cover 50%.
- T2: 2× compression range width. Trail remainder.

## Sizing notes

- Catalyst ORB is the highest-conviction setup in this playbook. Justify 20–30% of daily stop (maximum sizing framework).
- For Day-1 catalyst: size 75–100% of max allowed by allocator if RVOL > 5× and catalyst score ≥ 8.
- For Day-3 breakout: size 50% feeler on Day-2-high hold; add to 75–100% on volume confirmation above Day-2 high.
- For ATH breakout: size up 30–50% above standard ORB size — ATH breakouts have no overhead supply (highest historical win rate).
- For macro compression breakout: size 50% of max. The direction is unknown pre-event; adjust to 75% after confirmation.
- All sizing through `core/allocator.py`. Use trade grade: catalyst ORB with RVOL > 5× and all criteria met = A-grade.

## Crypto adaptation notes

**Catalyst taxonomy for crypto:**
| Equity analog | Crypto equivalent |
|--------------|------------------|
| Earnings triple-beat | Protocol TVL milestone + token burn announcement simultaneously |
| FDA approval (binary binary catalyst) | Spot ETF approval, major regulatory clarity |
| IPO day 1 | New token listing on Hyperliquid or Binance (IDO/IEO day 1) |
| Government contract | Major CEX/protocol integration (e.g., Visa using the chain) |
| ATH breakout post-earnings | BTC breaking prior all-time high on halving cycle |
| M&A announcement | Protocol merger or consolidation (L2 merging into major ecosystem) |

**Session-specific catalyst behavior:**
- **BTC ATH breakout:** Enter on volume-confirmed break of prior ATH on Hyperliquid BTC perp. Hold as long as daily candle stays above prior ATH. Monitor funding rate — extreme positive funding (> 0.15% per 8h) near ATH = reduce size.
- **Token listing ORB (Day 1):** First 15-min range on Hyperliquid is the ORB. RVOL will typically be > 10×. Enter on reclaim of ORH if price dipped first (flush-and-rip entry highest quality); enter immediately on hold-and-go if no flush occurs.
- **CPI/FOMC impact:** BTC 15-min chart pre-CPI. Define compression range (typically 30–60 min before). Trade break in either direction. Use 0.5% of portfolio risk for the directional bet.
- **Day-3 breakout on token:** Common after token catalysts. Day 1 = big pump, strong close. Day 2 = inside day / consolidation on lower volume. Day 3 = break above Day 2 high in first 30 minutes. Use 4h and daily timeframes for crypto variants.

**Funding rate as catalyst amplifier:**
- When entering a catalyst ORB long and funding is negative (shorts paying longs), the catalyst + squeeze dynamic = maximum conviction. Size up.
- When funding is already highly positive (longs paying shorts), the crowd is positioned for the move; be willing to take profits faster and not chase the runner.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

1. **Chasing after missing the opening drive.** On the highest-quality catalyst days, the best entry is on the open or at the first pullback — not 30 minutes later after it has moved 2 ATR. If missed, wait for the Day 2 or Day 3 entry. — *How_to_buy_a_hot_stock.transcript.md*
2. **Not sizing up on A+++ setups.** "A trades win 90% of the time and you need to size those appropriately bigger." — *The_Secret_Behind_Achieving_Million-Dollar_Trading_Months.transcript.md* — The single biggest profit-reduction mistake is treating all setups with equal size.
3. **Holding Day-1 catalyst runners without an explicit overnight thesis.** Only hold overnight if: RVOL remains elevated AND close in top 25% of day's range AND catalyst is binary/major (not routine). Otherwise scalp and exit.
4. **Not checking competitor/basket behavior.** For sector catalyst plays, all correlated assets should be moving in sympathy. If the sector is NOT confirming, the individual catalyst may be priced in or weak. — *Our_Response_To_Electric_Vehicles.transcript.md*
5. **Entering macro compression breakouts too early** (before the event) on anticipation. The entry is AFTER the event — when the actual direction is confirmed by the candle close outside the compression range.
6. **Ignoring funding rate on ATH breakouts.** Extreme positive funding near ATH is a key warning sign to reduce position size even while the trend continues.

## Example(s)

**Example 1 — BTC ATH breakout with macro catalyst:**
- BTC prior ATH: $99,800. Catalyst: spot ETF approval for major fund (8/10 catalyst score).
- RVOL at 14:00 UTC: 7× 20-day average. BTC gaps to $101,200 at 13:30 UTC.
- First 15-min range: $100,800–$101,500 (holding top 30% of the day's gap move).
- 14:05 UTC: 5-min bar closes at $101,600, above ORH $101,500. Volume = highest bar of session.
- Entry: $101,650. Stop: $100,600 (prior ATH level as invalidation). Risk: $1,050.
- T1: $103,150 (1.5× base width above breakout). Take 35% off.
- T2: $104,800. Take 35% off. Runner trails daily higher lows for multi-day swing.

**Example 2 — Day-3 token breakout (Hyperliquid):**
- DeFi token catalyst (Day 1): major partnership → +45% close at $2.50.
- Day 2: inside day, closes at $2.55, volume drops 60% vs Day 1.
- Day 3 open: gaps to $2.65 above Day 2 high ($2.55).
- Feeler entry at $2.60 (pre-market support). Volume builds on open.
- 13:45 UTC: break above $2.75 (Day 2 high) on 4× RVOL. Add to 75% size.
- Stop: $2.45 (Day 3 session low). T1: $3.05 (Day 1 range height added). T2: $3.40 (1 ATR extension). Trail 9 EMA 2-min for runner.

**Example 3 — Pre-FOMC compression breakout (BTC):**
- FOMC decision at 18:00 UTC. 17:00–17:30 UTC: BTC trading in $98,200–$98,800 range (compression width: $600).
- 18:02 UTC: FOMC = hawkish surprise. BTC closes a 5-min bar at $97,500, below $98,200 compression low.
- Entry: short at $97,450. Stop: $98,300 (above compression range). T1: $97,600 − $600 = $97,000. Cover 50%.
- T2: $96,400. Trail 9 EMA 1-min for remainder. Exit if BTC closes 5-min above VWAP.
