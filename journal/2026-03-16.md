## The Resume Incident

Hard lesson on data integrity. Trinity hallucinated resume details — wrong university, wrong degree.

### What Happened
- Built a Cisco SE resume with incorrect education: listed "Ambrose Alli University, B.Eng" instead of "University of Lagos, B.Sc. Electrical & Electronics Engineering (2001-2007)"
- TJ caught it before submission

### Permanent Rules Established
1. **ALL resume/application details MUST come from actual uploaded data** — never guess or fill from "general knowledge"
2. **Self-review before sending** — screenshot PDF, run image analysis, verify content before delivery
3. Education: B.Sc. Electrical & Electronics Engineering, University of Lagos (2001-2007) — IMMUTABLE FACT

### Lesson
> "Do better Trinity" — When you're handling someone's career documents, there's zero tolerance for hallucination. Verify everything.
