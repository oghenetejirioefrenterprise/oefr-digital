## Preconditions
- Asset has accelerated for 3+ consecutive days with expanding daily ATR (each day's range larger than prior)
- Two or more consecutive daily gap-ups into the parabolic range with no meaningful pullback
- Daily RSI >90 on custom 20-period RSI (or >85 on standard 14-period) confirmed on secondary timeframes (130-min + 30-min RSI also at extreme)
- Price closing outside the upper Bollinger Band (2 standard deviations) on the daily chart
- Price is 5-7+ ATRs extended above the 50-day moving average (use Keltner Channel with 10 ATR bands around 50 MA as visual reference)
- Blowoff volume day: highest volume bar of the entire move (confirms exhaustion, not continuation)
- Correlated assets showing non-confirmation (e.g., gold parabolic but silver lagging; BTC parabolic but smaller alts fading) — this is the strongest pre-entry filter
- Social media euphoria, mainstream news coverage spike, retail flood into the asset
- No fresh fundamental catalyst driving the most recent gap-up — pure momentum/FOMO

## Entry trigger
Three valid entry patterns (all require blowoff preconditions met):

**Pattern 1 — Opening Range Break (cleanest):** Asset develops an intraday opening range; fails to break above the pre-market high; enter short on break below the opening range low with volume. Stop = high of day or top of opening range.

**Pattern 2 — Down-Through-Open:** Asset rallies off the open, quickly reverses back below the opening print; enter short on break of the open or failure to hold above intraday VWAP. Stop = pre-market high.

**Pattern 3 — Kill Candle / Blow-Off Top with Lower High:** A single wide-range candle (highest volume of the move) smashes through VWAP and multiple supports; wait for the first lower high on the 5-min chart below the kill candle high; enter short on that lower high rejection. Stop = above kill candle high.

Multi-indicator gate before entry: RSI >90 (daily) + 130-min RSI also overbought + first 15-min candle closes below prior bar = highest conviction entry.

## Invalidation
- Price reclaims the high of day with expanding volume and tape shows absorbed offers (forced-buyer squeeze still active)
- New all-time high beyond the parabolic extreme on the backside day
- Correlated laggard suddenly catches up and confirms the lead (non-confirmation disappears)
- IV (crypto: DVOL/funding rate) continues to expand rather than compress — momentum continuation, not exhaustion

## Targets
- T1: Prior day's low (cover 25-33% of position; do not hold for full reversal on one entry)
- T2: 5-day moving average (cover another 25-33%)
- T3: 38.2% retrace of the full parabolic move from base
- T4 (extended): 50% retrace — trail remainder using hourly lower highs; exit when price stops making new lows or tapes buying

For single-session scalp: close the most recent gap; then prior week's VWAP. Cover in pieces; parabolic reversals can be violent both ways.

## Sizing notes
- Start at 25% of planned risk on the turn signal; add to full planned risk only after breakdown confirmed by price action (first 15-min close below prior bar)
- A+ blow-off top setups (all criteria met + collaborating traders independently identify same setup) justify up to 40% of daily stop
- Reduce size if no correlated non-confirmation exists — single-asset fades carry more variance
- Never hold for full mean reversion on one entry; always cover into flush moves

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Liquidation cascades accelerate the move dramatically: after the kill candle, the cascade creates faster, steeper drops than equity analogs. Cover T1 faster (within 30 min vs equity 90 min).
- Funding rate is the crypto-specific exhaustion signal: funding >0.1% per 8h combined with RSI >90 and 3+ gap-up days = near-certain exhaustion setup. Use funding flip from positive to negative as secondary confirmation after entry.
- DVOL (Deribit volatility index) or implied vol on BTC options: if IV blows out on the parabolic day, the unwind typically happens Thursday-Friday as options expire — time entries accordingly.
- Altcoin parabolics are more extreme and more frequent than equity parabolics; the same exhaustion signals (RSI extreme, volume spike, non-confirmation) apply but the reversals are sharper. Size down accordingly on low-liquidity tokens.
- Use anchored VWAP from the last gap-up open as short reference; if price fails to hold above that VWAP on a recovery attempt = short entry.
- Watch for the "correlated laggard" signal: if BTC is parabolic and SOL, ETH, or sector alts are failing to confirm new highs, that non-confirmation is the strongest pre-entry filter.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Shorting the parabolic too early (day 1 or 2 of the run) before exhaustion criteria are met — the trade is entering on day 3-4 with all criteria confirmed, not predicting the top
- Fading into a gamma squeeze (IV still expanding) — wait for IV contraction or funding rate reversal
- Holding for the full reversal on a single entry instead of covering in pieces into flush moves
- Being undersized on A+ blow-off tops ("your biggest risk is being undersized" — size up when the see-it moment arrives)
- Not waiting for the kill candle to print before entering — the first sign of exhaustion is the candle itself, not the gap
- Ignoring correlated non-confirmation as the filter — this is what separates A+ setups from B setups
- Covering too early (at 75% instead of 80%+ on the move) due to fear; use pre-defined targets to remove emotion

**Crypto altcoin blow-off (generalized):** Token gaps up 3 consecutive days totaling 200%+; daily RSI 92; funding rate +0.15% per 8h; smaller related tokens failing to confirm new highs; backside day opens with gap; opening range forms; fails to reclaim pre-market high; short on ORB break; covered 50% at prior day low, trail to 5-day MA.

**Gold (GLD) parabolic:** Multi-gap parabolic with daily RSI >85; silver (correlated asset) not confirming; London close fails to make new high; first lower high on 1h chart; short below lower-high candle; covered into 38.2% retrace.
