## Preconditions

1. **Benchmark is declining or neutral.** BTC or ETH is in a downtrend or at a key resistance level on the timeframe of interest. Shorting an individual token into a ripping BTC uptrend is fighting the tide.
2. **The target token is underperforming the benchmark.** BTC is -2%; the token is -5% or flat — showing distribution. The tokens that cannot bounce when BTC bounces are the best shorts.
3. **Structural deterioration (one or more):**
   - Price is below its anchored VWAP (anchored to prior session, catalyst, or 2-day period) and bounces fail below it.
   - Lower highs forming on the 5-min or 15-min chart.
   - Prior support level (which is now resistance) holding as a ceiling on bounces.
   - Bearish flag or descending channel consolidation following a sharp move down.
   - RSI bearish divergence on 1H or 4H: price makes a new high but RSI makes a lower high — reversal setup.
4. **Overhead supply confirmed:** Prior broken support is now clear resistance. Volume footprint shows large offers at the supply level (tape analysis or order book depth on Hyperliquid).
5. **Backside confirmation (for pump-and-dump setups):** Token pumped 50–200%+ in 1–2 days/sessions. Is now failing to reclaim 2-day anchored VWAP on repeated attempts. Each bounce is a lower high vs. the prior bounce.
6. **Funding rate:** Positive or neutral funding on the target token while price is declining = longs are trapped and paying. Elevated positive funding in a downtrend is a short edge amplifier.
7. **Sector under pressure:** The entire basket (L1s, memecoins, AI tokens) is weak; the target token is the weakest member — most distribution, largest % underperformance.

## Entry trigger

**Scalp (intraday flush setups):**
- Token bounces to anchored VWAP or 2-day VWAP zone, fails to close above it, curls lower. Enter short on the close of the rejection candle (5-min) below VWAP. Stop above the VWAP rejection wick high.
- Backside fade: Token had a massive pump; post-pump recovery push fails below overhead supply (prior base / 2-day VWAP). Enter short when price curls lower on volume at the supply zone.
- Flush-and-fail: Token undercuts a key level (pre-market low, prior day low), bounces briefly on covering, then stalls below the level. Short the stall when tape goes offered (bids lift, asks don't move).

**Intraday:**
- Tiered entry (3-step scaling into size): (a) 25% of risk at first VWAP rejection — confirms weak tape; (b) add to 75% on breakdown below the day's key support level with volume confirmation; (c) add final tranche on consolidation below that level (the "big-dog" confirmation where price coils and then resumes lower).
- BTC or benchmark breaks a key support level with volume: immediately short the weakest token in the sector basket. Confirmation = volume on the index break + individual token making new lows simultaneously.
- Opening drive short: Both BTC (macro proxy) and target token are below their key levels at the open. Tape offers at resistance. Enter short within first 5 minutes of the session with stop above both key levels.
- False sector strength fade: Token was a sector leader in early session; by mid-session it is below VWAP and 21 EMA. Bounce into declining 21 EMA + VWAP = short entry. Stop above VWAP wick high.
- RSI divergence (bearish): Token makes new intraday high but 15-min or 1H RSI makes a lower high — enter fade on confirmation candle below the prior swing high. Stop above the divergence high. Target 0.5–1x ATR reversion.

**Swing:**
- Two-negative-catalyst short: Token had one negative catalyst, recovered, then a second negative catalyst hits while at resistance — this is a high-probability swing short. Gap down on second catalyst, dead-cat bounce fails below prior support, enter short.
- Sell-the-news (anticipated event): Token pumped into a widely anticipated announcement (upgrade, launch, partnership). Event occurs, price gaps up, fails to hold the gap. Enter short on intraday tape failure of the gap. Stop above gap-open high.
- Multi-day VWAP failure short (swing): Token showed strength for multiple days then engulfed that whole move in one session. Multi-day VWAP zone (rolling average of prior days' VWAPs) acts as resistance. Enter short on first bounce into the VWAP zone that fails with a lower high vs. the zone's top.
- BTC bearish flag: BTC in confirmed downtrend (consecutive lower highs/lows), consolidating in a bear flag below declining 20-day MA. Short on break below the bear flag support with volume. Stop above flag consolidation. Target measured move.

## Invalidation

- **Scalp:** Token closes a 5-min candle above the VWAP rejection level or above the short entry's key level. Cover immediately — thesis invalidated.
- **Intraday:** Token shows relative strength vs. BTC — rallies more on a BTC bounce. Cover if token makes a new intraday high above the entry level's key resistance.
- **Swing:** Token closes above the 2-day VWAP / anchored VWAP on daily chart with volume. Reclaims the level that defined the short thesis — that is your stop level.
- **General:** BTC turns and rips higher — all relative weakness shorts weaken as the benchmark rises. If BTC reclaims its key level and holds, reduce or exit relative weakness shorts regardless of individual token action.
- Funding rate swings from positive to deeply negative after entry: short squeeze risk has increased. Consider tightening stop.

## Targets

**Scalp:**
- T1: Next support or -3–5% from entry. Take 40–50% here.
- T2: Prior consolidation base / origin of the pump / 50% retrace of the run. Exit 30–40% here.
- T3: Trailing stop with 5-min higher lows as signal to cover.

**Intraday:**
- T1: Prior day low / next key support. Partial cover (40%).
- T2: Measured move from breakdown / -8–12% from entry. Cover 40%.
- T3: Trail with tape (cover when buying pressure absorbs supply).

**Swing:**
- T1: Prior major support / -15–20% from entry. Partial cover (40%).
- T2: Origin of the move (pre-pump price) for backside trades. Cover 40%.
- T3: Trail with daily structure; hold if BTC trend remains intact to the downside.

**Note:** When price goes parabolic to the downside (capitulation candle — 5x normal volume, sharp spike lower), that is often a short-term bottom. Cover a portion on climactic volume, as these often produce sharp relief bounces.

## Sizing notes

- **Tiered entry:** Never enter full size on a weak-looking chart. Enter 25% on the first signal; add to 75% on breakdown confirmation; add final tranche on consolidation confirmation. This prevents fading bounces that become trend reversals.
- **Single-token max:** 2–3% of account. Relative weakness shorts in high-beta alts carry gap risk (sudden news reversal). Size accordingly.
- **Do not short in low-liquidity windows.** Thin book on Hyperliquid alts (e.g., 02:00–06:00 UTC) = slippage risk and fake moves. Stick to active sessions (08:00–16:00 UTC for US overlap, or Asia open 00:00–04:00 UTC for Asia-driven moves).
- **SSR (Short Sale Restriction) equivalent in crypto:** Some tokens on Hyperliquid have high short interest / negative funding already. If funding is deeply negative before you enter, the edge is reduced — other shorts are already in and squeeze risk is elevated.
- **In high-volatility regimes (macro fear cascades):** Full-size shorts are appropriate for the weakest tokens — these environments are the highest-probability periods for relative weakness shorts.

## Crypto adaptation notes

- **Sector = L1/L2/memecoin/AI-token/DePIN.** The weakest token in a falling sector is the highest-probability short. Scan for the coin with: (a) largest % underperformance vs. BTC, (b) failed VWAP reclaim attempts, (c) positive funding (trapped longs).
- **Benchmark = BTC or ETH.** Short a token when it cannot participate in a BTC bounce — that is the definition of distribution. "The stocks that can't rally when the market bounces are your best shorts" maps directly to crypto.
- **Pump-and-dump cycle (low-cap alts):** The backside of a 100–500% pump in a low-cap token is one of the cleanest relative weakness short setups in crypto. After the day-1 or day-2 pump, the token typically loses 60–90% over the next 3–7 sessions. Short day-2/3 lower high vs. 2-day anchored VWAP.
- **Competitor negative catalyst / sympathy short:** When a major protocol gets hacked, has a governance failure, or releases bad TVL data, competing tokens in the same sector often get sympathy sold. Short the consolidation breakdown in the competitor token — confirmed by: high relative volume on the competitor, tight range forming at lows, tape showing aggressive offers.
- **Sell-the-news in crypto:** Major anticipated events (mainnet launches, ETF approvals already priced in, token unlocks, CEX listings) are reliably "sell the news" setups. The crowd is positioned long; distribution begins at the event. Short the failed gap-hold.
- **FOMC / macro catalyst relative weakness:** Fed rate decisions, CPI prints — when BTC loses its anchored VWAP from the announcement time, short the highest-beta altcoin with the most relative weakness vs. BTC. Exit when BTC reclaims its anchored VWAP.
- **Funding rate as short entry amplifier:** Positive funding in a downtrend means longs are paying shorts — time is literally on your side. Deeply negative funding means shorts are crowded and a squeeze is the primary risk.
- **Anchored VWAP anchor points for crypto:** Catalyst day (token launch, major event), session open (00:00 UTC), or the top of the pump for backside trades.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- **Shorting without benchmark confirmation.** Going short on individual token weakness when BTC is holding steady or grinding up — the market can carry weak tokens higher temporarily. Wait for BTC to confirm weakness or at least neutrality.
- **Entering full size on the first pop.** Backside trades fail frequently on the first attempt. The token will often make one or two aggressive reclaim attempts before the downside resumes. Enter small on the first rejection; add only on confirmation.
- **Shorting into deeply negative funding.** If funding is already -0.1% per 8h or worse, the short squeeze risk is high — other shorts are underwater and any bounce causes a cascade of covers. Wait for funding to reset toward neutral.
- **No predefined invalidation.** The most common ruin in short selling is not having a hard stop. Every short entry needs an exact price level above which the trade is wrong. "I'll see how it acts" is not a valid plan.
- **Fading the sector head-of-snake.** Shorting the strongest token in a falling sector because it "has more to fall" is a common error. The weakest token (most underperformance, failed bounces, overhead supply) is the correct short. The strong one may hold or reverse.
- **Holding through macro reversals.** If BTC reverses sharply on surprise positive news (ETF approval, policy pivot), all relative weakness shorts become squeezes instantly. Have a clear BTC-based exit signal regardless of individual token position.
- **Ignoring SSR / squeeze dynamics.** Very high short interest (deeply negative funding) + any positive catalyst = violent squeeze. Know the funding rate before entering every short.
- **Chasing the flush.** Entering a short after a token is already down 20–30% in the session without a defined level to enter at — this is bottom-fishing in reverse. The correct entry is at the VWAP rejection or breakdown level, not mid-flush.

## Example(s)

**Example 1 — Backside Short on Extended Alt (Scalp/Intraday, High Confidence):**
Low-cap AI token pumped 300% on day 1 (ecosystem announcement). Day 2: Opens lower, attempts to reclaim the 2-day anchored VWAP at $0.85. Price pushes to $0.83, fails — lower high vs. yesterday's high of $0.94. Volume on the push is 30% of day-1 volume (weak conviction). Entry: $0.81 (short on curl lower from $0.83). Stop: $0.87 (above the failed reclaim wick). T1: $0.65 (prior consolidation base). T2: $0.45 (origin of pump). Cover 60% at T1, trail remainder.

**Example 2 — Relative Weakness Short when BTC Bounces (Intraday, High Confidence):**
BTC drops 4%, then bounces 2% into VWAP. SOL bounces 2.5% (in line with BTC — neutral). ARB is flat / up only 0.3% on the BTC bounce. ARB is showing relative weakness. ARB is also below its 5-min VWAP and making lower highs. Entry: ARB at $0.91 (short on the failure of the bounce attempt). Stop: $0.935 (above prior lower high). T1: $0.85. T2: Prior day low $0.80. Cover in tranches.

**Example 3 — FOMC Macro Catalyst Relative Weakness Short (Intraday, High Confidence):**
Fed announces hawkish minutes at 14:00 UTC. BTC drops 3% and loses its anchored VWAP from 14:00 UTC. ETH loses VWAP as well. SOL is down 5% vs. BTC's 3% — showing relative weakness. Short SOL on the VWAP rejection at 14:20 UTC. Stop: SOL reclaims its anchored VWAP. Target: ATR extension below VWAP; exit when BTC reclaims its anchored VWAP (thesis complete).

**Example 4 — RSI Bearish Divergence Fade (Scalp/Intraday, High Confidence):**
Memecoin makes a new session high at $0.0412. 15-min RSI is at 65 — lower than the prior high of 78 when price was at $0.0398. Bearish divergence confirmed. Entry: Short $0.0409 on the confirmation candle (first 15-min candle that fails to hold the new high and closes red). Stop: $0.0418 (above divergence high). T1: VWAP at $0.0385. T2: 0.5x ATR reversion to $0.0370.

**Example 5 — Two-Negative-Catalyst Swing Short:**
L1 protocol token had a smart contract exploit (negative catalyst 1). Token recovered 40% over 2 weeks back to resistance. A second exploit occurs (negative catalyst 2) while token is at resistance. Entry: Dead-cat bounce the next day fails below prior support. Short. Stop: Above prior resistance. Target: -20% / pre-recovery level. Two negative catalysts compound; this is a high-probability swing short setup.
