## Preconditions
- Macro data misses meaningfully (CPI hotter than expected, FOMC hawkish surprise, NFP miss with wage pressure) — or a negative protocol/market event hits crypto (exploit disclosure, regulatory enforcement, team exit, major unlock overhang confirmed).
- Pre-built bear scenario game plan: know your target levels, stop, and sizing before the release.
- Tape confirms sellers in control within the first 30–60 seconds: offers absorbing bids, no persistent green-tape lift.
- Ideally, price has already been extended or in a prior uptrend (the "sell-the-news" setup) — sellers have overhead supply to unload.
- For equities-analog crypto: if BTC/ETH has run 40–50% into the catalyst event and then misses, overhead supply from earlier longs creates sustained selling pressure.
- (Crypto-specific) Funding rate on HL perps is extreme positive before the negative catalyst — longs will be squeezed out, amplifying the move.

## Entry trigger
1. **Immediate short on negative print:** On a clearly bearish macro print, short the high-beta basket within the first 30 seconds. BTC and ETH perps on Hyperliquid are the primary vehicles.
2. **First consolidation breakdown:** If the initial dump is too fast to enter, wait for the first 5–8 minute consolidation after the flush; short the break below the consolidation low with volume confirmation.
3. **Resistance rejection short:** On bounces back to VWAP or to a key resistance level (prior support broken, pre-market high, VWAP), short when the tape shows sellers stepping in — offers not lifting, bids pulling.
4. **Sell-the-news short (gap-up fails):** If asset gaps up on a catalyst but had already run 40%+ into the event, short when the gap-up fails to hold and breaks the opening low or prior day's support.
5. **News breakdown continuation:** After the initial news flush, if price consolidates in a tight range for 5–6 minutes and the tape shows no real buyers, short into the first pop above the consolidation high. Target: new lows.
6. **Sympathy short:** When a major token gets hit by negative news, identify the most correlated alts and short those as well (they typically lag the primary move by minutes).

## Invalidation
- Tape flips: bids step in aggressively, buyers absorb the offer with volume; the "flush and reclaim" pattern forms — stand aside or flip long.
- Price reclaims VWAP on the 5-min chart with expanding buy volume.
- Funding rate on HL perps goes deeply negative rapidly (>0.15% per 8h short-side) — short is crowded; reduce size.
- Macro number is revised or follow-up commentary is dovish (e.g., Fed speakers walking back a hawkish print) — exit shorts.
- For individual token short: price holds above the prior day's high; supply is absorbed.

## Targets
- **T1:** 1–2 ATR from entry; cover 30–50% of position.
- **T2:** Prior key support level; prior daily lows; gap fill area.
- **T3:** On trend-day shorts with strong negative internals, trail with 5-min 20 EMA and hold for the close near the low.
- Conference call / extended negative catalyst: scale out into each flush of new lows; cover remaining at 2 ATR from entry.

## Sizing notes
- **Immediate short on hard macro miss:** Start at 50% of max size; add on first resistance rejection if trend holds.
- **Sell-the-news short:** Full size on the breakdown of opening low; stop just above the opening high.
- **Leverage:** HL perps 3–5x for macro catalyst shorts; do not use max leverage — leave room to add on confirmation.
- On trend-day shorts: trade to hold, not scalp. Do not cover prematurely at the first green tick.
- Size smaller when the news is ambiguous or mixed; size larger when the print is clear and unambiguous.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Hot CPI print (higher inflation than expected) → BTC/ETH short immediately at US cash open (~13:30 UTC) on HL perps.
- FOMC hawkish surprise (unexpected rate hike, dot-plot uplift) → same immediate short basket execution.
- Crypto-specific negative catalysts: protocol exploit/hack disclosure, SEC enforcement action, major exchange insolvency news, USDT/USDC depegging scare.
- Funding rate spike into the event (extreme positive) = fuel for the short when the negative catalyst hits; longs get liquidated cascade-style.
- Token-specific: team exit (core devs resign), unlock cliff approaching, TVL collapse — these are the "earnings miss + guidance cut" equivalents for crypto.
- "Conference call negative guidance" → protocol post-mortem call or team AMA with bad news is the crypto analog.
- Sell-the-news short is especially powerful in crypto for ETF approval events, halving events, or major upgrade announcements that the market pre-runs by 40%+ then reverses sharply.
