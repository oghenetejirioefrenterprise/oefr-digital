# Backside Trend Short

## Preconditions
- Asset made a large Day 1 move (>30%) and closed near the high of day (front-side momentum established)
- Day 2 (or Day 3): price gaps below prior day VWAP, OR opens flat and immediately fails to hold above Day 1 VWAP on first attempt
- Lower highs forming on the 5-min chart (each bounce weaker than the last)
- Tape showing: offers stepping down, bids not absorbing, volume contracting on bounces (relative to Day 1 volume)
- Anchored VWAP from Day 1 open is now acting as resistance (price cannot close above it)
- Short-side entry gating: confirm Day 1 closed near lows (bottom 25% of range for multi-day continuation thesis), or Day 2 gap-down below Day 1 low confirms backside

## Entry trigger
**Primary entry — Day 2 VWAP failure short:**
Price touches Day 1 anchored VWAP from below and shows tape weakness (offers stacking, bids lifting); short on VWAP failure rejection with stop above Day 1 VWAP. This is the highest-probability entry.

**Secondary entry — lower high short (3-day multi-day approach):**
- Day 1: first failed attempt to make new high after morning high established; probe short against morning high (25% of planned size)
- Day 2: open below prior day close or lower high vs Day 1 high; add to short against prior day high (total 75% of planned size)
- Day 3: continuation with full size as swing traders capitulate

**Tape confirmation gate:** Before entering, confirm tape shows offer-side dominance: bids stepping back, offers refreshing at resistance, speed of upticks slowing (tape turning heavy).

## Invalidation
- Price closes above Day 1 anchored VWAP on sustained volume with buyers absorbing all offers
- Stock makes new intraday high above prior session's high on volume
- Tape shows aggressive bid stacking (not just price level) — forced buyers (short coverers) stepping in
- Broader market provides strong directional tailwind that lifts even backside names

## Targets
- T1: Day 1 open (or Day 2 VWAP) — cover 33-50% of position
- T2: Prior day low (below which there is "free air" — no prior support)
- T3: 50% retrace of the full parabolic move
- T4 (swing): Return to origin of the move (base before the run)
- Trail remainder using 5-min lower highs; cover when tape shows buyers returning (bids stepping up aggressively at a level)

## Sizing notes
- Day 1 probe: 25% of planned risk (test entry, not full size)
- Day 2 add on confirmation: 75% of planned risk
- Day 3 if thesis holds: can bring to full risk as capitulation begins
- A+ setups (all criteria met, tape confirmed, Day 2 gap below Day 1 close): up to 40% of daily stop
- Reduce size on low-float crypto tokens due to liquidity and squeeze risk
- Cover partial on every new low; recycle shorts on each VWAP rejection

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Funding rate is the most powerful crypto-specific confirmation: if funding flips from positive to negative as price fails Day 1 VWAP, that confirms sellers are in control and shorts are no longer crowded
- Liquidation cascades fuel faster exhaustions than equity analogs: the backside move in crypto often completes in 2-3 hours what takes 2-3 days in equities. Take T1 and T2 faster.
- Low-cap altcoin backsides on Hyperliquid: after a pump-and-dump, use anchored VWAP from the pump start (catalyst day open) as the primary resistance level. Short the backside when price fails to reclaim it.
- BTC/ETH backside: use multi-day VWAP anchored from the high as overhead resistance; enter short on lower high rejection below that level; trail using 4h lower highs
- "Head of snake" timing: if BTC shows backside weakness first, time altcoin backside entries when BTC shows the first lower high. The laggard alt follows within 20-30 minutes.
- Asia session high acts as the crypto equivalent of pre-market high — failure to reclaim Asia session high in US session = early backside signal

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Shorting Day 1 of a momentum move — never short Day 1; wait for confirmation of failure on Day 2
- Entering the backside at the top (first red candle) instead of waiting for Day 2 VWAP failure — the first pullback on Day 2 is often a trap
- Holding through a VWAP reclaim: if price closes above Day 1 VWAP on volume, cover immediately — the thesis is invalidated
- Taking a full-size position on Day 2 before tape confirms (making the Day 1 probe too large)
- Not recycling shorts: on multi-day backside trades, take partial profits on each flush and re-short on every VWAP rejection rather than holding one position
- Failing to cover into parabolic flush moves (greed) — cover into sharp moves down where volume increases, not into slow grinds

## Example(s)
**VFS (IPO) Day 1 and Day 2 (from trade review):** Day 1 run-up; Day 2 gap below prior day lows confirms backside; short on pop into Day 1 VWAP; covered into flush; 9 EMA used as trailing stop.

**Low-cap altcoin backside (generalized):** Token pumps 80% on Day 1 on narrative; Day 2 opens flat, fails to reclaim Day 1 VWAP on first attempt; funding flips negative; short on the VWAP rejection with stop above Day 1 VWAP; covered T1 at Day 1 open, T2 at prior week's key support.

**MSTR overextension (equity, crypto-correlated):** Stock up 3+ days accelerating; Day 1 intraday morning high rejected; afternoon lower highs formed; short against morning high (25% size); Day 2 lower high vs Day 1 high = add to full size; covered into 30% retrace.
