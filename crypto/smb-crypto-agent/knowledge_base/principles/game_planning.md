## Core principles

### Asset selection

- **Asset selection is foundational**: News catalyst (unusual beat/miss) + high relative volume (RVOL > 2) + tight spread + moves away from price + above/below daily range. Get selection right before anything else. Sources: Five_Things_Profitable_Traders_Do_FULL_WEBINAR, 6_Keys_for_Trading_Earning_Season, A_Conversation_With_A_Developing_Trader
- **RVOL as primary filter**: RVOL above 2 = in-play; above 3 = high confidence. Never trade a name that isn't clearly in-play from a volume standpoint. Sources: Five_Things_Profitable_Traders_Do_FULL_WEBINAR, The_6_Trading_Rules_I_Learned_From_1_Year_of_Trading, The_Daily_Report_Card_with_a_7_figure_trader
- **VWAP as bias anchor**: VWAP is not just a level but a directional indicator. Holding below VWAP on rally attempts confirms short bias; reclaiming VWAP flips it. After 10:30 AM, only trade in the VWAP direction unless a confirmed reclaim occurs. Sources: The_DDD_Short_Idea_SMB_Midday_Recap, The_6_Trading_Rules_I_Learned_From_1_Year_of_Trading, A_Live_Trading_Event_Sydney
- **Quality over quantity**: Narrow focus to 2-3 names maximum at the open. One clean setup beats ten mediocre trades. Sources: 6_Keys_for_Trading_Earning_Season, A_Conversation_With_A_Developing_Trader, 8_Essential_Trading_Lessons

### Pre-market game planning

- **Pre-identify levels, not predictions**: Before the session, define specific entry levels, stops, and targets. The game plan sets levels — it does not predict direction. Sources: 101512_Live_From_The_Trading_Floor, Always_plan_for_market_reversals, Beating_Supply_Chain_Constraints
- **Scenario planning for macro events**: Build bull/bear/neutral scenario game plans before data releases (CPI, FOMC, NFP). Know your action for each outcome before it prints — you cannot improvise in real time. Sources: 10_Key_Strategies_to_Trade_an_Economic_News_Release, A_money_making_strategy_used_by_top_professionals, The_3_Trading_Problems_You_NEED_to_Solve
- **Set price alerts, never chase**: Pre-set alerts at key levels. If price runs away from your level before you can act, the trade is over — move on rather than chasing. Sources: The_6_Trading_Rules_I_Learned_From_1_Year_of_Trading, A_Mistake_Traders_Must_Correct
- **Plan both directions**: Before the session, define upside and downside scenarios for every trade. Continuation bias kills traders; assume reversal is always possible. Sources: Always_plan_for_market_reversals, A_Conversation_With_A_Developing_Trader

### Trend day vs. chop identification

- **Identify trend day in the first 30 minutes**: Use TICK (above zero sustained), ADD (>1000), VOLD, and VWAP hold to declare a trend day early. If confirmed, switch to hold-not-scalp mode. Sources: 10_Key_Strategies_to_Trade_an_Economic_News_Release, AM_Meeting_October_15th
- **On trend days, trade to hold**: Do not cover early on news-driven big moves. The math requires letting winners run. Buy pullbacks to VWAP or 15m EMA rather than chasing the open. Sources: 10_Key_Strategies_to_Trade_an_Economic_News_Release, How_to_Exit_Your_Winning_Trades_Properly
- **Adapt to regime**: Trend days require different tools than choppy days. In chop, reduce size, widen targets, and increase patience. Do not use fade strategies during confirmed trend days. Sources: 9_Trading_Lessons_Essential_for_Todays_Market, Bad_trader_habits_that_you_must_fix

### Entry timing and session windows

- **Avoid No Man's Land (NML)**: NML is inside the established intraday range. Entering there gives equal odds of hitting stop or target. Size down to 25-50% of normal inside NML; skip if possible. Sources: Avoid_This_Terrible_Trading_Mistake
- **Avoid midday 12-2 PM**: Low volume, choppy, setups fail at higher rates. Resume after 2 PM when institutional activity resumes. Sources: The_6_Trading_Rules_I_Learned_From_1_Year_of_Trading, The_Daily_Report_Card_with_a_7_figure_trader
- **Volatility calibration**: High VIX environments demand smaller size and wider stops. Historic volatility events (VIX > 40) require dramatically reduced position size. In volatility spikes, overshoots to downside become buys and vice versa. Sources: AM_Meeting_February_5th, AM_Meeting_February_6th, AM_Meeting_October_15th

### Technical level quality

- **Rate your levels**: Only trade support/resistance levels rated 7/10 or higher in significance. Do not trade arbitrary levels. More tests of a resistance level before the break = more stored energy = more explosive breakout. Sources: The_DDD_Short_Idea_SMB_Midday_Recap, Breakout_Trade_with_Mike_Bellafiore
- **Relative weakness/strength selection**: Use relative strength/weakness for side selection. Independent weakness (stock underperforming market during a rally) is the highest-conviction short signal. Sector laggards often follow the sector leader. Sources: 101512_Live_From_The_Trading_Floor, The_DDD_Short_Idea_SMB_Midday_Recap
- **Catalysts shift the odds**: Same technical pattern is higher probability with a real fundamental driver. Know exactly what news is driving a move before trading it. Fake news spikes reverse quickly. Sources: Beating_Supply_Chain_Constraints, A_money_making_strategy_used_by_top_professionals

### Sizing and conviction management

- **Sizing discipline by setup grade**: Risk full allocation only on playbook A+ setups. Reduce size on exploratory trades. Grow new setups with minimum size (1 share or 1 contract) until statistically validated. Sources: The_3_Trading_Problems_You_NEED_to_Solve, The_Daily_Report_Card_with_a_7_figure_trader, Bad_trader_habits_that_you_must_fix
- **Overtrading degrades edge**: Most PnL comes from a small percentage of trades. Trade your best setups, not all setups. Overconviction leads to outsized losses — size down when wrong. Sources: Bad_trader_habits_that_you_must_fix, 3_Simple_Ideas_to_Dramatically_Improve_Your_Trading
- **Define the one good trade**: Before the open, define the single highest-conviction trade for the day. If you get that, you can stop. Confidence to wait separates elite traders from reactive ones. Sources: A_Conversation_With_A_Developing_Trader, 6_Keys_for_Trading_Earning_Season

### Playbook and process

- **Playbook-based decisions eliminate indecision**: Discipline comes from trading your playbook, not from willpower. If you cannot name the setup, skip it. Sources: Five_Things_Profitable_Traders_Do_FULL_WEBINAR, 6_Must-Have_Rules_for_Trading_Psychology
- **Focus on process, not P&L**: Process and execution focus produces better outcomes than P&L obsession. Profitable traders try to improve daily — losing traders chase the number. Sources: Bad_trader_habits_that_you_must_fix, Five_Things_Profitable_Traders_Do_FULL_WEBINAR
- **Backtest before sizing up**: Backtest then forward-test before risking real capital. Need a minimum of 32 occurrences for statistical significance. A backtested edge justifies 60% of daily stop; without it, use standard sizing. Sources: 4_Techniques_that_Create_Winning_Trading_Strategies, Backtesting_for_Trading_Success

## Crypto adaptation notes

- **No session windows, but session analogs exist**: Crypto trades 24/7, eliminating the equities open/midday/close rhythm. However, NY open (9:30–11 AM ET), London close, and US close still produce elevated volume and setup quality. Treat 12–2 AM UTC as the crypto equivalent of midday chop.
- **Funding rates replace overnight gap risk**: Elevated positive funding on a long trade is a headwind equivalent to paying to hold overnight. Factor funding rate into scenario plans the same way equities traders factor earnings and gap risk.
- **Macro events hit crypto directly**: CPI, FOMC, and NFP move BTC/ETH immediately. Pre-build the same bull/bear/neutral scenario game plans. BTC is the high-beta basket; alts follow BTC dominance shifts.
- **Liquidation cascades substitute for equity gap-downs**: A liquidation cascade (visible in open interest and funding data) replaces the overnight gap-down event. Plan for flush-and-retest entries the same way equities traders plan post-gap entries.
- **Volume thresholds differ by venue**: On Hyperliquid perps, use funding rate direction + open interest change + price velocity as the RVOL analog. Negative funding during a flush = potential long setup; positive funding at highs = crowded long to fade.
- **Weekend liquidity risk**: Low liquidity on weekends is the crypto analog of a pre-holiday equities day. Reduce size and skip low-conviction setups on Sunday UTC.

## Common mistakes

- Entering in No Man's Land (middle of the range) where there is no edge; always wait for price discovery at a true level.
- Building a game plan that predicts direction instead of defining levels and reactions for each direction.
- Chasing a setup after price has run away from the identified entry; move on rather than adjusting the level up/down.
- Sizing the same for exploratory setups and playbook A+ setups — this destroys the expectancy math.
- Treating a chop day as a trend day and holding through pullbacks that do not recover.
- Not accounting for the volatility regime (VIX / funding rate environment) when sizing positions.
- Ignoring RVOL/volume confirmation and trading a catalyst on price alone.
