# Breakdown Retest Short
**Category:** second_chance_retest  **Timeframe:** scalp/intraday/swing  **Confidence:** high  **Venues:** HL perps (BTC, ETH, SOL, major alts), Binance futures (USDⓈ-M)

## Preconditions
- A key support level has broken on elevated volume (≥1.5× RVOL on the breakdown bar). The break was clean and fast — not choppy.
- After the breakdown, price bounced back toward the broken support (now resistance). The bounce should show declining volume and weak price action (small candles, lower highs on 5-min/15-min).
- Market context is bearish or neutral: BTC (if trading alts) or SPY analog is not in a strong trending-up session. Broad market internals confirm seller control.
- Broader downtrend already established (lower highs, lower lows on higher timeframe) adds conviction.
- For multi-day swing setups: price must have closed below the support level — not just wicked through intraday.

## Entry trigger
- **Primary:** Price rallies back to the broken support level (now resistance). Tape shows: offers refreshing at resistance, bids failing to lift through, bids being hit rather than lifted. Short on the first sign of rejection — price stalls and begins to fade from resistance zone.
- **Candle-confirmed:** Bearish reversal candle (shooting star, bearish engulfing, lower high on 5-min or 15-min) at the resistance zone. Volume contracts on the rally leg, expands on the rejection candle.
- **Tape confirmation:** Large persistent offer wall at the breakdown level; price tries multiple times but cannot lift through — short when bids thin at the level.
- Stop: 2 cents / 0.1–0.2% above the resistance zone (scalp). For swing: above the bounce high. Never above the prior swing high.
- Conservative entry: wait for price to print a lower high vs. the prior bounce high at the retest zone — confirms the second attempt fails.

## Invalidation
- Price closes back above the broken support level on volume — sellers failed; breakdown was a fake. Cover and reassess.
- Tape shows aggressive bid absorption through the resistance (offers being cleanly lifted on large volume) — potential squeeze; cover.
- Broad market flips to strong trend-up session (BTC/SPY analog breaks above its own resistance) — reduces short conviction for individual names.

## Targets
- **T1:** Prior intraday low or 1× ATR below entry (cover 25–33%).
- **T2:** Next major support level below (prior multi-session low, weekly support, measured move). Cover 33%.
- **Trailing stop:** Trail with 5-min or 15-min lower highs for remaining position; exit on first strong bullish reversal candle.
- For swing breakdown continuation: target prior swing low or 50% retracement of the prior up-move. Use prior bar high on daily as trailing stop.

## Sizing notes
- Standard allocation (100%) if all conditions met: clean breakdown bar, retest on declining volume, tape confirmation at resistance.
- Reduce to 50% if broader market is ambiguous (BTC flat, not down).
- Reduce to 25% or skip if the original breakdown candle was low-volume (thin — more likely a fake-out).
- Scale in: enter 50% at initial retest failure; add remaining 50% when price breaks below the prior intraday low (confirmed continuation).
- For day 2 earnings fade shorts: require institutional ownership >50% (forced multi-day selling), short float <10% (no squeeze risk).

## Crypto adaptation notes
- **Hyperliquid perps:** Primary venue for this setup in both scalp and swing forms. Use 5-min chart for scalp entries; 1-hour/4-hour for swing setups.
- **Binance USDⓈ-M futures:** Use for larger swing positions where Hyperliquid liquidity is insufficient (e.g., smaller alt breakdowns).
- **Funding rate filter:** Positive funding during the bounce into resistance = crowded longs supporting the bounce. This actually increases short conviction — trapped longs will fuel the breakdown when the level fails. Very negative funding during the retest = short covering, reduces conviction.
- **CME gap fills (BTC):** When BTC gaps up from a prior CME gap and rallies into the gap-fill level, then fails — this is the CME gap-fill failure short (a subset of breakdown retest short).
- **Applicable assets:** BTC, ETH, SOL, and any major alt (top 50 by market cap) with liquid perp funding. Less applicable to micro-cap tokens where levels are easily manipulated.
- **News context:** Post-negative catalyst (protocol exploit, regulatory action, exchange hack), the dead cat bounce into prior support-turned-resistance is the highest-conviction entry. "When a stock gets crushed, wait for the bounce to fail before shorting — don't short into the flush."
- **Multi-day continuation:** After a large catalyst sell day (close in bottom 25% of range, >5× ADV volume), day 2 bounce to the prior intraday resistance or 2-day VWAP that fails = highest-probability continuation short setup in crypto.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Shorting into the initial flush** before the bounce develops — low R/R, risk of temporary counter-move. "Wait for the bounce to fail before shorting."
- **Not waiting for tape confirmation** at the retest level — entering on price alone without seeing bids thin out leads to premature entries that get squeezed.
- **Too wide a stop** placed above a random level instead of directly above the bounce high / resistance zone.
- **Missing the second retest:** If first retest fails and price falls but then bounces again to resistance — the second test is often the highest-probability entry, not the first.
- **Confusing breakdown retest with failed-breakout reversal:** If the original breakdown was itself questionable (low volume, immediate snap-back), this setup morphs into a failed-breakout-retest-reversal (see separate playbook). Distinguish by checking volume on the break bar.
- **Shorting into strong market-wide uptrend:** This setup underperforms when BTC is in a hard trend-up day. Market context filter is mandatory.

## Example(s)
**FDX intraday breakdown retest (SMB Trade of the Week):** FDX breaks below 83 on strong seller flow (tape showing bids pulled). Bounces back to 83 area. Tape: "when a stock breaks a technical level, makes a nice down move, and then returns to this level, we almost always want to re-short." Short at 83 retest. Target: 82.65, then 81.80. Stop: above 83.20.

**TWTR held offer short (SMB):** Large seller sits at $21 — "the seller never traded 21.01." Stock consolidates below. Break of 20.80 consolidation support → momentum short entry. "As long as that seller is there, the bias is short." VWAP retest failure from below = second entry.

**BTC post-exploit bounce:** Protocol exploit causes 30% flush. Next session bounces 15% back toward the prior day's consolidation zone. Funding turns less negative (covering). Tape shows sell walls refreshing at prior breakdown level. Short the rejection. Stop: above prior consolidation zone. Target: retest of flush lows.

**One-of-the-Easiest-Ways intraday pattern:** Overextended daily stock. Morning: first leg lower establishes range. Consolidates 75%+ of session in tight range. Second leg lower: break of range low with volume → enter short on first failed pop back to range low. "Exit on the first green candle — do not get greedy after a second leg breakdown."
