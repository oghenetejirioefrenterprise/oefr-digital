### BiggerPockets thread body (third-person research-aggregator voice, persona-fiction-gate compliant)

```
Approximately 5 million US homeowners with 2021-2023 vintage adjustable-rate mortgages are hitting the end of their initial fixed period across 2026-2028. Boston Market Pulse documented this as the "ARM-reset crisis 2025-2027" — 40-60% payment jump, $2,500-3,800 monthly increase on conforming loans, jumbo borrowers worse.

The Point survey (2024) found 70% of ARM holders regret the loan. Most took the ARM in 2021-2023 under the assumption of refinancing before reset — but rates went UP not DOWN between origination and reset, so the refinance-out-before-reset thesis materially failed.

Reviewed seven mortgage-publisher walkthroughs (Bankrate, Charles Schwab, Mortgage Reports, Financial Samurai, Credit.org, Mothebroker.com 2026 guide, MidFlorida 2026 guide). Across all seven, the WHAT-an-ARM-reset-is content is solid. The HOMEOWNER-SIDE INDEPENDENT DECISION-ORGANIZATION layer is consistently absent — every publisher uses ARM-reset content as content-marketing lead-magnet for refi services.

Lender refi-funnels are worse: Bank of America, Chase, U.S. Bank, Freedom Mortgage, Schwab, and PNC all bundle reset-prep into their own refi-application as a customer-acquisition tool. None ships a homeowner-side worksheet to shop competitors against them — that would defeat the purpose of the funnel.

The four places homeowners get blindsided at reset:

1. Rate-cap-vs-margin-vs-index confusion. The "5/1" naming convention is shorthand for the initial fixed period and reset frequency, but the actual reset rate is index + margin, capped by initial-cap, periodic-cap, and lifetime-cap. Many homeowners assume the cap protects more than it does. The reset rate calculation requires all three cap parameters from the original note plus the current index value (1-yr CMT, SOFR, etc.) plus the margin from the note. Without the note in front of them, most homeowners can't reconstruct their own reset math.

2. Escrow-impound recalculation surprise. The reset moves principal+interest, but the escrow line (property tax + homeowners insurance) reanalyzes annually independent of the rate reset. If property tax assessment went up between 2021 and 2026 (Sun Belt + HCOL markets did substantially), the escrow line jumps alongside the rate line, compounding the payment shock. Lenders disclose the new total payment at reset but rarely pre-flag the escrow component separately.

3. Refi-application paperwork lead-time. Lenders quote 30-45 day close from application-to-fund. Realistic is 60-75 days from first-inquiry-to-fund because the application requires 60-day asset statements, gift-letter (if applicable), 2-yr W-2/1099, 30-day pay stubs, current homeowners insurance binder, and (for self-employed) 2-yr full tax returns.
```
