## Preconditions
**Day 1 requirements:**
- Strong directional catalyst (major news, protocol upgrade, exchange listing, ETF approval, macro event) drove a significant move (>5% intraday range on >3× ADV volume).
- Day 1 closed in the extreme 20% of its range (bottom 20% for shorts on day 2; top 20% for longs on day 2). This is the institutional conviction signal.
- A key intraday consolidation zone formed during Day 1 — this becomes the S1/R1 reference level for Day 2.
- Day 1 did NOT fill its gap (for gap plays) — gap held, confirming institutional absorption of the initial supply/demand.

**Day 2 setup:**
- Pre-market: stock/token holds above Day 1 close (long) or below Day 1 close (short). Pre-market activity near key Day 1 levels (consolidation zone).
- Day 2 RVOL ≥ 1.5 in the first 30 min (continued institutional participation).
- The 2-day anchored VWAP (from Day 1 open) is a key reference — price holding above it = long bias; failing to reclaim it = short bias.

## Entry trigger
**Day 2 Long (Second Chance Retest Buy):**
- Price pulls back to the Day 1 consolidation zone (S1) on Day 2. Volume dries up on the pullback.
- "Stuff and rubber-band" turn: price presses through S1 briefly, bids absorb, snaps back above. Enter on the reclaim.
- Standard: enter when price holds above S1 and confirms with a bullish 5-min or 15-min candle at that level. Stop: 2 cents below the rejection candle low.
- Aggressive (2-day VWAP): buy when price taps 2-day VWAP and shows tape absorption. Stop: 5-min close below 2-day VWAP on volume.

**Day 2 Strong Opening Drive Long:**
- Day 2 opens with momentum that breaks through multiple Day 1 resistance levels in the first 15–30 min.
- Wait for consolidation above the broken resistance. Enter on consolidation breakout or pullback to broken resistance-turned-support.
- "If stock shows strong opening drive (breaks through multiple Day 1 resistance levels on volume in first 15-30 min)" — this is the institutional follow-through signal.

**Day 2 Short (Fade):**
- Day 1 closed near low (bottom 20%) with high volume. Day 2 gaps up or bounces into S1 (Day 1 intraday resistance).
- Short when Day 2 bounces into S1 resistance zone and fails — tape shows offers refreshing, bids thinning. Enter on the rejection. Stop: above S1.

## Invalidation
**Long:** Day 2 price closes below Day 1 consolidation low (S1 fails) — institutional buyers not present. Exit.
**Short:** Day 2 price reclaims and holds above S1 with volume — continuation, not a fade. Cover.
**Both:** RVOL drops below 1.5 by 10:30 AM (30 min after open) — no follow-through participation; exit or tighten stop to breakeven.

## Targets
**Day 2 Long:**
- T1: Day 1 high (first target; cover 33%).
- T2: Measured move = Day 1 range added above Day 1 high (cover 33%).
- Trail: trail remainder with 5-min higher lows or 90-MA on 1-min.

**Day 2 Short:**
- T1: Day 1 intraday support (S3; cover 33–50%).
- T2: Next major support from multi-week chart (cover 33%).
- Trail: trail with 5-min lower highs; exit on first strong bullish reversal candle.

**2-day VWAP bounce:**
- Scale: take first half at 50% of expected move toward prior session high; trail remainder with 90-MA on 1-min.

## Sizing notes
- Day 2 plays carry high conviction when all preconditions met. Standard to 125% allocation.
- Reduce to 75% if Day 2 RVOL is 1.0–1.5 (participation below ideal).
- Reduce to 50% if broader market (BTC) is in a hard opposing trend.
- "Multiple entry points increase the probability of getting filled at the best price" — scale into Day 2 setups in 3 tranches: (1) initial touch of S1, (2) tape confirmation of hold, (3) break above Day 1 prior resistance.
- Day 2 short: require institutional ownership >50% equivalent (large wallet concentration) and low squeeze risk (low funding rate positive spread) before sizing up.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- **BTC/ETH/SOL on Hyperliquid perps:** Directly applicable. After any major macro event (FOMC, ETF approval, regulatory ruling), use Day 1 consolidation zone (S1 = post-open consolidation low) as the Day 2 reference.
- **Binance spot:** Use for Day 2 continuation longs in BTC/ETH where funding costs would erode a 1–2 day hold on perps.
- **2-day VWAP tool:** Available on TradingView for all crypto pairs. Anchor VWAP to Day 1 session open. This is the primary institutional reference level for Day 2 direction. "Day 2: strong Day 1 closes become long opportunities on Day 2 pullback; overextended Day 1 closes become short opportunities on Day 2 gap into resistance."
- **Funding rate on Day 2:** Cooling funding (from elevated Day 1 levels) during Day 2 pullback = natural long setup confirmation. Elevated funding on Day 2 gap-up = short Day 2 fade signal.
- **Token listing days:** First listing on exchange = Day 1. Second trading session = Day 2. Day 2 plays on newly listed tokens (hold above Day 1 VWAP = long; fail Day 1 VWAP = fade) are highly applicable on Hyperliquid new listings.
- **Protocol upgrade / catalyst events:** Same structure. Day 1 = upgrade goes live with large volume. Day 2 = institutional follow-through if price holds above Day 1 close; fade if it fails.
- **Day 3 caution:** Third-day plays carry lower probability. "Third day catalyst gap up that's quite often sold for a reset." On Day 3, shift to fade bias if the first two days were strong.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Entering late on Day 1** instead of waiting for Day 2. "Don't be the 1000th trader in after the stock has moved 10+ points" — wait for Day 2 pullback to S1.
- **Not identifying S1/R1 pre-market** — "without prep, you cannot identify S1/R1 fast enough at the open." Pre-market preparation (the night before) is mandatory.
- **Entering on the Day 2 open without pullback** — Day 2 opens strong but chasing is dangerous. Wait for the S1 retest or the strong opening drive confirmation.
- **Holding past the Day 2 RVOL dry-up** — if RVOL drops below 1.5 by 10:30, the institutional participation is absent; exit or move to breakeven stop.
- **Day 2 short without checking short float (funding)** — high positive funding = potential Day 2 squeeze. Avoid Day 2 shorts when funding is very positive.
- **Confusing Day 2 short with Day 2 continuation long** — Day 1 closed near HIGH = Day 2 continuation long. Day 1 closed near LOW = Day 2 short/fade. These are opposite setups triggered by Day 1 close location.
