## Preconditions
- Asset has completed a large parabolic move (typically 50–400%+) on a catalyst, meme, or squeeze event; the "day 1" or "day 2" of the parabolic run has clearly established a high
- Price is now on the "backside" of the parabola: overhead supply from the initial run (trapped longs who bought into the hype, now sellers on any bounce) creates resistance
- VWAP is now above price — each bounce attempt back toward VWAP is a short opportunity (VWAP = resistance, not support, on the backside)
- Key supply zone identified from prior trading: the breakdown area, the prior consolidation base, or the "underbelly" of the parabolic move where the supply is concentrated
- For the "overextension" variant: 4+ ATRs over 5 trading days, outside 2nd standard deviation Bollinger Band, below 200-period MA — statistical extreme that precedes mean reversion
- For the "bear flag" / "descending wedge" variant (BTC): price in confirmed downtrend with consecutive lower highs; consolidates in a bear flag structure below a declining 20-period MA
- Regime: a genuine supply zone short requires a prior parabolic move; do not apply in a normal trending market without the overextension component

## Entry trigger
- **Primary trigger — lower high confirmation:** wait for the bounce into the supply zone to form a lower high on the 5-minute chart versus the prior bounce high; short the break of the lower high with stop above the high of day
- **Secondary trigger — VWAP rejection:** price approaches VWAP from below, tape shows sellers refreshing, bids thin; short on failed VWAP reclaim; stop above VWAP with 5-minute close above as invalidation
- **Tertiary trigger — "look-above-and-fail" with base:** price pokes above a key resistance in the supply zone, consolidates for 15–25 minutes below it (the base confirms resistance), then breaks the base support; short aggressively on the support break
- **Bear flag breakdown (BTC/ETH variant):** break below bear flag consolidation support with volume; stop above consolidation high; trail on 5-minute lower highs

## Invalidation
- Price reclaims VWAP on the 5-minute chart with volume and holds for 2+ candles — shorts are wrong; supply zone broken
- Asset makes a new high above the parabolic high — the move was not a top, it is continuing; cover and stand aside
- News catalyst reversal: positive announcement causes a genuine fundamental re-rating; stop at high of day
- For the crowded short: when the short is universally known and the setup is exactly right, the squeeze before the fail can be violent — if price spikes 5%+ above the entry with no tape reversal, honor the stop

## Targets
- T1: 50% retracement of the parabolic move — first meaningful supply absorption (cover 25%)
- T2: Base of the parabolic move / origin of the spike — where the meme/catalyst buying began (cover 25%)
- T3: Prior day reference level or round number major support (cover 25%)
- T4: Pre-catalyst price — the "undisturbed" equilibrium before the meme/catalyst event drove the move (trail final 25%)
- Bear flag measured move: distance from the bear flag pole, projected below the consolidation

## Sizing notes
- This is a patience trade — backside recognition requires waiting for the lower high confirmation; early entries that try to catch the exact top are the most dangerous
- Two-tranche approach: small initial at the lower high or VWAP rejection; larger second at the "look-above-and-fail" base break when the pattern is fully confirmed
- Reduce size by 30–50% when the setup is widely discussed (Crypto Twitter, major Telegram channels all calling the same top) — short squeezes on crowded fades are violent
- For overextension shorts (4 ATR variant): position is a full unit given statistical confirmation; trail exit at moving average crossover rather than a fixed target
- Cover partial at each target; never hold the entire position for the "origin of move" target — most backside fades only reach T1 or T2

## Crypto adaptation notes
The backside short is one of the highest-yield setups in crypto specifically because of the leverage structure of perpetual futures:

1. **Funding rate as exhaustion signal.** When a parabolic move drives funding rates to extreme positive levels (longs paying 0.1%+ per 8 hours), the setup becomes structural: every 8 hours, a transfer of wealth from longs to shorts acts as a slow bleed that accelerates the backside move. A funding rate flip (from positive to neutral or negative) during a VWAP bounce attempt is a powerful short confirmation.

2. **Open interest divergence.** On the backside, if price makes a small bounce but open interest increases (new longs entering), those new longs are the fuel for the next leg down — they will be liquidated in the next flush. Track OI on each bounce.

3. **Liquidation cluster mapping.** On Hyperliquid and similar platforms, visible liquidation levels above and below current price show where forced buying/selling will occur. A backside bounce into a dense long-liquidation cluster above (already triggered, now supply) confirms resistance.

4. **No spot buying support.** In low-liquidity altcoin perps, the backside move is amplified because there is no spot market bid absorbing the selling. Institutional spot buyers who provided support in the initial move are no longer active — only retail momentum and forced sellers remain.

5. **Bear flag is the most reliable structure.** On BTC and ETH intraday shorts, the bear flag consolidation is the highest-probability entry — clean measured move target, defined stop above the consolidation, and consistent with the broader downtrend structure. Use 5-minute chart for entry, 15-minute for regime confirmation.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Shorting the absolute high ("catching the knife" from the short side) — the highest point is unknowable in real time; wait for the first lower high, then short the break of the subsequent lower high
- Not accounting for backside timing: the backside can take hours or days to develop; do not hold a backside short through an overnight funding reset without a defined plan
- Confusing a "bull flag" consolidation with backside distribution — if price consolidates above VWAP with higher lows, it is a bull flag, not a backside; verify which side of VWAP the consolidation is on
- Shorting into forced buying: if the token has a known redemption or settlement event that requires buying, the supply zone short will fail; check the mechanics
- Trading the "crowded short" at full size — when the setup is universally discussed, price squeezes the consensus first; the right trade is to wait for the squeeze to exhaust before entering (same failed-breakout-above-the-key-level trigger applies here)
- Holding through a fundamental catalyst reversal (positive news, protocol upgrade, institutional announcement) — a real re-rating invalidates the supply zone thesis; stop is non-negotiable
