## Preconditions

- Asset has made a clean breakout above a clearly defined resistance level (prior high, range high, consolidation ceiling, whole number)
- The break was confirmed: close above the level with volume expansion (RVOL ≥ 1.5×)
- Price has now pulled back toward the broken level, which should act as new support
- Pullback volume is meaningfully lower than breakout volume (prior sellers are no longer present)
- Market/BTC environment is not in reversal — broader trend must be intact
- The level was significant: tested 2+ times before breaking, or held for multiple weeks — the more tests, the more reliable the reversal to support
- For HL perps: funding rate not deeply negative (would indicate short bias dominating)

## Entry trigger

**Touch-and-Go (primary entry):**
1. Price pulls back to within 0.2–0.5% of the breakout level
2. Tap or slight wick below the level; buyers immediately step in (re-bid or absorption visible on HL DOM)
3. First green candle that closes above the breakout level — enter on the close or on confirmation tick
4. Stop: just below the breakout level (or below the wick low if a brief penetration occurred)

**Leak-and-Rebid (higher conviction variant):**
1. Price dips slightly below the breakout level (0.3–0.8%), immediately re-bids
2. Second test of the level is shallower than the first (higher low) — confirms buyers are defending
3. Enter on the re-bid reclaim; stop at the leak low

**Multi-tier entry (for swing holds):**
- Tier 1 (10–15% position): flush below breakout level on tape showing bids absorbing
- Tier 2 (add to 40–60% position): on reclaim of the breakout level with volume
- Tier 3 (full position): on VWAP reclaim above the level

## Invalidation

- Price closes a 15m or daily bar below the prior breakout level with volume
- Breakout level is tested 3+ times without bouncing — exhaustion signal, step aside
- Tape shows persistent offers at the level, not just a brief flush (distribution, not absorption)
- BTC rolls over on a 4h timeframe basis simultaneously

## Targets

- T1: First resistance above (next prior high, round number, or 1 ATR from entry) — take 30–50% here
- T2: Measured move (consolidation height or breakout impulse leg) added to breakout level
- T3: New high extension — hold runner with trailing stop at each new prior bar low
- For swing holds: trail using daily higher lows; exit on daily close below the most recent swing low

## Sizing notes

- Standard full size for first touch of the breakout level after confirmed break
- 50% size only if: RVOL below average on pullback day, or BTC environment uncertain
- 1.5× size (max) only when all criteria met: multi-tested level + clean breakout volume + RVOL elevated on pullback hold + anchored VWAP at same level (double confluence)
- Round-number levels (e.g., BTC $100k, ETH $4k) justify extra size due to psychological significance and institutional awareness

## Crypto adaptation notes

- Prior resistance levels on crypto charts function identically to equities — the most frequently tested levels with the heaviest volume create the strongest support on re-test
- For BTC: $100k, $90k, $80k, $70k, $50k are canonical whole-number levels with very high probability of acting as support on first pullback after breakout
- In crypto, the pullback to the prior breakout level often happens within 12–48 hours of the initial break — the setup is compressed vs equities
- Use anchored VWAP from the breakout candle as confirmation: if anchored VWAP aligns with the breakout level during the pullback, double conviction
- Avoid if open interest spiked >30% on the breakout (potential overleverage = violent pullback through the level)
- Token-specific: very thin order book on small-cap alts means the "level" is a zone ±2–3% not a precise price; scale entry across the zone

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- Buying the breakout itself (chasing) rather than waiting for the pullback to the level — the level is the entry, not above it
- Entering on the 2nd or 3rd visit to the same level without checking volume — each revisit reduces probability; after 3 touches of the same level as "new support," the buyers are getting exhausted
- Ignoring the tape: price at a level is not enough — wait for re-bid, absorption, or leak-and-rebid confirmation
- Setting stops too tight (inside the level) — allow for a wick to the level; stop below the wick low, not at the level
- Not scaling out at T1 — greed causes giving back profits when the level acts as ceiling on the first visit

- 48 hours later: BTC pulls back to $69,800 (the breakout level) on below-average volume
- HL DOM shows large bids refreshing at $69,750–$70,000; slight wick to $69,600 then immediate re-bid
- Enter long at $70,100 on re-bid confirmation; stop at $69,400 (below wick); T1: $74,000; T2: measured move to $78,000
