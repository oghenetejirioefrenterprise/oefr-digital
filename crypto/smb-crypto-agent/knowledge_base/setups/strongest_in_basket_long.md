## Preconditions

1. **Identify the basket / sector first.** In crypto: L1s (SOL, AVAX, SUI, APT), L2s (ARB, OP, STRK), memecoins, AI tokens (VIRTUAL, TAO, GRASS), DePIN (HNT, RNDR, IO), or a BTC-miner cluster (MSTR, COIN analogs = exchange tokens like OKB, BNB).
2. **Benchmark is rising.** BTC or ETH trending up on the timeframe of interest (higher highs/higher lows on 5-min for intraday; daily for swing). Index must be in your favor before picking leaders.
3. **The target token is outperforming the benchmark.** BTC is +2% on the day; the token is +5%+. This is the long-tail alt basket equivalent of "relative strength vs. the tape."
4. **Structural confirmation (one of):**
   - Token holds above anchored VWAP from catalyst day while BTC dips — higher low forms above VWAP.
   - Multi-day consolidation with higher lows; clear resistance overhead.
   - Price is above its 20 EMA (or 21 EMA on 5-min) and EMA is rising.
   - RSI on 1H or 4H shows higher lows (bullish momentum divergence or non-distribution confirmation).
5. **Volume profile:** Volume contracting on pullbacks (healthy), expanding on pushes into resistance (accumulation).
6. **Funding rate:** Neutral to slightly negative on the setup token = not overcrowded long. Avoid entries when funding is highly positive (already squeezed).
7. **Pre-market or pre-session read:** Token holds gains overnight while BTC is flat or slightly red — persistent relative strength across sessions.

## Entry trigger

**Intraday (scalp/intraday):**
- Buy the first touch/bounce off anchored VWAP (anchored to catalyst or session open) when the token has shown relative strength. Confirm with: (a) tape absorbing offers without printing below VWAP, (b) a green 5-min candle closing above VWAP, (c) volume picking up on the reclaim.
- OR: Open range breakout — token clears the first-30-min range high while BTC is also constructive. Enter on the break with stop below the range midpoint.
- Flush-and-rip variant: token briefly undercuts VWAP on high volume, buyers step in hard, reversal candle forms — enter the rip. Stop below the flush low.

**Swing:**
- Higher low above 2-day anchored VWAP forms on daily chart. Enter on the first bounce off the higher low with volume expansion. Confirm BTC is in uptrend.
- Multi-day consolidation breakout: token clears resistance on volume after forming higher lows over 3+ days. Enter on the daily close above resistance or on the break if intraday volume is clearly above average. Trail with 20-day EMA.
- Pre-market relative strength divergence: token is green pre-market while BTC is flat — enter near open VWAP with stop below pre-market low.

**Sector-head-of-snake timing:** Within a rising sector, enter the strongest token first. Laggards in the same sector often follow 30–90 minutes later; the head of snake gives the cleanest entry with lowest risk.

## Invalidation

- **Intraday:** Token closes a 5-min candle below anchored VWAP with volume after entry; or BTC rolls over and makes a new intraday low while token cannot hold above VWAP.
- **Swing:** Daily close below the 2-day anchored VWAP; or close below the higher low that formed the entry pattern; or 20-day EMA turns down with price below it.
- **General:** Token begins to underperform BTC (loses relative strength). If BTC is +1% and the token is flat or red after entry — exit or reduce. The edge is the relative strength; when it evaporates, the thesis is gone.
- Funding rate spikes to strongly positive after entry = crowding risk, reduce size.

## Targets

**Intraday:**
- T1: Prior intraday swing high or session VWAP ± 1 ATR. Take partial (30–50%) here.
- T2: Round number or key daily level; trail with 5-min lower highs as stop.
- T3: Only hold a runner with explicit reason (trend day confirmation, ADD/market breadth still expanding).

**Swing:**
- T1: Prior swing high. Take partial (30–40%) at T1.
- T2: Next round number or 10% above breakout. Trail remainder with daily 20 EMA.
- T3: Trail with weekly levels; only hold if macro/BTC trend intact.

**Exit acceleration signal:** When the token goes parabolic (3–5 ATR single candle, volume climax) — this is exhaustion, not continuation. Reduce aggressively.

## Sizing notes

- Start at 25–33% of planned position on first signal (VWAP bounce or opening range break). Add to full size on confirmation (second bounce, breakout hold, volume expansion).
- For swing: begin with 50% at breakout, add 50% on first pullback that holds above the breakout level.
- Max position in any single alt: 2–3% of account. Basket plays spread risk across 3–5 tokens.
- In high-volatility regimes (VIX analog = funding rates spiking, market-wide liquidations in the last 24h): halve size. Relative strength edge degrades in cascading liquidation environments.
- When the whole basket is moving, pyramiding into the leader is appropriate — only add when trade is profitable and invalidation level has moved up.

## Crypto adaptation notes

- **Sector = L1/L2/memecoin/AI-token/DePIN cluster.** The strongest within the rising cluster is the trade; avoid picking randomly.
- **Benchmark = BTC or ETH.** Any token you go long should be up more than BTC on the same timeframe; if it's not, wait for another setup.
- **Anchored VWAP** works the same way on crypto perpetuals on Hyperliquid. Anchor to: (a) catalyst day (listing, major upgrade, ecosystem announcement), (b) session open (00:00 UTC), or (c) significant swing low.
- **Sector-head-of-snake in crypto:** Identify which token led the move in the last 1–4 hours. New money often rotates to laggards (second-mover effect) but the leader is still the cleanest trade. On Hyperliquid, filter by 24h volume and 1h % gain within the sector.
- **Funding rate as relative strength proxy:** When a token sustains positive funding but price is still holding or making new highs, longs are paying but bulls are in control — confirms relative strength.
- **BTC-miner analog = exchange tokens (BNB, OKB) or mining-adjacent tokens (RNDR, HNT for compute).** BTC correlation lag arbitrage: BTC moves, compute/exchange tokens follow 15–30 minutes later on high-conviction BTC moves.
- **ETH as sector leader:** When ETH outperforms BTC in a risk-on session, L2s (ARB, OP) and DeFi blue-chips typically follow. ETH relative strength vs. BTC is the signal to start L2 basket longs.
- **Intraday RSI divergence (bullish):** Token makes a lower low but RSI makes a higher low on the 1H chart — this is the strongest pullback buy entry for swing setups. Enter on the next confirmed green 1H candle.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- **Chasing without relative strength confirmation.** Buying a token just because the sector is hot, without verifying the specific token is outperforming BTC — results in buying the laggard that doesn't follow.
- **Entering before index confirms.** Going long a crypto leader when BTC hasn't yet shown a higher low or cleared resistance — leads to getting caught in the fade when BTC continues lower.
- **Ignoring the anchor timeframe.** Using a 5-min VWAP signal but ignoring that the daily trend is down — fighting the macro regime.
- **Not trimming at T1.** Letting a full position ride through T1 and having it reverse; partial profits lock in the trade's viability.
- **Holding through funding spikes.** When funding goes from neutral to strongly positive after entry, it means the crowd is now long — distribution begins. This is a trim signal, not a hold signal.
- **Entering the laggard instead of the leader.** Within a rising sector, traders often enter the "cheap" (laggard) token thinking it has more upside. The leader has the strongest tape and is the correct entry; the laggard is the pair-trade short.
- **No plan for sector rotation.** Sectors rotate fast in crypto. If AI tokens are the strength theme but DePIN starts outperforming after 2h, the edge shifts. Monitor inter-sector performance and be willing to exit and rotate.
