## Preconditions
**Multi-timeframe overextension to the downside — all must align:**
1. Daily/hourly: price has extended 2-3 ATRs lower in a straight line without meaningful consolidation (no inside bars, no flat periods — those indicate price acceptance = disqualifier)
2. Intraday: price extended >0.5-1.0 ATR below intraday VWAP on the session (also extended on the lower timeframe)
3. Approaching a higher-timeframe support level with documented prior bounce history (prior ATH, multi-month support, weekly MA, round number like $50k, $100k BTC)
4. Forced selling dynamics visible: straight-line move indicates margin calls or liquidation cascades (no consolidation = no voluntary sellers)
5. Volume crescendo: the biggest volume bar since the open appears near the extension extreme (this is the blow-off signal — underwater longs capitulating)
6. For multi-day setup: 2-3+ consecutive sessions with large declines (>3-5%); each day larger than prior; Day 3+ gap-down into the support area

**Capitulation checklist (from systematic study):**
- Multiple down days in a row (3-5+) with each day's range larger than prior
- Volume increasing day over day (2x prior day volume = key confirmatory signal)
- Stock/asset was showing relative strength vs market earlier (not a weak name being crushed; structural strength = real buyers waiting)
- High-float, liquid name (BTC/ETH in crypto = the liquid equivalent)
- No inside bars or flat consolidation periods during the flush (price acceptance = disqualifier; wait for a cleaner setup)

## Entry trigger
**V-bottom long (primary — highest probability):**
Wait for the intraday volume crescendo (biggest volume bar since open) at the extension extreme near the higher-timeframe support. Observe: (1) sellers fail to hold price below support or fail to make new lows within 2-3 candles, (2) tape changes — bids absorbing offers, prints stop going lower, price fails to make new low on a 3rd test at the same level. Enter long on this change of character; stop below the capitulation wick low.

**Gap-down open higher low entry:**
Asset gaps down on Day 3+ open (panic conditions); forms a higher low vs the gap-down pre-market low within first 15-30 minutes; tape shows bids stepping in, selling decelerating. Buy when gap-down open forms a higher low vs the pre-market low; stop below that higher low.

**Flush-and-reclaim at round number:**
Price flushes 0.5-1% below a whole-number / key support level on capitulation volume; immediately reclaims the round-number level with bids holding at the level. Enter long on the reclaim; stop $0.50-1% below the flush low (or 0.5% below in crypto scaling).

**Multi-leg probability scaling (for experienced traders):**
- Leg 1 attempt: 30% probability (small probe); Leg 2: 40%; Leg 3: 60%; Leg 4 (final): 80%
- Each leg: entry is 1 unit of risk; scale up as each successive leg confirms (each subsequent leg should be larger and faster; if legs slow down, exit the thesis)

## Invalidation
- Sellers succeed in making new lows AND holding below the higher-timeframe support on volume — do not add; exit
- Inside bar forms (price acceptance = sellers still in control at higher price)
- Volume does not crescendo at the extension — without the blow-off volume, the floor may not be in
- Price makes new lows after the higher-low setup fails to hold within 2 candles
- Fresh negative fundamental news drives the flush (not a forced-seller flush = a fundamental flush; different trade)

## Targets
- T1: VWAP of current session (cover 50% of position; this is where the overhead supply concentrates)
- T2: Opening range high (cover 25%; if buyers hold above VWAP, next level)
- T3: Prior day close or opening range (hold 25% for potential multi-hour reversal if flush was genuine capitulation)
- For multi-day capitulation: T3 = 38.2% retracement of the entire down leg; T4 = 50% retracement

## Sizing notes
- This is a counter-trend trade — start smaller than trend-following trades
- Enter 25-33% at the first tape change signal; add to 50% on first higher low above the capitulation wick; full size only if price reclaims VWAP and holds
- Never add before tape confirmation at the low — the first flush is rarely the exact bottom; wait for the V
- Hold larger positions only if close is in the top quartile of the day's range with strong tape

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Liquidation cascades create the ideal capitulation setup: when open interest (OI) drops sharply on a volume spike at a known support level, the cascade has forced out the weak longs. Smart money waits for this.
- Funding rate deeply negative during the flush: if perpetual funding is very negative (forced short payment to longs), the squeeze fuel is loaded. Capitulation long + deeply negative funding = higher-than-normal probability bounce.
- Round numbers ($50k, $60k, $100k BTC; equivalent round levels on alts) create self-fulfilling support — institutional and retail both recognize them
- Hyperliquid-specific: watch for the order book to "refill" the bid side after a cascade — when large bids appear again below prior support level, the cascade is exhausted
- VIX analog in crypto: when DVOL spikes to multi-month highs on the flush day, fading the spike by going long the asset is historically high-probability
- After the bounce, watch funding — if funding flips positive quickly, consider trimming; if funding stays negative, the bounce has more room

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Catching the falling knife (entering before volume crescendo / before V confirmation) — first flush is not the entry; wait for the V
- Lazy scaling: adding before tape change confirmation; this turns a smart fade into "averaging down into loss"
- Holding the bounce through a consolidation that forms below VWAP — consolidation = price acceptance; if it consolidates below VWAP, exit the trade
- Ignoring the capitulation checklist items (inside bars, volume not expanding, prior price was already weak — these disqualify the setup)
- Converting what should be an intraday bounce trade into an overnight hold without EOD confirmation (top quartile close + strong tape)

## Example(s)
**Tesla multi-day capitulation flush (systematic checklist):** 5 consecutive days down, each day larger; volume 2x prior day on Day 5; no inside bars during flush; strong stock YTD; gap down on Day 6; higher low forms above pre-market low by 10:15am; bids step in; long on the higher low; T1 = VWAP, T2 = prior day close, T3 = 38.2% retrace.

**BTC $50k round-number flush (generalized):** BTC flushes $1,500 below $50k in a cascade; order book refills at $49,500; price snaps back above $50k within 3 minutes; enter long on $50k reclaim; stop $49,200 (below flush low); T1 = daily VWAP; T2 = prior session high.

**Gold bounce after extended move (flush-and-reclaim):** Gold flushes 60-70 cents below a whole-number support on capitulation volume; tape shows bids at the level; reclaims whole number; long on reclaim; T1 = $1.50 move to prior S/R, T2 = $3 move to prior range high.
