## Preconditions
- Asset has undergone a multi-ATR straight-line sell-off (2–3+ ATR on daily, or 3–5 legs lower intraday without consolidation).
- No significant consolidation during the sell-off — "sellers sold in straight line, no inside bars" — confirming forced / liquidation-driven selling (not methodical distribution).
- Price is approaching a **prior explosive breakout level** or **major structural support** (multi-session prior support with multiple prior touches, round psychological level, weekly/monthly key support).
- **Volume crescendo signal:** The largest volume bar since the selling began prints near the support zone — confirms capitulation (underwater longs finally giving up).
- **Dual overextension (for intraday):** Extended on both the higher timeframe (2–3 ATR from daily/4h VWAP) AND intraday (>0.5 ATR below intraday VWAP) simultaneously — both must align.
- For the capitulation checklist to qualify: (1) 3–5+ consecutive down days, each larger than prior; (2) volume increasing day over day; (3) no inside bars during the sell-off (no price acceptance); (4) stock/token was relatively strong vs market earlier.

## Entry trigger
- **Five-bar consolidation above support (highest conviction):** After the capitulation flush to the support zone, price forms a 5-bar sideways consolidation above the support level. Buy the break of the consolidation upside. Stop: below consolidation low.
- **Tape-driven entry:** Tape shows large bids appearing at the support level, absorbing sell flow. "When bids overwhelming offers — large buying volume spike — price fails to stay below prior low and immediately rebounds." Enter on the re-bid confirmation. Stop: below the flush low.
- **VWAP reclaim trigger:** Price flushes through support, immediately re-bids back above intraday VWAP within the same 15-min candle. Enter on VWAP reclaim. Stop: below flush low.
- **Higher low formation (V-reversal):** After capitulation low, price forms 2+ consecutive higher lows (intraday). Enter on break above the most recent lower high. Stop: below the most recent higher low.
- **Falling knife protocol (four-entry scale):** 
  - Entry 1 (most aggressive): flush below key support into pre-defined zone; tape shows buyers; enter with 15-cent / 0.15% stop.
  - Entry 2: price recaptures prior support level and holds.
  - Entry 3: next session forms higher low vs Entry 1 level.
  - Entry 4 (most conservative): break above prior session high.

## Invalidation
- Sellers succeed: price continues below support with no bounce attempt, or volume remains elevated with no tape reversal signal (sellers reloading rather than exhausting).
- Inside bar during the sell-off (price acceptance) — disqualifier. Signals this is deliberate distribution, not forced liquidation.
- No volume crescendo: if volume on the sell-off is not the largest in the sequence, capitulation has not occurred — pass.
- New lower low after the initial bounce (for 5-bar consolidation variant): consolidation breaks to the downside with volume.

## Targets
- **T1:** Intraday VWAP (primary; cover 50%).
- **T2:** Opening range low (if started as gap-down setup); then opening range itself.
- **T3 (swing):** Prior breakdown area / undisturbed price before the catalyst event.
- For multi-day capitulation bounce: first target = prior consolidation zone; second target = 38.2% retracement of full decline; full target = 50% retracement.
- For 5-bar consolidation break: target = 1.5× the consolidation width above the breakout.
- Scale: take 50% at T1 (VWAP), hold 50% for potential full reversal; exit entirely on first strong red candle or VWAP failure.

## Sizing notes
- Initial nibble: 25–33% of planned position at the support level before tape confirmation.
- Add on tape confirmation (bids absorbing, offers lifting): bring to 75%.
- Full size: on VWAP reclaim or 5-bar consolidation break: bring to 100%.
- "Never scale in lazily — do not add until right; structural edge as active trader is nimbleness."
- Maximum stop = flush low. This is a non-negotiable hard stop regardless of conviction.
- Reduce size in low-RVOL environments (holiday, thin weekend session in crypto) — "exaggerated moves in both directions; trade intraday ranges not trend positions."
- For The Tempting Tesla capitulation checklist: only trade the full checklist — partial setups (missing any criterion) get reduced to 50% or skipped.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- **Liquidation cascades on Hyperliquid:** Crypto perp liquidation events create the exact capitulation pattern: forced liquidations produce a straight-line flush to key support with maximum volume. The flush itself is the setup trigger — wait for tape absorption at the support zone.
- **Funding rate as capitulation signal:** When funding spikes very negative during the flush (shorts massively crowded), it confirms capitulation (existing longs were liquidated). Recovery as shorts cover provides additional fuel for the bounce.
- **BTC/ETH/SOL as primary assets:** These are the "high-float, liquid, institutionally owned" equivalents. Capitulation bounces on micro-cap tokens are too noisy and prone to continuation lower — skip or reduce size dramatically.
- **Hyperliquid DOM confirmation:** Watch order book for large bid walls appearing at the support zone after the flush. Multiple large bids refreshing = institutional absorption. "Stuffed trade at support: institutions clearing out weak hands before the real move higher."
- **Undisturbed price target:** The price before the catalyst event that caused the flush is the extended target for the capitulation reversal. "Undisturbed price (before the catalyst) is the extended profit target."
- **Anchored VWAP from catalyst:** If a specific event caused the sell-off, anchor VWAP to that event. Reclaim of anchored VWAP = confirmation of reversal; holding above it = swing hold opportunity.
- **Binance spot:** Use for multi-day bounce trades in BTC/ETH after bear market capitulation — lower funding cost than perps for 2–5 day hold.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Catching falling knives without checklist:** Buying on the first red candle without confirming: (1) volume crescendo, (2) prior structural support reached, (3) tape absorption signal. "The most important lesson is not being seduced by big moves that are not capitulating and matching all the characteristics."
- **Not waiting for consolidation:** Buying the first bounce candle rather than waiting for the 5-bar consolidation — this results in entries before sellers have exhausted. "Wait for morning low to be tested and rejected before committing to full position."
- **Inside bar disqualifier ignored:** If the sell-off contained inside bars (price acceptance), the thesis is distribution, not capitulation. This is a manual check that must be performed.
- **Holding past VWAP failure:** Once the capitulation bounce reclaims VWAP but then closes back below it on a meaningful candle — that's the exit for 50%+ of the position. Do not hold through VWAP breakdown.
- **Lazy scaling:** Adding to a position before tape confirms the reversal (i.e., buying into a still-declining tape) rather than waiting for bid absorption signal.
- **Overextension without HTF support:** Price that is overextended intraday but has NOT reached a significant HTF support level — the bounce may be minor. Wait for HTF support confluence.
