## Preconditions

- Trend day confirmed and bullish direction established in the first 30-60 minutes (see trend_day_recognition_checklist.md)
- Price held above VWAP for the majority of the morning session (never closing a 5-min bar below VWAP)
- A midday consolidation has formed: price is ranging in a tight band (0.3-0.5 ATR) above VWAP
- Volume during the consolidation is BELOW morning volume — sellers are absent, not distributing
- This consolidation is the "second consolidation" or "above-the-clouds" base building
- 9 EMA remains above VWAP throughout the consolidation
- Market internals (CVD/breadth) remain aligned with the bull trend — not deteriorating during consolidation

**Timing context:**
- Equity session: typically 12:00-14:30 ET (midday lull) forms the consolidation; 14:30-16:00 ET is the second-session resumption
- Crypto equivalent: there is no fixed US midday for crypto, but the low-volume drift period between the Asia close (~06:00 ET) and US open (09:30 ET) or the European lunch window (11:00-13:00 ET) often produces the same consolidation above VWAP

## Entry trigger

**Primary (Above-the-Clouds / Second Consolidation Add):**
- Consolidation has formed: tight range (0.3-0.5 ATR wide) above VWAP for 60-120 minutes
- Volume during consolidation is declining vs morning levels (coiling)
- Enter long at the BOTTOM of the consolidation range (best risk/reward vs entering at breakout)
- Stop: below the false-break wick of the consolidation low, or below VWAP
- Alternative entry: break above the TOP of the consolidation range with volume expansion

**Secondary (Afternoon VWAP Hold Long):**
- Late-session (14:00-15:30 ET equity analog, or US session open for crypto) pullback to VWAP
- Price touches VWAP on declining volume; buyers step in and hold VWAP
- Enter on confirmed 5-min close ABOVE VWAP on the afternoon retest
- Stop: below the afternoon low (the consolidation low near VWAP)
- "3-4 PM trend is very hard to fade; institutional buying into close is consistent." In crypto: the US session close (15:30-16:00 ET) often sees institutional accumulation into the equity close.

**Tertiary (Big Dog 2-Hour Wedge Breakout):**
- Sector leader (narrative leader in crypto: the most-in-play token) forms a 2-4 hour tight wedge above VWAP
- Volume contracts during the wedge; price coils near the upper boundary
- Enter on breakout above wedge resistance with volume expansion (5-min close above wedge top)
- Stop: below the wedge low (which should be at or above VWAP)
- Target: measured move = wedge height added to breakout level

## Invalidation

- Consolidation breaks to the DOWNSIDE with volume: sellers overwhelm buyers in the base; exit/reduce
- 5-min close below VWAP after afternoon entry — bull thesis is over for the session
- Volume EXPANDS on the consolidation (not contracting) — this signals distribution, not accumulation
- CVD starts declining during the consolidation — internals deteriorating means the trend may not resume
- Significant new negative headline during consolidation period

## Targets

- **Hold into close:** Confirmed second-session resumption means hold all remaining position into EOD close
- Measured move: height of consolidation range added above breakout level = first target
- Psychological round number above
- Prior intraday high or all-time high (if trend day is from an ATH breakout)
- "Trail with 5-min 20 EMA break" — exit when 5-min candle closes below 20 EMA after resumption leg
- On crypto: hold until 00:00 UTC (daily reset) if still above VWAP; do not exit intrabar

## Sizing notes

- Enter consolidation bottom: up to 30-40% of remaining daily risk budget (some budget already deployed from morning entries)
- Enter on breakout: slightly smaller (20-30% of daily risk) since entry is higher and R:R is compressed vs bottom entry
- Stop distance from consolidation bottom is tight (below the false-break wick) — this is one of the best R:R entries of the day
- "Add at bottom of consolidation, hold for close" — the second consolidation entry is often the highest-conviction add of the trend day

## Crypto adaptation notes

**"Afternoon" window in 24/7 crypto:**
The equity "midday lull" (12:00-14:30 ET) and "afternoon continuation" (14:30-16:00 ET) are driven by institutional behavior around the US cash session. In crypto, the equivalent windows are:
1. **Asia → Europe transition (01:00-07:00 ET):** Low-volume drift period. If BTC holds above VWAP during this window with tight range = accumulation. The European open (07:00-09:30 ET) often provides the second-session resumption.
2. **US lunch lull (12:00-13:30 ET):** After the morning session, US-driven crypto volume dips. Tight consolidation above VWAP during this window = second-session resumption at the US afternoon re-open (13:30-14:30 ET).
3. **Post-equity-close (16:00-18:00 ET):** Sometimes a second leg in BTC occurs here as equity desks rebalance.

**"Above-the-Clouds" signal for crypto:** If BTC has held above its session VWAP for 4+ consecutive hours without a close below, and is now forming a tight consolidation (range <0.5% wide), the above-the-clouds pattern is active. Volume declining during the consolidation = institutional accumulation. Entry at the bottom of the range.

**Big Dog = Narrative Leader:** In crypto, the "Big Dog" is the highest-volume, highest-narrative-relevance token in the sector that is in play. On an L2 narrative day, it might be ARB or OP. On an AI day, it might be WLD or FET. The 2-4 hour wedge above VWAP in the narrative leader = above-the-clouds breakout entry.

**Sector-wide "above clouds" on crypto trend days:** On strong bull trend days where the entire market is participating, multiple tokens will form simultaneous above-the-clouds patterns. This is a signal to increase conviction — when the whole sector is building a second base, the continuation move tends to be larger.

**Funding rate during consolidation:** Healthy sign = funding rate stays mildly positive during the consolidation (longs are comfortable, not scared). Concerning sign = funding spikes sharply during consolidation (overheating; too many longs piling in). If funding spikes >0.05% per 8h during consolidation, reduce add size.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- **Entering at the top of the consolidation breakout only:** Missing the bottom-of-consolidation entry (best R:R) and only entering the breakout results in worse entry and smaller position from a tighter daily risk budget.
- **Exiting during midday consolidation:** "Patience for the setup to develop fully — consolidation must be complete before scaling." Do not exit just because price stops going up during the midday lull.
- **Adding during volume expansion inside the range:** Volume expanding inside the consolidation = distribution, not accumulation. Only add when volume contracts during the base.
- **Fading the afternoon continuation:** "3-4 PM trend is very hard to fade." In equity markets, the closing hour of a trend day almost always resolves in the direction of the trend. Do not short the afternoon continuation just because the morning move looks "extended."
- **Confusing a normal intraday consolidation with a trend reversal:** On trend days, consolidations above VWAP are NOT tops — they are loading zones. Only exit if VWAP is lost with expanding volume.

## Example(s)

**BTC Bull Trend Day — Second Consolidation Add:**
- Morning: BTC trends from $66,000 (ORB long at 09:35) to $68,500 by 11:30 ET. VWAP held every pullback.
- 11:30-13:30 ET: Midday consolidation. BTC ranges $68,200-$68,700 (tight 0.75% range). Volume declining. VWAP at $67,800. CVD still positive.
- Entry at bottom of consolidation: $68,200. Stop: $67,700 (below VWAP). This is the above-the-clouds entry.
- 13:45 ET: US afternoon session opens with volume. BTC breaks above $68,700 on expanding volume.
- 15:00 ET: BTC reaches $70,500. Trail with 15-min 20 EMA. Final exit at 15:45 at $70,200.
- R:R on the consolidation add: Risk $500 ($68,200-$67,700), gain $2,000 ($68,200-$70,200) = 4:1.

**ETH Big Dog 2-Hour Wedge:**
- Narrative: ETH ecosystem day (L2 protocol upgrade). ETH leads the morning with strong opening drive.
- 10:00-13:00 ET: ETH forms a 3-hour wedge ($3,180-$3,240 range, wedge narrowing). Volume declining.
- Wedge height = $60. Entry at $3,245 (breakout above wedge top). Stop at $3,190 (below wedge low).
- Target: $3,245 + $60 = $3,305. Hit at 14:30 ET.
