## Preconditions
- Major macro data release imminent or just printed (CPI, FOMC, NFP, PCE) — or a scheduled protocol/market event for crypto.
- Pre-built scenario game plan: bull / bear / neutral expected outcomes defined before the release.
- High-beta basket pre-identified: BTC, ETH, SOL (or top correlated altcoins on Hyperliquid) — the names that move first and farthest.
- Tape bias confirms buyers are in control within the first 30–60 seconds of the print: bids lifting asks, no persistent offer pressure.
- No open conflicting position from a prior session that would skew sizing judgment.
- (Crypto-specific) For token catalyst events (ETF inflow print, protocol upgrade, major exchange listing, large airdrop): analogous preconditions apply — news must be genuinely fresh, not re-reiterated.

## Entry trigger
1. **Immediate basket entry:** On a clearly favorable macro print (CPI miss = lower-than-expected; FOMC dovish pivot; NFP beat without wage pressure), get long the high-beta basket within the first 30 seconds. No waiting for candle confirmation if the thesis is clean and game plan was built pre-release.
2. **Opening range breakout (ORB):** If the initial move is not clean enough for immediate entry, wait for the first 5–15 minute opening range to form, then enter on the break of the range high with expanding volume.
3. **VWAP pullback add:** After a trend day is confirmed (funding rate rising on HL perps, BTC holding above VWAP all morning), add on the first light-volume pullback to VWAP or the 5-min / 15-min EMA. Do not chase; wait for the bounce.
4. **Catalyst + setup convergence:** Enter when a catalyst (macro print, ETF inflow news, protocol upgrade) aligns with a clear daily-chart technical breakout level (prior resistance, 52-week high reclaim). This double-confirmation entry commands larger sizing.
5. **Sector / sympathy long:** When a major token (BTC or ETH) gaps on macro, look for highest-beta correlated alts on Hyperliquid to express the same trade with better leverage efficiency.

## Invalidation
- Print hits and market fades within 2–3 minutes with no recovery — wrong-way reaction; exit immediately.
- BTC closes below VWAP on the 5-min chart after initial thrust — trend day off; reduce or exit.
- Funding rate on HL perps spikes aggressively positive at entry (>0.1% per 8h) — crowded long, wait for reset before adding.
- Tape shows persistent offer pressure (large asks not lifting) after the initial 30-second window.
- Price reclaims the ORB level on volume after the breakout fails — abort and reassess.

## Targets
- **T1:** 1 ATR from entry; trim 30–50% of position.
- **T2:** Trend-day close at or near the high of day; hold via trailing 5-min 20 EMA.
- **T3:** On multi-day trend days with strong internals, hold overnight to capture second-day follow-through (hand off to continuation playbook).
- For ORB entries: first extension is 1 ATR above ORB high; then trail with 5-min 20 EMA cross.

## Sizing notes
- **Immediate basket entry on hard macro beat:** Start at 50% of max size. Add on first VWAP pullback if the trend holds.
- **ORB entry:** Full size on the breakout; stop tight below ORB level.
- **VWAP pullback add:** Add 25–50% to existing position; total exposure capped at 1.5x normal unit.
- **Leverage:** HL perps 3–5x is appropriate for macro catalyst days; do not use max leverage on the initial entry — leave room to add.
- On trend days, do not scalp out early. Trade to hold, not scalp.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- CPI, FOMC, NFP prints move BTC/ETH directly and immediately (US cash session open ≈ 13:30 UTC). Build a basket of BTC, ETH, SOL perps on Hyperliquid before the print.
- Rising positive funding on Hyperliquid perps = trend day confirmation; add into pullbacks aggressively.
- "ETF inflows" are a direct crypto catalyst; large Blackrock / Fidelity IBIT inflow prints typically drive BTC spot and cascade into ETH/SOL alts.
- "Earnings" in equity context → "token unlock / protocol upgrade / scheduled chain event" in crypto context.
- "9:30 NYSE open" → "US cash session open ~13:30 UTC" or "BTC daily candle 00:00 UTC open" for Asian-session setups.
- BTC dominance rising during the catalyst confirms BTC is leading — rotate to BTC perps. BTC dominance falling during rally confirms alts are outperforming — add alt perps.
- Low-float tokens (new Hyperliquid listings, new Binance spot listings) can produce 5–10x the BTC move on equivalent news — size accordingly with tighter stops.
- For spot longs (long-only, no leverage): use Binance spot for BTC/ETH. Perps (HL or Binance futures) for leveraged directional plays.
