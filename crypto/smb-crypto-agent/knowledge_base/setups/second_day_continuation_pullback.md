## Preconditions

- Day 1: Asset had a strong catalyst-driven move (gap up, protocol news, token listing, earnings equivalent): closed near highs or in top 20% of the day's range
- Day 1 close: above prior day's high (institutional holding pattern confirmed)
- Day 2 opens near Day 1 close or slightly below; a pullback occurs during Day 2 session
- The pullback during Day 2 holds above the Day 1 VWAP or 2-day anchored VWAP — this is the key test
- Volume on the Day 2 pullback is lower than Day 1 volume (corrective, not distributive)
- Higher low formed on Day 2 vs Day 1's intraday lows — confirms buyers are stepping in at higher prices
- For crypto: funding rate on Day 2 should not be spiking extreme (avoid if perp premium > 0.15% per 8h)

## Entry trigger

**2-Day VWAP bounce (primary):**
1. Day 2 VWAP calculated (anchored from Day 1 open) — this is the institutional average cost of all Day 1-2 buyers
2. Price dips to or below the 2-day VWAP during Day 2 session
3. "Stuff and rubber-band" turn: sellers push below 2-day VWAP but bids immediately absorb; price snaps back above within 1–3 candles
4. Enter on the reclaim of 2-day VWAP with stop 2 ticks below the rejection candle low

**Higher-low reclaim (alternative):**
1. Day 2 forms a series of higher lows vs Day 1's intraday low on 5m chart
2. Price breaks above the most recent lower high (Day 2) with volume expansion
3. Enter on the breakout bar close; stop below the most recent higher low

**Day 3 continuation (most conservative):**
1. Day 2 consolidates in upper portion of Day 1 range on elevated volume (absorption)
2. Day 3 opens above Day 2 consolidation high — buy the first pullback to VWAP or prior high that holds
3. Stop: below Day 3's first 15m low

## Invalidation

- Day 2 price closes below the 2-day VWAP with expanding volume — catalyst buyers are underwater and selling
- Day 2 fails to hold above Day 1's opening level — full gap fill signals reversal
- Higher low structure breaks: price undercuts Day 2's intraday low with volume
- For Day 3: fails to break above Day 2 consolidation range by midday with no sign of buyers

## Targets

- T1: Day 1's high of day — take 40–50% here (first real resistance)
- T2: Measured move from the 2-day VWAP (Day 1 range added above Day 1 high)
- T3: Swing extension — hold 20–25% as a runner with daily higher-low trailing stop
- Exit discipline: take first half at T1; hold remainder until 90-period MA on 1m breaks (for short-term trail) or daily higher low breaks (for multi-day hold)

## Sizing notes

- Base size on Day 2 setups (catalyst is 24+ hours old; more information available)
- Add to 1.5× on Day 3 if Day 2 closed in top quartile of its range with elevated volume — Day 3 has highest institutional follow-through rate
- Reduce to 50% base size if: RVOL below 1.0× on Day 2, or market environment uncertain (BTC at key resistance)
- For crypto IPO/listing analogs: Day 3 post-listing with elevated volume is equivalent to "Day 3 breakout with Big-Dog consolidation add" — size up on Day 3 if price held above Day 1 closing price each day

## Crypto adaptation notes

- In crypto, the "day" = a full 24-hour session (midnight UTC to midnight UTC); the 2-day VWAP anchors from the prior day's midnight open and is visible on TradingView for all perp pairs
- Catalyst-day equivalent events for crypto: token listing on major exchange (Binance, Coinbase), ETF approval announcement, major protocol mainnet launch, large institutional buy announcement
- Post-catalyst consolidation signals in crypto are faster — if a token held its VWAP for 6+ hours after the initial move, that is the crypto equivalent of Day 2 healthy consolidation
- Funding rate is the Day 2 positioning signal: if funding goes from elevated (>0.08%) back toward neutral (<0.03%) on Day 2 — excess longs have been shaken out and the next leg is cleaner
- Asia session open (8 PM ET) on Day 2 is a critical test: if price holds the 2-day VWAP during Asia session, US session continuation is likely
- For small-cap alts (<$500M market cap): the Day 2 pullback is often more violent (20–30% retrace of Day 1 move); the higher-low entry on Day 2 or Day 3 is safer than the 2-day VWAP for these names

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- Chasing the Day 1 move instead of waiting for Day 2 entry — "fashionably late" is higher-probability than chasing the initial spike
- Entering on any Day 2 dip without confirming that volume is below Day 1 levels — heavy Day 2 volume on the decline = distribution, exit immediately
- Ignoring the "closed in top 20% of range" requirement for Day 1 — a weak Day 1 close (below midrange) signals weak institutional conviction; skip the Day 2 setup
- Shorting "day 2 fade" setups prematurely — institutions who missed Day 1 come in on Day 2; the trade is with them, not against them
- Using the same R target as Day 1 — Day 2 moves are typically 50–70% of Day 1 size; calibrate targets accordingly

## Example(s)

**BTC/USDC perp (HL), post-ETF approval — 2-day VWAP:**
- Day 1: ETF approval news; BTC runs from $84,000 to $95,000; closes at $94,500 (top 10% of range); VWAP Day 1 = $90,000
- Day 2 open: $93,000; pulls back during Asia session to $90,500 (2-day VWAP territory)
- "Stuff and rubber-band": wick to $89,800; immediately re-bids to $90,600
- Enter long at $90,800; stop at $89,600; T1: $95,000 (Day 1 HOD); T2: $100,000
- R:R: ($95,000 - $90,800) / ($90,800 - $89,600) = 3.5:1 to T1, much higher to T2

**SOL/USDC perp (HL), post-mainnet upgrade — Day 3 consolidation:**
- Day 1: SOL protocol upgrade; runs 25% from $140 to $175; closes near high
- Day 2: Consolidates $160–$175 on elevated volume; ends at $173 (top 15% of range)
- Day 3: Opens $172; first pullback to $168 (Day 2 VWAP equivalent) on declining volume
- Higher-low pattern: Day 2 low was $160; Day 3 low is $168 — confirmed higher low
- Enter long at $170; stop $164; T1: $185; T2: measured move to $205

**ETH/USDC — anchored VWAP from catalyst date (swing):**
