# Demand-Discovery Scan Summary
Date: 2026-05-17
Researcher: opportunity-researcher
Scan Type: Wide demand-discovery (daily scan)

## Research Methodology
- Web search for commercial B2B data vendors selling professional license and business contact databases
- Validated pricing signals, buyer segments, and deliverability claims
- Confirmed public data availability via state regulatory boards, federal databases, and municipal directories
- Scored opportunities based on willingness-to-pay, data availability, audience size, competitive landscape, and engineering complexity

## Opportunities Identified: 5
All scored >= 6 (threshold for brief creation)

### 1. Licensed Attorneys/Lawyers (State Bar) (Score: 9)
- **Market size:** 1.3M licensed attorneys nationwide (900K-1M unique individuals)
- **Commercial validation:** 7+ vendors (BizProspex $200, Sales.co $295, Thomson Data 1.3M+ profiles, ContactOut 1M+, BookYourData 373K)
- **Data source:** All 50 state Bar Associations + DC (public attorney license registries, no auth required)
- **Key buyers:** LegalTech vendors (Clio, MyCase), CLE providers, legal research platforms, malpractice insurers, legal marketing agencies
- **Price point:** $99-$149 one-time CSV
- **Engineering lift:** Moderate (51 state bar websites, scrape/download, dedupe multi-jurisdiction)

### 2. NMLS Mortgage Loan Originators (Score: 8)
- **Market size:** 400K NMLS-registered mortgage professionals nationwide
- **Commercial validation:** 6+ vendors (BookYourData 114K, ITNow 72K+, AverickMedia 37K, DataCaptive, LakeB2B, InfoGlobalData)
- **Data source:** NMLS Consumer Access database (www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org) — authoritative federal SAFE Act public registry
- **Key buyers:** Mortgage tech vendors (Encompass, Calyx, Blend), CRM platforms, CE providers, lead generation platforms, marketing agencies
- **Price point:** $99-$149 one-time CSV
- **Engineering lift:** Moderate (NMLS searchable interface, no bulk API — requires state-by-state scraping or integration)

### 3. School Superintendents & Principals (K-12) (Score: 8)
- **Market size:** 13.5K district superintendents + 130K school principals nationwide
- **Commercial validation:** 7+ vendors (Principals-EmailList 5M+ contacts, K12Data 98% deliverability, SchoolDataLists, EducationDataLists, BookYourData)
- **Data source:** NCES Common Core of Data (CCD) for superintendents + state education department directories for principals
- **Key buyers:** EdTech vendors (Google Edu, Clever, Canvas, PowerSchool), educational publishers, school safety vendors, facilities contractors, PD providers
- **Price point:** $99-$149 one-time CSV
- **Engineering lift:** Low-to-moderate (NCES CCD bulk downloads available; state education dept websites for principals)

### 4. Licensed Hotels/Motels/Lodging (Score: 7)
- **Market size:** 50K+ licensed hotels, motels, inns, B&Bs nationwide
- **Commercial validation:** 6+ vendors (BookYourData 433K contacts, BizProspex $200, LeadsPlease $299.75, TargetNXT, ITNow 25K+, InfoGlobalData)
- **Data source:** State health department public lodging databases (FL, MA, MN confirmed accessible; remaining 47 states case-by-case)
- **Key buyers:** Hospitality tech vendors (Opera PMS, Cloudbeds, Mews), hotel supply/equipment vendors, payment processors, marketing agencies
- **Price point:** $99-$149 one-time CSV
- **Engineering lift:** Moderate-to-high (state-by-state harvest; not all states offer bulk downloads — some require scraping or FOIA)
- **Note:** Score reduced from 8 to 7 due to inconsistent public data accessibility across states

### 5. Municipal Government Officials (Score: 7)
- **Market size:** 21K municipalities (cities, counties, townships) with 250K+ elected officials and department heads
- **Commercial validation:** 5 vendors (Power Almanac 250K records across 21K municipalities, InfoGlobalData, ContactOut NYC 5,387, ITNow, AverickMedia, Civic Data)
- **Data source:** Municipal staff directories (public record on 21K+ city/county websites) + state municipal league member directories
- **Key buyers:** GovTech vendors (Tyler Technologies, CivicPlus, Granicus), infrastructure contractors, municipal bond underwriters, government consultants
- **Price point:** $99-$149 one-time CSV
- **Engineering lift:** Moderate-to-high (21K-source aggregation, no centralized federal database, high maintenance burden due to official turnover)
- **Note:** Score held at 7 (not 8) due to engineering complexity and data maintenance challenges

## Opportunities Below Threshold (Researched but not briefed)
- **Property Managers/Apartment Buildings:** Strong commercial validation (Scrap.io 251K apartment buildings, BizProspex $200+) but weak public data availability — no centralized property manager licensing (varies by state), would require county-by-county property records aggregation. Score: 5-6
- **Public Libraries:** Weaker commercial validation (BookYourData $99 for 250 leads, smaller TAM ~17K library systems) and smaller B2B buyer market vs. other opportunities, though IMLS Public Libraries Survey provides easy bulk data. Score: 5-6

## Key Insights
1. **Professional license databases continue to show exceptionally strong willingness-to-pay** — attorneys (score 9) and mortgage loan originators (score 8) join insurance agents, pharmacists, veterinarians as top-tier opportunities with 7+ commercial vendors and premium pricing ($200-$295 baseline)
2. **Federal/authoritative databases reduce engineering lift significantly** — NMLS Consumer Access (federal SAFE Act mandate) and NCES Common Core of Data (Department of Education) provide higher-quality baseline data vs. state-by-state aggregation
3. **K-12 education market is underexplored** — school superintendents/principals (score 8) tap into $50B+ EdTech and education services market with 98% deliverability claims from commercial vendors
4. **Government and hospitality verticals show moderate-to-strong signals but higher complexity** — hotels/motels and municipal officials both score 7 due to strong B2B demand ($10K-$50M contract values) but inconsistent public data accessibility across states increases engineering lift
5. **All top 5 opportunities have zero affordable flat-file competitors** on Gumroad/similar marketplaces — vendors sell annual subscriptions ($2,000-$25,000/year) or custom lists, not one-time CSVs
6. **Maintenance burden varies significantly** — professional licenses (attorneys, MLOs) have low turnover and stable credentials; municipal officials have 15-25% annual turnover requiring ongoing data refreshes

## Competitive Landscape Analysis
- **Attorneys:** No flat-file CSV competitor; all vendors (BizProspex, Thomson Data, ContactOut) sell subscriptions or per-record pricing
- **NMLS MLOs:** No flat-file CSV competitor; BookYourData and others sell custom extracts, not bulk downloads
- **School Officials:** No flat-file CSV competitor; Principals-EmailList, K12Data, SchoolDataLists all subscription-based
- **Hotels/Motels:** No flat-file CSV competitor; LeadsPlease, BizProspex, BookYourData sell custom lists or subscriptions
- **Municipal Officials:** Power Almanac likely subscription-based; no CSV alternative identified
