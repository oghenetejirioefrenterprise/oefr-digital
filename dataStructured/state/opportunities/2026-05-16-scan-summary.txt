# Demand-Discovery Scan Summary
Date: 2026-05-16
Researcher: opportunity-researcher
Scan Type: Wide demand-discovery (daily scan)

## Research Methodology
- Web search for commercial B2B data vendors selling professional license databases
- Validated pricing signals and buyer segments
- Confirmed public data availability via state regulatory boards
- Scored opportunities based on willingness-to-pay, data availability, audience size, and competitive landscape

## Opportunities Identified: 4
All scored >= 6 (threshold for brief creation)

### 1. Licensed Insurance Agent Registry (Score: 9)
- **Market size:** 1.2M licensed agents nationwide
- **Commercial validation:** 5+ vendors (InfoGlobalData 181K-245K, InsuranceAgentLists.com 1.2M+, pricing $0.15/record baseline)
- **Data source:** NIPR + all 50 state Departments of Insurance
- **Key buyers:** Insurance carriers, InsurTech vendors, CE providers, recruiting firms
- **Price point:** $99-$149 one-time CSV

### 2. Licensed Veterinarian Registry (Score: 8)
- **Market size:** 120K licensed veterinarians nationwide
- **Commercial validation:** 6+ vendors (Scrap.io 61K, LakeB2B 50K+, pricing $0.10-$0.50/contact)
- **Data source:** All 50 state Veterinary Medical Boards (FL offers downloads, LA charges $0.02/record)
- **Key buyers:** Veterinary pharma, equipment vendors, practice management software, pet food companies
- **Price point:** $79-$99 one-time CSV

### 3. Licensed Pharmacist Registry (Score: 8)
- **Market size:** 340K licensed pharmacists nationwide
- **Commercial validation:** 7+ vendors (BookYourData 40K+, Scrap.io 171K+, pricing $200+ baseline)
- **Data source:** All 50 state Boards of Pharmacy (TX updates M-F by noon)
- **Key buyers:** Pharmaceutical manufacturers, pharmacy software vendors, equipment suppliers, CE providers
- **Price point:** $79-$99 one-time CSV

### 4. Licensed Chiropractor Registry (Score: 7)
- **Market size:** 75K-90K licensed chiropractors nationwide
- **Commercial validation:** 5+ vendors (LeadsPlease 87K, pricing $42-$997 wide range, IBLead aggressive at $42 for 10K)
- **Data source:** All 50 state Chiropractic Boards
- **Key buyers:** Chiropractic equipment vendors, practice management software, supplement companies, CE providers
- **Price point:** $59-$79 one-time CSV (competitive pricing vs. IBLead)

## Opportunities Below Threshold (Researched but not briefed)
- **Professional Engineers (PE licenses):** Privacy legislation (TX SB 510, 2023) removed contact info from public rosters; may affect other states. Score: 4
- **Notary Public:** Public databases found (NY, CA downloadable) but no commercial vendor demand signals. Score: 5
- **Architects:** Public license lookups via NCARB and state boards, but no commercial list vendors found. Score: 5
- **Food establishment inspections:** Public databases in many cities/states but weak B2B buyer signals (inspection data, not business contact info). Score: 5

## Key Insights
1. **Healthcare/professional license databases have exceptionally strong willingness-to-pay** - validated by 5-7 commercial vendors per vertical with premium pricing ($0.10-$0.50/contact baseline)
2. **Insurance vertical is highest-scoring** (score 9) due to massive TAM (1.2M agents), premium baseline pricing ($0.15/record = $15K for 100K records), and NIPR national aggregation
3. **All top opportunities have zero affordable flat-file competitors** on Gumroad/similar marketplaces - vendors sell annual subscriptions ($800-$10,000/year) or per-record pricing, not one-time CSVs
4. **Public data is confirmed available** across all 50 states for all 4 opportunities, with some states offering downloadable databases (FL veterinarians, multiple states for various professions)
5. **Engineering lift is moderate** for all opportunities - 51-state scrape/download, deduplication, normalization

## Recommendation
All 4 opportunities scored >= 6 and merit CEO review for prioritization and data-engineer assignment. Insurance agents (score 9) should be prioritized given highest willingness-to-pay validation and largest TAM.
