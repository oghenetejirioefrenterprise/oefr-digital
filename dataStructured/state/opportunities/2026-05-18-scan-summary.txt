## Research methodology
- Web search across commercial B2B database vendors to validate willingness-to-pay
- Public data source verification (state licensing boards, federal registries, NCEES/NCARB/ASC aggregators)
- B2B buyer segment identification (software vendors, CE providers, insurance, recruiters, suppliers)
- Baseline pricing analysis to establish commercial market benchmarks
- TAM estimation based on license counts and buyer universe sizing

### Score 7 — Solid signal
4. **Licensed Funeral Directors** — 2026-05-18-licensed-funeral-directors.json
   - Commercial validation: Dietrich Direct ($69), IT NOW (8456), Bancomail (4338), InfoGlobalData, SphereSçout

5. **General Contractor State Licenses** — 2026-05-18-general-contractor-state-licenses.json
   - Commercial validation: BookYourData (27K), ContactOut, EmailListUS, CampaignLake
   - Public data: ~25-30 states with statewide contractor licensing (California CSLB downloadable master list = 350K contractors!)
   - TAM: ~500K-750K contractor businesses across licensed states
   - B2B buyers: Construction software (Procore, Buildertrend $5K-$15K/yr lists), surety bond companies, commercial insurance, building suppliers
   - Key advantage: California CSLB downloadable database (massive single-state source), high-value B2B segments
   - Limitation: Only ~25-30 states have statewide licensing (not all 50), state-by-state variance in formats

## Research notes
- ASC federal appraiser registry is downloadable — rare federal data source advantage
- California CSLB contractor database is downloadable — 350K records from single state
- NCEES (PEs) and NCARB (architects) provide centralized directories linking to all state boards
- The International Conference aggregates funeral director lookups across all 51 jurisdictions
- All opportunities have validated commercial vendors at $69-$300 baseline pricing
- All opportunities have 100% public data (no PII, no auth required)
- All opportunities have 3-5 distinct B2B buyer segments with proven purchasing behavior
