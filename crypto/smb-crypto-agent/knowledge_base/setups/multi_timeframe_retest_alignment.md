# Multi-Timeframe Retest Alignment
**Category:** second_chance_retest  **Timeframe:** intraday/swing  **Confidence:** high  **Venues:** HL perps (BTC, ETH, SOL), Binance spot (long-bias), Binance futures (USDⓈ-M)

## Preconditions
- A significant support or resistance level is identified on the **higher timeframe (HTF)**: daily chart (swing trades) or 4-hour chart (intraday trades). The level has at least 2–3 prior touches to establish significance.
- The **intermediate timeframe (ITF)** — 1-hour (swing) or 15-min (intraday) — shows price setting up a pattern (consolidation, flag, higher low, or reversal candle) as it approaches the HTF level.
- The **entry timeframe (LTF)** — 15-min (swing) or 5-min (intraday) — provides the precise trigger at the confluence zone.
- All three timeframes point in the same direction at the moment of entry: HTF trend, ITF setup, LTF trigger.
- Anchored VWAP (AVWAP) from the most significant catalyst or breakout date aligns with the HTF level or lies between price and the level — adds confluence.
- Institutional alignment (for crypto): on-chain whale accumulation or large bid visible in order book at the HTF level.

## Entry trigger
- **Three-tier framework (HTF → ITF → LTF):**
  1. HTF confirms the level is relevant (prior bounces, confluence with Fibonacci 38.2–61.8% retracement, or anchored VWAP).
  2. ITF shows a setup pattern forming as price approaches: flag, consolidation, or higher low (for longs) / lower high (for shorts).
  3. LTF gives the precise entry: reversal candle at the level, tape shows bid absorption (for longs) or offer wall holding (for shorts), volume contracts then expands on the signal bar.
- **Fibonacci multi-timeframe long:** H4 shows 61.8% Fibonacci support. H1 shows a developing setup. 15-min gives the trigger — enter at the 15-min confirmation with stop at the 61.8% level. "H4 for the level, H1 for the setup, 15-minute for the trigger."
- **Anchored VWAP second-day play:** HTF = anchored VWAP from catalyst day. ITF = price approaching AVWAP on day 2. LTF = reversal or hold pattern at AVWAP with tape confirmation. Long on AVWAP hold, stop below AVWAP.
- **Flag breakout with LTF confirmation:** Daily uptrend flag. 15-min flag boundary defined. 1-min tape shows bid absorption at flag low; break of flag high on volume = entry.
- Stop: at the HTF level (most conservative) or at the LTF trigger candle low (most aggressive). Never beyond the HTF level.

## Invalidation
- HTF level fails: price closes through the HTF support/resistance on volume — multi-timeframe alignment breaks down.
- ITF setup pattern breaks before LTF trigger fires — wait and reassess.
- Anchored VWAP closes below (for longs) or above (for shorts) — primary catalyst buyers now underwater; thesis weakens.
- Correlated market (BTC for alts, ETH for DeFi tokens) moves strongly against the expected direction.

## Targets
- **T1:** ITF resistance or support (next level on the 1-hour or 15-min chart). Cover 33%.
- **T2:** HTF measured move target — range width added to breakout on the daily/4-hour chart. Cover 33%.
- **Trailing target (runner):** Trail with ITF (1-hour or 15-min) higher lows (long) or lower highs (short) until trend reverses on ITF.
- For Fibonacci-based plays: T1 = 61.8% retracement resistance; T2 = 100% extension (market cycle level 1). "100 pips on this trade from a very precise entry."
- For AVWAP-based plays: T1 = prior catalyst day high; T2 = 1.5× ATR extension above AVWAP.

## Sizing notes
- Full allocation (100%) when all three timeframes are aligned AND tape/volume confirms at entry. This is the highest-quality setup in the second_chance_retest category.
- Add on: break of the ITF resistance (longs) or support (shorts) — adds 25–50% after initial thesis confirmed.
- Reduce to 50% if only two of three timeframes align (e.g., HTF + ITF but LTF is ambiguous).
- The tight stop at the HTF level allows maximum position size for given dollar risk — this is the structural edge vs. random-entry traders.
- For swing positions held overnight: require HTF close confirmation; do not hold through a gap that breaks the HTF level.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- **BTC/ETH on Hyperliquid (perps):** Primary application. Daily chart provides HTF context. 4-hour provides ITF setup. 15-min provides LTF entry. Identical framework to forex/equity SMB methodology.
- **Anchored VWAP significance:** Anchor to the most significant price events: ETF approval date, halving candle, major exchange listing, or protocol upgrade. "The anchored VWAP from the catalyst date tells you the average price of everyone who bought on the news — if it holds, they're supporting it."
- **50-day / 200-day MA confluence:** BTC above its 200-day MA in an uptrend + pullback to 50-day MA (or 200-period on 4h) with ITF flag forming + LTF hammer = prime multi-timeframe long setup.
- **Sector confirmation:** When ETH or SOL shows the same multi-timeframe alignment as BTC (or confirms the same Fibonacci level), conviction is higher — "Use correlation with other instruments to increase confidence."
- **Binance spot:** Preferred for swing multi-timeframe alignment longs in BTC or ETH where the HTF level is significant (200-day, major breakout retest). Avoids funding costs for multi-day holds.
- **Binance USDⓈ-M futures:** Use when needing leverage on swing setups with multi-day hold and defined HTF stop.
- **24/7 market note:** Crypto does not have a defined session open. Use UTC midnight or 9:30 ET as session reference. Asian session highs/lows serve as the ITF context for US session multi-timeframe entries.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Entering on HTF alone without ITF/LTF confirmation** — price at the daily 61.8% Fibonacci doesn't mean entering immediately; wait for the ITF setup and LTF trigger.
- **Using only one timeframe for stop placement** — stop should be at or just below the HTF level, not the LTF trigger candle low (too tight) or some arbitrary ATR (too wide).
- **Skipping correlated instrument confirmation** — in forex/crypto, if BTC fails to confirm the ETH setup at a key level, conviction is lower. "Use correlation to increase confidence."
- **Not anchoring VWAP to the right event** — anchoring VWAP to the wrong date destroys the signal. Anchor to: IPO/listing day, catalyst/earnings day, ATH day, or breakout day.
- **Adding on ambiguous ITF signals** — only add when the ITF level breaks cleanly with volume; do not add on ambiguous candles at midrange.
- **Ignoring time-of-day / session** — for intraday setups, LTF triggers after 2:30 PM ET (or after 18:00 UTC in crypto) carry lower follow-through probability in equity-correlated sessions.

## Example(s)
**Multi-timeframe Fibonacci long (forex → crypto):** 4H BTC chart shows price at 61.8% Fibonacci support. 1-hour chart shows a tight consolidation forming at that level. 15-min shows a hammer candle. Enter long at the 15-min level. Stop: below 61.8% on 4H. Target: 100% Fibonacci extension. "H4 for the level, H1 for the setup, 15-minute for the trigger." R:R = 5:1.

**AVWAP second-day play (BTC post-ETF approval):** Anchor VWAP to ETF approval date (day 1 catalyst). Day 2 BTC pulls back to AVWAP on lower volume. 15-min shows buyers stepping in at AVWAP. 5-min hammer candle with tape absorption. Enter long. Stop: below AVWAP. Target: prior day high, then 1.5× ATR extension. "The stock held above anchored VWAP — if it holds, they're supporting it."

**Flag breakout three-timeframe (BTC 4H uptrend):** Daily chart: BTC in clear uptrend. 4H chart: tight flag consolidation forming at prior resistance (now support). 15-min chart: tape shows bid absorption at flag low; orders on ask slowing. Enter on 15-min flag break above lower-highs resistance with expanding volume. Stop: below flag low (4H). Target: flagpole height added to breakout point.

**50-day MA retest (ETH swing):** Daily: ETH above 50-day MA in uptrend. ETH pulls back to 50-day MA on declining volume. 1-hour: hammer candle at 50-day MA. 15-min: volume dry-up then reversal. Enter long at 15-min trigger. Stop: below 50-day MA. T1: prior swing high. T2: 1 ATR extension. Trail with 15-min higher lows.
