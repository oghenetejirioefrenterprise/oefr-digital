## Preconditions
**Gap fade criteria — the more that align, the higher the confidence:**
1. Asset has gapped significantly (5%+ for large-cap; 20%+ for small-cap) on a catalyst that is already priced in (buy-the-rumor scenario, anticipated event, slight beat on high expectations)
2. Gap opens directly into a major multi-timeframe resistance zone (prior ATH, multi-month resistance, round number, options max pain / pin level)
3. Asset is already massively extended YTD or on the recent run (30%+ YTD for large-cap; 100%+ for small-cap/crypto) — priced-in news = distribution opportunity
4. Low short float (crypto equivalent: circulating supply relative to locked/vesting) — verifies no squeeze risk before fading
5. Market regime: overall market flat or weak; fading gap-up into resistance is strongly amplified in weak/bear-market regimes
6. Historical gap behavior: for the specific asset, gap-fades have a >60% close-below-open rate (backtest-validated when possible)
7. For sell-the-news specifically: event was widely anticipated by crowd; no surprise element in the catalyst

**Intraday readiness before entry:**
- Map the resistance level pre-market; have a written game plan with specific entry/re-entry/stop/target before open
- Verify the stock is NOT making new 52-week highs cleanly with volume (that would be continuation, not fade)
- Check sector: if the entire sector is gapping up on the same catalyst, individual name fade risk is higher

## Entry trigger
**Primary — gap fade short (pre-planned level):**
Offer stock in pre-market at the identified resistance zone (or set alert). At open: if price stalls within first 30 minutes at the pre-market resistance level and tape shows heavy sellers (unlimited offers refreshing, bids that cannot hold), enter short with stop above the pre-market high + small buffer.

**First move confirmation entry:**
If gap opens and first move is downward (price breaks below the opening print within first 15 minutes), enter short on that break. Stop: above the gap-open high. This is the highest-probability entry pattern (first move direction tends to persist for 1-3 hours).

**Re-entry after first pull down (more conservative):**
After initial fade, price retraces back to the resistance zone (now confirmed resistance). Tape again shows sellers refreshing at that level; re-enter short on the failed re-test. Stop: above the re-test high.

**3-trade breaking-news playbook (for major gaps):**
1. Pre-market break: short immediately on gap below prior-day support
2. Opening range breakdown: wait for 1-min candle close below ORB low
3. Consolidation breakdown: short when price breaks below day's consolidation range on volume
4. (Swing) Overnight: weak close below key level with no buyer defense = overnight short via perp

## Invalidation
- Price holds above resistance level after initial fade and buyers absorb all offers — NOT a fade, likely continuation
- Stock makes new session high above the gap-open high with volume (gap-and-go continuation; cover immediately)
- Tape shows offer absorption on the first test of resistance (big bids hitting offers = institutional buyers present)
- Market regime flips bullish intraday (broad market reversal provides lift to all gap names)
- High short float discovered after entry (squeeze risk — cover and reduce size)

## Targets
- T1: Morning low (partial cover 50%; established by first 30-min price action)
- T2: VWAP of current day
- T3: Prior day close (gap fill target for full fades)
- T4 (swing): Prior support level 4-5 points below; hold as swing with stop at the re-test high
- Daily ATR-based: first target at 1 ATR below resistance; second at 2 ATR for extended moves

## Sizing notes
- Market regime adjusts size: bear market / weak market = full intended size; neutral market = 75%; bull market = 50% or skip
- Enter initial position at 33% on the first fade confirmation; add 33% on the re-entry at resistance; final 33% on ORB break confirmation
- Three-strikes rule: maximum 3 entry attempts on the setup; if 3 attempts fail, the fade is not working — do not force
- Take profits faster in volatile bear markets; don't hold for full ATR extension as reversals are sharp

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Sell-the-news is among the most reliable crypto patterns: BTC/ETH events (ETF approval day, halving day, major protocol launch) frequently exhibit run-into-event → gap-up → fade structure
- Crypto tokens that pump 200%+ in a cycle and then gap up on bullish catalyst news are prime fade candidates; use rising BTC dominance as an additional filter (rising BTC dominance + altcoin gap up = strong fade candidate)
- For funding-driven gaps on Hyperliquid perpetuals: a gap-up open with very positive funding rate (>0.08%/8h) at resistance = ideal fade setup; the funding also provides directional confirmation
- The 3-trade playbook maps directly to crypto 24/7 markets: (1) pre-session break (funding flip), (2) US open equivalent ORB, (3) consolidation break, (4) overnight swing via perp
- Crypto "sell the news" timeline: token runs into event (typically 2-3 days before), event occurs, then fades. Enter the fade on the event day itself when the gap fails to hold
- Backtesting gap behavior on crypto: BTC gap-up after weekend open >2% closes below open historically >55-60% of the time — use as directional bias + tape for entry timing

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Not having a written game plan before the open for earnings/catalyst fades — entry must be mechanical, not reactive
- Fading without checking the long-term chart for resistance first — the best fades are when the gap opens directly into a multi-year resistance zone
- Not verifying short float / circulating supply before fading — high short interest = squeeze risk; always check
- Entering the fade before the first move confirms (anticipating the fade pre-open = lower probability; wait for the 15-min direction signal)
- Holding the full fade position through a VWAP reclaim — cover partials at the morning low, hold remainder only if tape stays heavy at the re-test level
- "Being a wuss" — covering too early (at 75% of the expected move) instead of holding to pre-defined targets; use tiered exits to remove emotion
- Not using gap stats to determine directional bias — combine backtest edge with tape timing for highest confidence

## Example(s)
**AAPL sell-the-news gap fade:** Stock gaps up on major deal/product news with no fundamental earnings beat; first move is downward, takes out prior opening price; short on that break; stop above gap-open high; T1 = VWAP, T2 = prior support; if close below key support, hold as swing.

**BTC ETF approval sell-the-news (generalized):** BTC/IBIT ran strongly into ETF approval announcement; gaps up 4% at open; opens at prior multi-month resistance; first 15 minutes shows heavy tape (offers refreshing, bids not absorbing); short at resistance with stop above pre-market high; T1 = gap fill, T2 = prior week's VWAP.

**BBBY meme sell-the-news gap fade (>100% gap, no fundamental catalyst):** Gaps >100% pre-market; spray candles (long upper wick, close near low) = exhausted buyers; short below VWAP on failed reclaim; VWAP = short entry on each re-test; cover into each new low flush.
