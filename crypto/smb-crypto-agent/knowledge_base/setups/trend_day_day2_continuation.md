## Preconditions

**Day 1 requirements (the catalyst day):**
- Strong directional move on Day 1 driven by a fresh, high-quality catalyst (protocol upgrade, exchange listing, major partnership, macro catalyst)
- ATR on Day 1 is 3%+ (significant move — institutional participation confirmed)
- Volume in first 10-15 minutes of Day 1 exceeds 30% of average daily volume (strong signal: "30% ADV in less than 5 minutes is a strong signal")
- Day 1 closes in the top 25-30% of its intraday range (buyers in control at close; institutions could not get full allocation on Day 1)
- Day 1 gap does NOT fill by close — the catalyst energy is sustained

**Day 2 pre-market requirements:**
- Price holding ABOVE Day 1 close in pre-market (not giving back the prior day's gain)
- Pre-market volume confirms ongoing institutional interest (AVAL >35% of ADV in pre-market window)
- Key levels from Day 1 identified and mapped: Day 1 open, Day 1 high, Day 1 afternoon high, Day 1 close
- No contradictory news or catalyst reversal overnight

**Thesis:** Large institutional funds need overnight time to complete analysis and get allocation approval. Day 2 represents their entry — they buy on Day 2, creating the follow-through that Day 1 retail chasers missed.

## Entry trigger

**Primary (Day 2 Strong Opening Drive):**
- Day 2 opens and within first 15-30 minutes breaks through multiple Day 1 resistance levels on volume
- Wait for the opening drive to stall and consolidate ABOVE the broken resistance levels
- Enter long on: (a) the consolidation breakout above Day 2 opening range high, or (b) pullback to the broken resistance level (now support) that holds
- Stop: below the Day 2 consolidation low, or below Day 1 close

**Secondary (Day 2 VWAP Hold — Key Level Retest):**
- Day 2 opens near or above Day 1 key support/resistance level
- Price tests Day 1 key level in morning and holds (buyers absorb sellers at this level)
- Enter long on confirmation hold or on 5-min close above Day 1 high
- Stop: below Day 1 support (the key level)
- Targets: Day 1 high → 1.5-2x Day 1 range extension

**Tertiary (Day 3 Accumulation Day Breakout — "Elite Breakout" variant):**
- Day 1: strong catalyst move (5%+), closes strong
- Day 2: inside day / low-volume consolidation (accumulation) — price holds near Day 1 high but volume is subdued
- Day 3: break above Day 2 high within first 30 minutes with volume expansion
- Entry: buy the break of Day 2 high on Day 3 open; stop below Day 3 low of day
- Targets: scale 1/3 on momentum, trail with 2-min 9 EMA; final exit on first 5-min close below 9 EMA
- "When it works, it really works." — The Day 2 inside/low-volume accumulation traps chasers and gives shorts false confidence before the Day 3 squeeze.

**Day 2 continuation target:** "Approximately 90% of Day 1 measured move" — if Day 1 moved 10%, Day 2 continuation targets 9% from the Day 2 open in the same direction.

## Invalidation

- Day 2 opens BELOW Day 1 close with volume — institutions are distributing, not adding
- Day 2 volume drops sharply below Day 1 volume in first 30 minutes — no institutional continuation
- Day 2 price fails to hold Day 1 close as support during morning; instead rolls over
- Sector rotates negative during Day 2 (the catalyst sector is no longer in play)
- New negative development contradicting the catalyst (hack recovery fails, protocol bug found)

**Day 2 failed rally short (opposite play):**
- If Day 2 rally stalls and FAILS below Day 1 highs with relative weakness vs market → flip to short
- "Short into up move on day 2 (failed rally)" — trapped longs from Day 1 provide fuel for reversal
- Entry: short on loss of Day 2 opening range low or VWAP
- Stop: above Day 2 HOD
- Targets: 50% retracement of Day 1 move as first target

## Targets

- **Day 2 continuation target:** 90% of Day 1's measured move, applied from Day 2 open
- First partial (33%): at Day 1 HOD (prior day high)
- Second partial (33%): at Day 1 HOD + Day 1 range extension
- Runner (33%): trail via VWAP hold; exit on first 5-min VWAP break after Day 2 peak
- **Day 3 breakout variant:** trail with 2-min 9 EMA; hold 10-20% as a swing piece through close

## Sizing notes

- Standard to full size on high-quality Day 2 setup (Day 1 closed strong, pre-market holding, volume confirmation)
- Day 3 inside-day breakout: slightly more aggressive sizing due to tight stop distance (only a few cents below Day 2 high in equities; 0.5% in crypto)
- Use ATR-normalized sizing: stop distance = distance from entry to Day 1 close (or Day 2 consolidation low). Size so 1x stop = 1% of account.
- "Backtest shows >50% of similar catalyst days have Day 2 continuation" — use this statistical backing to justify full allocation

## Crypto adaptation notes

**"Day 1" in 24/7 crypto:** Because crypto never closes, "Day 1" is defined as the session with the catalyst event. The "close" is the 00:00 UTC daily candle close. "Holding above Day 1 close" means the 00:00 UTC price is above the prior session's 00:00 UTC close.

**Day 2 in crypto:** The full 24-hour period following the catalyst day. The primary entry windows are:
1. **Asia session open (00:00-06:00 ET):** First test of Day 1 levels. If price holds and drifts higher, institutions are accumulating overnight.
2. **US cash open (09:30-10:00 ET):** The most common Day 2 opening drive window. US institutions enter after overnight analysis.
3. **Europe session open (03:00-04:00 ET):** Secondary driver if catalyst is EU-relevant.

**Day 1 high is key reference:** On Day 2 in crypto, the Day 1 high (00:00 UTC session high) is the primary resistance level to watch. Break above Day 1 high with volume on Day 2 = Day 2 continuation confirmed.

**Volume check for Day 2:** Use 24-hour rolling volume comparison. If Day 2 first 2 hours shows >50% of Day 1 first 2 hours volume = institutional continuation. If Day 2 volume is <30% of Day 1 = catalyst energy has faded, do not trade continuation.

**Day 3 inside-day breakout on crypto:** Low-float or lower-liquidity altcoins show the clearest Day 2 inside/accumulation day patterns. BTC and ETH are too liquid for clean Day 2 inside days on most catalysts — this setup is better suited to mid-cap altcoin perps where institutional buying creates observable tape patterns.

**Short squeeze on Day 3:** High short interest (perp funding rate went deeply negative on Day 1 and 2) combined with Day 3 breakout = short squeeze fuel. The higher the negative funding during the Day 2 accumulation, the more violent the Day 3 squeeze. Monitor funding rate during Day 2 consolidation.

**Venues by setup variant:**
- Day 2 opening drive continuation: Hyperliquid perps (leveraged, quick execution)
- Day 2 key level retest long: Binance spot (for size, overnight hold without funding cost)
- Day 3 inside-day breakout on altcoins: Hyperliquid perps (tight stops require leverage efficiency)

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)

- **Buying Day 2 without confirming price action:** "Do not simply buy the stock because it gapped and held on Day 1 — wait for price action confirmation on Day 2 before committing size." The confirmation is the opening drive breaking through Day 1 resistance levels.
- **Chasing after the opening drive:** Day 2 opening drive often gaps up and runs immediately. Do not chase the gap extension. Wait for the first consolidation above Day 1 resistance to form and then enter at the BOTTOM of that consolidation.
- **Missing the Day 2 failed rally short:** When Day 2 fails to make new highs vs Day 1, the trapped longs provide excellent short fuel. "If Day 2 fails to extend Day 1 move, trapped longs provide fuel for reversal." Keep both long and short scenarios mapped before Day 2 open.
- **Ignoring pre-market volume:** A Day 2 with no pre-market volume is a warning sign. Institutional buyers entering would create pre-market volume. Low pre-market volume = lower conviction on the continuation.
- **Holding through contradictory news:** If a new negative development emerges on Day 2 that contradicts the catalyst, exit immediately. Do not "give it time" to recover.
- **Not taking notes on Day 1 key levels the night before:** "The night before after close, write down the key levels from the stock in play and set alerts for the next morning." Preparation is mandatory for Day 2 plays.

## Example(s)

- Day 2 09:35 ET: US open. BTC drives through $47,800 (Day 1 close) and $48,500 (Day 1 HOD) within 20 minutes on heavy volume.
- Entry: $48,600 (on first 5-min consolidation break above Day 1 HOD). Stop: $47,500 (below Day 1 close).
- Target: 90% of Day 1 move = $48,500 - $42,000 = $6,500 × 0.9 = $5,850 + $42,000 = $47,850 (day 2 open) → $47,850 + $5,850 = $53,700.
- Partials: 33% at Day 1 HOD = $48,500, 33% at $51,000, runner trail via 15-min 20 EMA. Final exit $52,800.

- Day 3: First 20 minutes, ETH breaks above Day 2 high ($3,680) with highest volume bars of the 3-day period.
- Entry: $3,685 (break of Day 2 high). Stop: Day 3 low of day (initially $3,640, trail up as trade develops).
