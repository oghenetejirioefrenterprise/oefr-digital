## Preconditions
- Day 1 had a clear, catalyst-driven directional move with strong volume — closed at or near the session extreme (top 20% for longs; bottom 20% for shorts).
- Day 1 established at least one identifiable consolidation zone during the session — this becomes the day-2 entry reference.
- Day 1 volume was significantly above average — institutions were actively involved, not just retail.
- The catalyst is genuine and ongoing (not fully resolved) — e.g., macro policy change, protocol upgrade adoption, ETF inflow series, multi-day regulatory event.
- Day 2 pre-market: price is holding above (for longs) or below (for shorts) the day-1 closing consolidation zone — not fully reversing overnight.
- Funding rate on HL perps is not extreme in the direction of the trade (i.e., not already overcrowded into day 2).

## Entry trigger
1. **Prior-session closing consolidation zone re-entry:** On day 2 open, wait for a flush or push back to the prior day's closing consolidation zone. Enter long (or short) as price holds at that zone and begins to reclaim the direction of day 1. Stop just beyond the zone boundary.
2. **Day-2 VWAP reclaim:** On day 2, if price opens and dips toward or through VWAP of day 1 (or anchored VWAP from the catalyst candle), buy (or short) the reclaim with volume confirmation.
3. **Second chance breakout:** Price breaks the day-1 high (for longs) or day-1 low (for shorts) on day 2 with volume — confirms institutional follow-through. Enter on the breakout or on the first pullback to the breakout level.
4. **Same-industry / sympathy follow-through:** When a leading asset in a sector makes a catalyst move on day 1, look for the second-largest correlated asset to make the same move on day 2 (the contagion trade). Enter early in the session before it catches up.
5. **Multi-day institutional accumulation:** On days 2–3, institutions continue building positions. Set alerts at the day-1 support inflection level. Buy day 2 or 3 when price dips to that level with a controlled flush and reclaim. Hold for 3–5 days.

## Invalidation
- For longs: price closes below day-1 low on day 2 — institutional buying is done; catalyst is digested.
- For shorts: price reclaims day-1 high on day 2 — short thesis broken.
- Volume on day 2 is significantly below day-1 volume at equivalent time-of-day — low conviction follow-through; fade risk is high.
- Funding rate on HL perps spikes to extreme in the trade direction pre-market before day-2 open — too crowded; wait for reset.
- A counter-catalyst drops during day 2 (e.g., regulatory reversal, protocol fix announced) — exit immediately.

## Targets
- **T1:** Prior day's resistance or prior session's consolidation high (for longs); prior session's opening price (for shorts).
- **T2:** Prior key multi-day resistance/support level beyond day 1's range.
- **T3:** For multi-day institutional accumulation: 10–15% above entry over 3–5 days.
- Trailing stop: 1h 20 EMA on swing holds; 5-min 20 EMA for intraday management.

## Sizing notes
- Day-2 entries are typically lower-conviction than day-1 entries — start at 50–75% of max position size.
- Add if the consolidation zone holds and price begins trending again with volume.
- If funding is already elevated at day-2 open, reduce initial size further and only build on proof that the move is resuming.
- For multi-day swing (3–5 day institutional accumulation): use Binance spot for no funding-rate drag; or HL perps at 2–3x max.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- After a catalyst-driven BTC/ETH trend day (e.g., CPI beat, major ETF inflow print, protocol upgrade), the next session's pullback to the prior day's closing consolidation zone is the primary day-2 setup on Hyperliquid perps.
- Crypto sessions: US session trend days often have follow-through in the next Asian or European session as global participants catch up.
- Funding rate filter: if funding resets toward 0 overnight (from extreme positive), that is the best day-2 setup — the crowded longs flushed out, clean entry available.
- Sector contagion in crypto: when Ethereum has a major upgrade catalyst on day 1, ETH L2s (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base tokens) typically follow on day 2 — express the sympathy trade there.
- "Earnings" → "scheduled token unlock, protocol revenue announcement, or on-chain metrics release."
- "Same-industry sympathy": if BTC leads day 1, ETH follows day 2; if a major DeFi protocol surges day 1, similar protocols follow day 2.
- Second-chance breakout on Hyperliquid: use the day-1 candle high as the key break level; enter on volume expansion above with 4h VWAP confirmation.
