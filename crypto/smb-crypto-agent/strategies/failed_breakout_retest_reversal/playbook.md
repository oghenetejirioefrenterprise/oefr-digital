# Failed Breakout Retest Reversal

## Preconditions
- A breakout above resistance (or below support) occurs but fails to sustain. The "failed breakout" criteria:
  - Price breaks the level then closes back inside the range within 1–3 candles on the entry timeframe (5-min or 15-min).
  - Volume on the false breakout candle is lower than the prior breakout attempt (exhaustion), OR very high (climactic — blow-off).
  - Price returns to the breakout level from the "wrong" side — now the level acts as resistance (was broken upward and failed → now overhead supply) or as support (broken downward and snapped back → now floor).
- For **false breakout to the upside (setup is SHORT):** price breaks above resistance, traps breakout buyers, then falls back below resistance. Trapped longs provide fuel for the reversal.
- For **false breakdown to the downside (setup is LONG):** price breaks below support, stops out longs, then snaps back above support quickly. Forced sellers are out; smart money absorbed the flush.
- Prior trend or multi-timeframe context should favor the reversal direction (e.g., false breakout to upside in a broader downtrend = highest conviction short).

## Entry trigger
- **False breakout short (trap-and-fade):**
  - Price breaks above resistance, fails within 1–3 bars, and closes back below the resistance level.
  - Short entry: on the close back below the resistance level, or on the first lower high that forms after the failed break.
  - Stop: above the false breakout high (the "trapped longs" stop level).
- **False breakdown long (pin bar / hammer reversal):**
  - Price breaks below support, prints a long lower wick (hammer / pin bar), and closes back above the support level within the same candle or next candle.
  - Long entry: on the reclaim of support (close back above the support level); or on the open of the next candle after the hammer.
  - Stop: below the hammer low (the wicked-through level). This gives a very tight stop with 4:1+ reward/risk potential.
- **Confirmation add:** If a second test of the level (from the correct side) occurs and holds, add to position — second touch without breaking confirms the level holds.

## Invalidation
- For false breakout short: price closes above the false high again with volume — the squeeze is on; cover immediately.
- For false breakdown long: price closes below the hammer low on the next full candle — the snap-back failed; stop and reassess.
- Correlated market (BTC, sector) moves strongly against the reversal direction — reduces conviction; tighten stop to breakeven.

## Targets
- **False breakout short:** 50% retracement of the prior up-move as T1; full retracement / prior swing low as T2. For swing: measured move = width of the consolidation applied below the breakdown.
- **False breakdown long:** Prior resistance / top of range as T1; 100% Fibonacci extension as T2. Reward/risk minimum 4:1.
- Scale: Cover 50% at T1, trail remainder with lower highs (short) or higher lows (long) on 5-min.
- For forex-derived patterns (Fibonacci): first target = 61.8% Fibonacci resistance; extended target = level 1 (100% extension).

## Sizing notes
- Full standard allocation (100%) when all three criteria meet: (1) confirmed failed break close, (2) stop is tightly placed at the false high/low, (3) correlated market confirms direction.
- The tight stop (placed at the false extreme) is the structural edge — it allows larger share/contract count for same dollar risk.
- Reduce to 50–75% if broader market is neutral (not providing directional tailwind).
- The "second-mouse" principle: let the first group of counter-trend traders get squeezed, then enter when the reversal is confirmed — do not be first.

## Crypto adaptation notes
- **Stop hunts / liquidity sweeps:** In crypto, false breakouts are frequently engineered by large players (market makers, whales on Hyperliquid) who sweep stops above resistance or below support before reversing. This setup exploits that dynamic.
- **False breakout to upside (short):** Common at all-time highs, round psychological levels ($100k BTC), and prior ATH resistance. Wicks above the level on 4-hour or daily that close back below = strong reversal signal.
- **False breakdown long:** Common at major support levels ($20k, $30k BTC equivalents) and after liquidation cascades. Flash crashes create massive wicks below support that snap back — the pin bar / hammer entry is directly applicable.
- **Funding rate context:** During a false breakout to the upside with subsequent reversal, funding rate was very positive (crowded longs) — amplifies the reversal as longs are forced out. During a false breakdown recovery long, very negative funding = short covering fuel.
- **Applicable assets:** Primarily BTC and ETH on Hyperliquid perps (most liquid, levels most respected). Top-10 altcoins secondary. Micro-caps too illiquid for reliable false-breakout identification.
- **Expanding triangle:** Correlated assets (BTC/ETH) sometimes show expanding triangle patterns where both the false breakout high and false breakdown low are swept before the real move — identify via widening range on low volume.

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Fading too early** — shorting the breakout move itself before it confirms as a false break. "The responsible short is entering on Day 1 against the morning high, not chasing the move." Wait for the close back inside the range.
- **Stop too tight** — placing stop at the resistance level rather than above the false high. Must accommodate the full wick of the false breakout.
- **Ignoring the prior trend** — taking a false breakout short in a strong uptrend is lower probability than in a neutral or downtrending market. Context matters.
- **Missing the second test:** If the first reversal attempt is slow, a second test of the false high (from below, as resistance) gives a cleaner entry with less slippage and higher conviction.
- **Confusing fake breakdown with real breakdown:** If snap-back after the break fails within 1–2 bars = false breakdown. If price consolidates below support for 5+ bars before recovering = likely a real breakdown then recovery (different setup mechanics).
- **Not confirming with correlated instruments:** False breakouts validated by correlated assets (ETH confirms when BTC fakes out) have significantly higher win rates.

Volume on the spike is climactic but price immediately falls back below $95,000 within 2 candles. Short entry: close back below $95,000. Stop: above $95,800 (spike high). Target: $90,000 (prior support / 50% of up-move). Funding rate was +0.08% (crowded longs). "Fake breakout traps breakout buyers and provides fuel for sharp reversals."

**A forex lesson (SMB):** Prior downtrend. Price attempts breakout above resistance on exhaustion volume. Breakout fails — price breaks level, traps long breakout traders, drops back below. "Exhaustion at a level is confirmed when buying effort does not produce price advance."

"The long tail is the confirmation I was looking for." Long on confirmation candle. Stop below hammer low. Target: prior resistance / top of recent range. R:R = 4–5:1.
