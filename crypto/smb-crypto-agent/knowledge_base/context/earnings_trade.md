# Earnings Trade

> **Reference only — not tradeable on crypto venues.** This file documents concepts from SMB equity trading content that do not have a clean crypto perp/spot analog.

## What it is

An earnings trade exploits the scheduled quarterly release of a public company's financial results (EPS, revenue, guidance). The release creates a defined binary catalyst — price gaps significantly in pre-market or after-hours, then the opening session resolves directional conviction. SMB uses earnings as the primary catalyst to select "in-play" stocks each day.

## Mechanic

SMB traders apply a two-axis framework before every earnings open: (1) **quality of the beat** — EPS beat, revenue beat, or miss — and (2) **guidance direction** — raise, in-line, or guide-down. Beat + raise = institutional buy pressure, long bias on gap-holds. Beat + guide-down = distribution despite good numbers, fade the gap. They identify pre-market support/resistance levels, plan A/B/C scenarios before the bell, then trade the first confirmed move rather than chasing the initial gap. Day-2 and Day-3 continuation trades (targeting ~90% of Day-1 move) are treated as a separate, often cleaner setup after the initial volatility settles.

## Why it doesn't port to crypto

- **No quarterly earnings cycle exists on-chain.** Crypto protocols do not file 10-Qs. There is no scheduled date on which fundamentals are compressed into a single print that the whole market reacts to simultaneously.
- **No analyst EPS consensus system.** The edge of the earnings trade relies on the gap between analyst consensus and the actual print — a mechanism absent from crypto markets.
- **No pre-market / after-hours session structure.** Crypto trades 24/7, so there is no defined "open" that resolves overnight price discovery in a single auction.
- **Guidance language has no equivalent.** Management raising or cutting forward guidance is a company-specific legal disclosure. Crypto teams rarely provide binding forward revenue or margin targets.

## Near-analogs worth watching (if any)

- Token unlocks ≈ earnings dates (scheduled catalyst with position squaring ahead of the event; similar beat/miss dynamic where actual unlock size vs. market expectations drives the move)
- Protocol metrics releases (TVL, fee revenue, user growth) ≈ earnings reports (fundamental beat/miss, though unscheduled and not standardized)
- Macro events (FOMC, CPI) ≈ market-level earnings catalysts (drive gap-and-fade or gap-and-go dynamics across all crypto)
