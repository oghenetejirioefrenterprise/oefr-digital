## Preconditions
- A key level is identified before the session: prior session high/low, pre-market/Asia-session range boundary, round number, VWAP anchor, or volume profile boundary
- Asset is in play: catalyst active (news, earnings analog, macro print) or elevated relative volume in pre-session
- Bias established from pre-session action: whether price is likely to test the level from above (breakout) or below (breakdown)
- Session "open" defined:
  - **Hyperliquid:** NY session open (13:00 UTC, 09:00 ET) is primary; Asia open (00:00 UTC) is secondary
  - **Binance:** CME futures open (14:30 UTC) or NY open for cross-asset correlation
- Pre-session range is clearly defined (at least 2h of consolidation forming a measurable high and low)

## Entry trigger

### Sub-type A — Pre-Session High Break (gap-and-go / opening drive long)
1. Price opens at or near the pre-session high; tape shows aggressive buyers absorbing offers
2. Two-step confirmation: (a) price holds above pre-session high for first 2–5 min (no immediate rejection); (b) tape confirms bids stacking above the level
3. Enter long on first 1-min close above pre-session high with expanding volume; or use if-then entry: IF price holds above pre-session high for 3 candles THEN long
4. Alternative aggressive entry: buy the first pullback to pre-session high after initial pop — tape must show bid absorbing at the level

### Sub-type B — Pre-Session High Failure / Opening Drive Short
1. Price opens near pre-session high but tape immediately shows sellers — bids back off, offers refresh
2. If-then trigger: IF bid cannot step up to hold the pre-session high AND bids step away THEN short
3. Enter on first 1-min close below the pre-session high; stop above the session open high
4. Add if price breaks below the pre-session low (confirms failed opening drive)

### Sub-type C — Down-Through-Open Reversal Scalp
1. Price opens and immediately sells through prior session close to a key intraday support
2. Selling velocity decreases (tape slows, bids hold) at support level
3. Buy the pause as velocity of selling decreases; stop below the support level
4. This is the "down through open — quick reversal buy" from playbook: works when the selling was emotional/mechanical rather than informed

### Sub-type D — Opening Consolidation Breakout (GMA — Good Morning America Range)
1. First 15–30 min of session establishes a clear range (high and low); volume normalizes after initial spike
2. Range is well-defined with at least 2 touches of each boundary; tape shows two-sided absorption
3. Enter long on break above range high with volume confirmation; or short on break below range low
4. Stop: inside the range, below the prior consolidation midpoint (for longs) or above (for shorts)
5. Target: 1.5–2x range width from breakout level

## Invalidation
- **Long invalids:** price returns inside the pre-session high within 2 candles on volume; tape shows relentless seller; opening range breaks to downside
- **Short invalids:** bid steps back up and holds above pre-session high; price reclaims the level and holds for 3+ candles on volume
- **Opening range invalids:** price re-enters the range after breakout within 3 candles; volume on breakout candle thinner than prior 2 candles (false breakout signal)

## Targets
- **T1:** 0.5 ATR from entry — take 25–30% off (capital return on the open requires fast partial exits)
- **T2:** 1 ATR from entry or next session level (prior session low for shorts; prior session high for longs) — take 30–40% off
- **T3:** trail remainder on 5-min 20 EMA; exit on EMA break or tape reversal

## Sizing notes
- The open has the most volume and fastest tape; sizing must be deliberate — limit slippage by using aggressive limit orders rather than market orders on entry
- On Hyperliquid: the first 5 minutes post-NY-open has the highest liquidity; use full normal position size
- Automation: if-then entries should be pre-programmed; cannot react fast enough to hand-enter during first candle price discovery — use conditional orders or scripts
- On down-through-open reversals: start with 50% size and add after tape confirms; the timing risk is higher on reversals than breakouts

## Crypto adaptation notes
- In 24/7 crypto markets, "session opens" are defined events even without a formal market open:
  - **NY session (09:00–10:00 ET):** most liquid; highest correlated moves with equity markets; primary opening drive window
  - **Asia session (00:00 UTC):** sets the tone for the rest of the day; pre-session range = prior NY close to Asia open
  - **CME Bitcoin futures open (14:30 UTC Sunday / 09:00 ET Monday):** major institutional reference; CME gap fills are a crypto-specific opportunity
- Pre-session range equivalent = Asia session range (00:00–08:00 UTC) or pre-market range (06:00–13:00 UTC)
- Funding rate at open: if funding is highly positive at session open and price is near pre-session high, the opening drive long is weaker (crowded longs); prefer the short setup in that context
- Volume at first candle: use 3–5x pre-session average as the threshold for "confirming volume" on an opening breakout (not the 1x ADV used in equities)
- BTC opening range on Hyperliquid typically spans 0.3–0.7% on quiet days and 1–2% on catalyst days; calibrate target to actual range width, not fixed dollar amounts

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Chasing the open:** the best entry opportunity is right on the open; waiting for full confirmation means price is already 0.5–1% away — automation removes this temptation
- **Ignoring the tape on the open:** the level alone is not the entry; the tape at the level in the first 2–5 min is the entry signal — this is especially true for down-through-open reversals
- **Trading every gapper:** only assets with a clear, high-scoring level (7+/10) deserve opening drive attention; scanning for largest % movers and then selecting the one with the cleanest level is the correct process
- **Missing the if-then trigger:** having the level and the bias is useless without a pre-defined if-then execution statement — write it before the open and execute it, don't decide in the moment
- **Oversizing during price discovery:** first 2–3 candles of an opening are price discovery (chaotic, wide spreads, low fill quality); enter with 20% of size on the open and save 80% for the confirmed turn or pullback

## Example(s)

### Example 1 — Opening Drive Long on BTC (Hyperliquid)
- Pre-session: BTC Asia range = $62,500–$63,200 (700-point range)
- At 09:00 ET, BTC opens at $63,100; tape immediately shows bids lifting offers at $63,200 (top of Asia range)
- Volume on first 5-min candle = 4x average pre-session volume
- If-then: IF BTC holds above $63,200 for 5 min THEN long
- Entry: long $63,250 with stop at $62,800 (below Asia range midpoint)
- T1: $63,700 (0.5 ATR) — 30% off
- T2: $64,200 (1 ATR / prior session high) — 40% off
- T3: trail remainder with 5-min 20 EMA

### Example 2 — Pre-Session High Failure / Short on ETH (Hyperliquid)
- Pre-session: ETH trades up to $3,200 overnight but fails; sellers present at $3,200 pre-open
- At NY open (09:00 ET), ETH gaps to $3,190; tape shows bids backing off immediately
- Bid cannot step up above $3,180; offer refreshes at $3,200
- Entry: short at $3,180 on bid failure; stop at $3,220 (above pre-session high)
- T1: $3,130 (0.5 ATR) — 25% off
- T2: $3,050 (1 ATR / pre-session low) — 40% off
- T3: trail remainder on 5-min lower highs
