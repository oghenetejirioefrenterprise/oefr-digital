## Preconditions
- Asset is "in play": fresh catalyst, elevated relative volume (RVOL > 2x), or key technical level at stake
- A key price level is identified pre-session: prior session high/low, round number, VWAP, value area boundary, or volume node
- Order book (Level 2 / Hyperliquid DOM) is readable: enough resting size on both sides to identify the aggressor
- Market regime is not a strong trend day in the opposing direction (avoid fading relentless trend days)
- For bid-absorption longs: broader market not in freefall
- For offer-absorption shorts: broader market not in parabolic squeeze

## Entry trigger

### Sub-type A — Bid Absorption Long (buy when buyers win the battle)
Large persistent bid at key support absorbs repeated waves of selling:
1. Bids step up sequentially at support (price lifts then returns, bid holds each time)
2. Speed/velocity of offer-hitting slows; tape prints decelerate on the downside
3. Offers start to lift off the level (thin ask appears above bid)
4. Enter long on next uptick above the held bid; alternatively enter as bid stacks and offer clears

### Sub-type B — Offer Absorption Short (sell when sellers win the battle)
Large persistent offer at key resistance refreshes after every lift attempt:
1. Buyers hit the offer repeatedly; offer refreshes (iceberg or institutional distribution)
2. Bids begin to decrement or step back from the level
3. Speed of upside prints slows; spread widens on approach
4. Enter short as bids step away and the offer holds; enter on first lower-high confirmation

### Sub-type C — Tape Control Flip (breakout confirmation)
Price approaches key resistance/support:
1. Watch for the control flip: the moment bids overpower the offer (or offers overpower the bid) — prints accelerate cleanly in one direction, spread tightens, size stacks
2. Enter in direction of control flip immediately on confirmation
3. Do not anticipate; wait for the flip to complete before committing full size

### Sub-type D — Hidden Refreshing Bidder / Iceberg Absorption
A large resting bid (or ask) refreshes repeatedly after partial fills — institutional accumulation/distribution in progress:
1. Large size appears at the level, gets hit, same size reappears → iceberg order
2. Price refuses to move below the defended level despite selling pressure
3. Enter long (or short for iceberg ask) with stop just beyond the defended level
4. Conviction rises with each refresh cycle (min 3 refresh cycles before full size)

## Invalidation
- **Long invalids:** bid finally lifts with volume; price trades through support on an accelerating tape; offer does not clear after multiple bid refresh cycles
- **Short invalids:** offer absorbed on a surge of buying volume; price closes above resistance for 2+ consecutive 1-min bars with tape acceleration
- **Universal:** tape scoring turns opposite (≤3/10 on entry side); if the control flip reverses within 2 candles, exit immediately — it was a trap

## Targets
- **T1 (primary scalp):** 0.5–1.0 ATR from entry, or next obvious level 2 cluster / round number — take 50–70% off
- **T2 (runner):** VWAP (for mean-reversion entry) or prior swing high/low (for breakout entry) — take 25%
- **T3 (trail):** trail final 5–25% on 1-min structure (lower highs for shorts, higher lows for longs); exit on tape slowdown or spread blow-out in wrong direction

## Sizing notes
- Start with 0.5x normal size until 20+ live executions confirm edge on this specific venue
- In weak/choppy regime: reduce to 0.3x; target only 50–70% of normal ATR move
- Strong conviction (hidden bidder absorbing 3+ waves, 10/10 tape score): up to 1.5x normal size with stop sized to keep dollar risk constant
- Never remove stop — if the hidden bid lifts, exit at the level

## Crypto adaptation notes
- Hyperliquid DOM is equivalent to equity Level 2; bid/ask walls at $1,000-round-number BTC levels and whole-dollar ETH levels cluster institutional order flow identically to equities
- Iceberg orders are visible in Hyperliquid time-and-sales as rapid-fire fills at the same price before size reappears
- Liquidation heatmaps (Coinglass) show where cascades will accelerate — use as directional overlay, not as primary entry signal
- Funding rate adds confluence: rising positive funding + offer absorption short = stronger setup; deeply negative funding + bid absorption long = stronger setup
- Tape velocity is critical in crypto because markets are 24/7 and liquidity varies by session: NY session (13:00–21:00 UTC) has deepest books and most reliable tape signals; apply this setup primarily then
- CVD (cumulative volume delta) on 1-min chart is the crypto-native proxy for time-and-sales imbalance — a CVD divergence (price new high, CVD not) at resistance = offer absorption short signal

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- **Reading the tape at the wrong moment:** highest EV entry is often the most uncomfortable (price moving against you toward support) — train to act at the level, not after it's confirmed safe
- **Evaluating the trade at current prices:** a 6.30 trade and a 7.00 trade are different trades with different EV; don't hold because it already worked
- **Chasing the tape:** entering after the control flip is already complete means chasing into 70% of the move; wait for the next setup
- **Ignoring speed/velocity:** slow tape lifting off support = weak buyers, not strong ones; speed and urgency of bounces is the key discriminator
- **Bid taken vs bid pulled:** a bid that gets taken (hit by sellers) is bullish; a bid that is pulled (removed by the same party who placed it) is bearish; these are completely different signals
- **Using tape in a strong trend:** tape signals work best at identifiable levels with two-sided flow; in a relentless trend day, the tape will lie — bid absorption longs on trend days require no fade signal, just pullback confirmation
- **Over-sizing before fluency:** take tiny size (1/10th normal) until you can consistently read the tape in real time on the specific exchange

## Example(s)

### Example 1 — BTC Bid Absorption Long at $60,000 (Hyperliquid perp)
- Pre-session: $60k identified as prior-week high; market profile POC at $59,800
- Open: BTC pulls back to $60k; order book shows 50 BTC resting bid refreshing after each hit
- Tape: three waves of selling absorbed; bid refreshes each time; CVD stops declining
- Entry: long at $60,020 (just above held bid) with stop at $59,700 (below the bid support)
- T1: $60,500 (0.5 ATR) — take 60% off
- T2: $61,200 (VWAP) — take 25%
- T3: trail final 15% with stop at 1-min higher lows

### Example 2 — ETH Offer Wall Short at $3,500 (Hyperliquid perp)
- Context: ETH rallying into prior breakdown level ($3,500) which is now resistance
- Tape: large 1,000 ETH ask wall sitting at $3,490–$3,500; refreshes after each lift attempt
- CVD divergence: price makes higher high, CVD makes lower high = institutional distribution
- Entry: short at $3,490 as bids step back; stop at $3,520 (above offer wall)
- T1: $3,400 (0.5 ATR) — 50% off
- T2: $3,300 (VWAP) — 25% off
- T3: trail with stop on 1-min lower high breaks
