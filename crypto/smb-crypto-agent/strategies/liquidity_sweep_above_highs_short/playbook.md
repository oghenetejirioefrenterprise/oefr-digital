## Preconditions
- Asset has a clearly defined prior swing high, range high, double top, or round-number resistance level that is widely watched
- Price has been consolidating below the level (accumulating sell-stop liquidity above it) — the more tests without breaking, the higher the probability of a sweep-and-fail
- Breakout buy stops and short covering orders are stacked just above the level (this is the institutional exit liquidity)
- Volume on prior approaches to the level has been declining — each push weaker than the last
- For the "Space Invader" / "look-above-and-fail" variant: the prior move up was strong but the most recent push to the level shows decelerating momentum (lower volume, shorter candles)
- Overextension qualifier (highest conviction): price 4+ ATRs above the 20-day MA, outside 2nd Bollinger Band, and the level has been tested 7+ times

## Entry trigger
- Price breaks above the key resistance level briefly — triggering buy-stop clusters and short-covering — then fails to sustain a close above on the 1-minute or 5-minute chart
- The reversal candle (the "space invader" / false breakout candle) closes back inside the prior range, below the swept high
- Short on the break of the reversal candle's low, or on confirmed consolidation below the swept level (do not short the high itself)
- Volume on the sweep candle should be moderate to high, but the follow-through candle should show contraction or dominant selling volume
- For the "Overextension Short with Level Failure" variant (Row 45): wait for the 8th (or repeated final) test to fail with momentum confirmation before entering

## Invalidation
- Price closes and holds above the swept high with expanding volume for 2+ candles — the breakout is genuine
- Volume surges further on the continuation above the level — real demand, not a sweep
- Stop: just above the swing high of the sweep candle (the highest print of the false breakout)

## Targets
- T1: VWAP or back to the top of the prior consolidation range (cover 33%)
- T2: Midpoint of the range (50% retracement of the run-up into the sweep) (cover 33%)
- T3: Bottom of the consolidation / prior support or neckline of the double top (trail remainder)
- For statistically overextended assets: exit at 10 SMA cross on 5-min or 20 EMA cross on 1-min

## Sizing notes
- Entry is at the break of the reversal candle's low, not at the swept high — this avoids getting trapped in the squeeze phase
- Stop is tight (above the sweep wick) relative to the target, giving 2:1+ R/R
- Position sizing: normal single-trade unit; do not oversize because the setup is "obvious" — crowded short setups above known levels frequently squeeze before following through
- If the level is universally discussed (CT, Discord calls, everyone watching the same double top), reduce size by 30% to account for squeeze risk from coordinated short covering

## Crypto adaptation notes
In crypto perpetual markets, sweeps above prior highs are designed stop-hunts executed by well-capitalized market makers who can see the order book. The mechanism is identical to equities but operates 24/7 and at higher frequency. Key crypto-specific signals:
- Funding rate: if funding is already elevated (positive, longs paying) before the sweep, the setup is higher conviction — longs who were chasing are now trapped above the swept level
- Open interest: a spike in OI during the sweep followed by a rapid decline confirms forced liquidation of breakout longs
- Order book: look for large passive bids disappearing (pulled, not taken) just under the swept high after the reversal candle — institutions withdrawing support
- ICT terminology: this is the "purge" of sell-side liquidity above the high; the "inducement" for retail breakout buyers; the reversal trade is the institutional short entry
- Works across all timeframes — the same pattern on a 1-minute chart is valid, but 15-minute and 1-hour sweeps carry higher conviction and larger moves

## Common mistakes (from review transcripts)
- Shorting at the swept high itself rather than waiting for the confirmed failure — being the first short means being the squeezed short; wait for the close back inside the range
- Not accounting for consolidation time: the "Space Invader" requires a consolidation phase below the swept level before the real drop; entering immediately after the wick often leads to chop
- Ignoring whether the broader market is in a trend day up — fading a sweep when BTC is in a strong trend day is low probability; regime matters
- Missing the "crowded trade" risk (Row 24): when every trader is watching the same short, the squeeze before the fail can be violent; size accordingly
- Holding the short through a genuine breakout retest — if price comes back to test the swept high from below as support and holds, exit immediately, the trade is wrong
